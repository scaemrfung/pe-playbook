Monthly fitness checklist

Private student record for Grades 1–6. Matches each month’s PE unit. Never rank the class or post numbers.

Download printable PDF (11 pages)

Guidelines

  • Private record only — no class ranking, no reading scores aloud.
  • Compare only to your own earlier month.
  • Stop if there is sharp pain. Tell the teacher.
  • Modifications count: march instead of skip, knee plank, walk–jog.
  • 1–2: teacher may help count · 3–4: quiet self-count · 5–6: self-count + optional private goal.

How to run the checklist (teachers)

  1. Introduce once in September. Tell the class this is a private record. Numbers stay on the sheet. We never rank or read scores out loud.
  2. One sheet per student per month (or a folder with all months). Name, grade, and date at the top. Teacher initials when the check is done.
  3. Use one PE class late in the month (or the fitness lesson already in the unit). About 8–12 minutes at the end of class, or a short station rotation.
  4. Demonstrate each station. Show the safe version first (e.g. march instead of skip). Say out loud: “Modifications count.”
  5. Students work quietly. They tick boxes, write a private count if there is one, and circle how it felt (easy · just right · hard).
  6. Collect or return. Keep in a class folder until year-end, or send home and ask for it back next month. Do not display sheets or compare students.
  7. Use for PEW, not ranking. Report effort, safety, and growth (Active Living). Fitness numbers never go on a public board.

What students need to do

Before the stations

  • Listen to the demo.
  • Know it is okay to march, walk, or use a shorter hold.
  • Get your sheet and a pencil.

During each station

  1. Try the activity the teacher shows.
  2. Check the box when you finish that station.
  3. If there is a count (skip, dribble, wall-pass), write only your number — do not ask friends for theirs.
  4. Circle how your body felt: easy · just right · hard.
  5. Move to the next station when the teacher signals.

If something hurts

  • Stop.
  • Tell the teacher.
  • Use a different version (march, sit stretch, shorter time).

After the stations

  • Optional: write one thing that felt stronger and one station to practise again.
  • Hand the sheet to the teacher or put it in your folder.
  • Do not share numbers to compare with classmates.

Grade-band expectations

5–6Self-count. Optional one private goal. Compare only to your own earlier month.

Student script (read aloud)

Today we do our monthly fitness check. It is private.

You will try a few short stations. Check the box when you finish.

Write your own count if there is one — do not look at a friend’s paper.

If it feels too hard, use the easier version. That still counts.

If something hurts, stop and tell me.

At the end, hand me your sheet. We do not rank anyone.

What “done” looks like

A student has finished the month when:

  • Most stations are attempted (or modified)
  • Boxes are ticked
  • Name and date are filled in
  • Teacher has initialed

They do not need the highest count in the class.
