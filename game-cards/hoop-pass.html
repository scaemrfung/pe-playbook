197. Hoop Pass

Cooperative & circus

When: September, December · Slot: 0–5

Hold hands in a circle and pass a hoop all the way around without letting go.

Equipment: One hoop per group

Set-up: Groups hold hands in a circle or line. A hoop starts on one person’s arm.

How we play

  1. Pass the hoop around without letting go of hands — step through and wriggle it over.
  2. If the chain breaks, restart the hoop at the break.
  3. Time trials, team vs team, or one giant class circle.
  4. Talk about communication between rounds.

More details · Grades K-8

Equipment: Hoop

A team-building game. A group holds hands in a circle or line and passes a hoop from player to player without letting go. Play as a timed trial, team against team, or as one large group.

  1. A group holds hands in a circle or line. A hoop starts on one person’s arm.
  2. Pass the hoop around the group WITHOUT letting go of hands — step through, wriggle it over bodies.
  3. Time trials, team vs team, or one giant class circle all work.
  4. If the chain breaks, restart the hoop at the break. Talk about communication.

Source · Video

Grades 1–2. Small groups of 4–5. Teacher shows it first. No timer.
Grades 3–4. Groups of 6–8. Time trial against your own best.
Grades 5–6. Whole class or team vs team. Add a rule (no talking, fewer touches).

Safety. Agree the freeze signal and a safe way to stop. No pulling or dragging a teammate.
