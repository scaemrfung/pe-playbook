149. Throw-Clap-Catch

Relays & stations

When: May, June · Slot: 16–25 · 1 ball · circle or pair relay lanes

Clap first, then catch. A fake that draws a clap is a miss.

Also called: Throw-clap-catch relay · Bounce and Clap · Catch Clap

Equipment: 1 ball per circle or pair

Set-up: Show the three fail states: clap on a fake, drop, catch with no clap.

How we play

  1. Throw or bounce to a player.
  2. They clap once, then catch.
  3. Miss = short task (5 marches) and they stay in. Next player.
  4. Relay version: clap-catch, then pass down the lane.

If this happens

  • Uncatchable throw: replay, no miss.
  • Whole class claps on a fake: laugh, reset, no mass out.

How a round ends. Personal clean catches, or first pair through the relay.

Cues

  • Eyes on the ball.
  • Hands underneath.
  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • Wait behind the cone. Go on the high-five.
  • Stay in your lane. Pass on the right if you meet someone.
  • Quality over sprint — reset if the skill falls apart.

Skins — same game, different wrapper

  • Teacher in the middle of a circle.
  • Pair relay down the gym.

Teaching tips

  • Also called: Throw-clap-catch relay; Bounce and Clap; Catch Clap.
  • House rules: Freeze on the whistle. A miss is a short task and you stay in. Extra players get a job (bumper, next wave, fetcher). No elimination.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Throw-clap-catch relay for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Watches the ball into the hands.
  • Skill focus. Starts on the signal, not early; chooses a path that does not cut off another team.
  • Skill focus. Waits behind the line for their turn.
  • Character Development. No extra steps past the line; a drop is picked up and the leg continues.
  • Safety. Stays in the lane.
  • Healthy Relationships. Encourages the next runner; does not shame a drop.
Grades 1–2. No clap — toss and catch only.
Grades 3–4. Jog. Full lane. High-five hand-off. One quality redo if a pin or ball is dropped.
Grades 5–6. Skip one way, jog back — or weaker foot/hand home. A student starter on the whistle.

Safety. Pass on the right. No diving at a hoop, pin, or beanbag. No elimination: a sit is a stretch, a jail, a hospital, or a role swap, then back in. Freeze on the whistle. Watch a demo or clip before the first play if you have one. A miss is a short task and you stay in. Extra players get a job (bumper, next wave, fetcher).
