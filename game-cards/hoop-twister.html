171. Hoop Twister

Locomotor & listen

When: February · Slot: 0–5 or 16–25

Musical hoops meets Twister: when the music stops, put the called body part in a hoop of the called colour.

Equipment: Coloured hoops, music

Set-up: Scatter coloured hoops like a giant Twister board.

How we play

  1. Music on: travel around the hoops with the skill you name.
  2. Music off: call a body part and a colour (“right hand, blue”). Put that body part in a matching hoop.
  3. Sharing a hoop is allowed. Anyone who cannot get in does 5 reps and rejoins.
  4. Remove 1–2 hoops each round.

  1. Scatter coloured hoops like a giant Twister board.

  3. Music off: call a body part + colour (“right hand, blue”). Students place that body part in a matching hoop.
  4. Sharing a hoop is allowed. Anyone who cannot get in does 5 reps and rejoins.
  5. Remove 1–2 hoops each round.

Source · Video

Grades 1–2. Teacher calls. Two or three commands only. Walk before you jog.
Grades 3–4. Add commands and speed. Let a student call one round.
Grades 5–6. Add fitness moves or a sequence to remember. Students lead.

Safety. Spread out to arm's-length space. Look where you go. Freeze on the whistle. Step into hoops — no diving.
