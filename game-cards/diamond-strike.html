118. Diamond Strike

Strike & field

When: June · Slot: 16–25 · Grades 3–6 · 10 min · four hitters start on bases.

A fielder bowls underhand from the middle, and all four hitters run one base on any hit. Use it to practise striking and fielding with continuous play.

Equipment: 4 bases or buckets, t-ball, bats or hands.

Set-up: A square. One hitter at each base. Fielders around. A middle cone. Agree the freeze signal before the first round.

How we play

  1. A fielder bowls underhand from the middle to any hitter.
  2. On a hit, all four hitters run one base clockwise.
  3. A catch in the air, or a ball placed on a base before a runner arrives, rotates that hitter to the waiting line.
  4. Everyone hits before teams swap.
  5. Balls come back along the side. Restart on a teacher call, not a scramble through the middle.
  6. Freeze on the whistle. Reset spots, then the next round.

Cues

  • One hit, everyone moves one base.
  • Field to the middle cone.
  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • Ready position in the field — feet still, hands out.
  • Hit, then run the path. Do not watch your own hit.
  • Fielders send to the base or hoop, not at a runner.

Variations

  • Must hit to the opposite field.
  • Force at every base.
  • Tee or a bounced teacher-toss for grades 1–2.
  • Three-pitch maximum, then a run-out anyway so the line keeps moving.
  • Fielders rotate one spot after each batter.

Teaching tips

  • Reset: freeze, walk back to start spots, change the key role, then GO. Two or three short rounds beat one long messy one in Diamond Strike.
  • Watch for a long batting line. Use a continuous format or a 3-pitch max.
  • Year plan: June — match the month’s sport ball when you can.
  • Four hitters occupy the bases. One hit (or kick) and all four run one base.
  • Fielders return the ball to a middle cone to stop the next bowl.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Diamond Strike for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Everyone gets a kick or hit.
  • Skill focus. Ball goes to a base or hoop, not at the runner.
  • Skill focus. Takes batting order and a fielding spot; rotates so the same student is not always pitcher.
  • Character Development. Honest out or safe; no arguing a force.
  • Safety. Looks up after contact. Bat or kicker away from the waiting line.
  • Healthy Relationships. Calls ‘mine’ on a fly; helps a runner who is unsure of the next base.
Grades 1–2. Tee or a bounced toss. Run to one base or hoop and stay. Teacher fields the first few.
Grades 3–4. Live send (kick, strike, or throw). Three chances, then a run so the line moves.
Grades 5–6. Fielders call ‘mine.’ Rotate the strike job every three batters. Add a force-out at one base.

Safety. Bowl from the middle only. No throw at runners. No elimination: a sit is a stretch, a jail, a hospital, or a role swap, then back in. Freeze on the whistle. Watch a demo or clip before the first play if you have one.
