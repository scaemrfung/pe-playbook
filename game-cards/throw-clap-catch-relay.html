150. Throw-clap-catch relay

Relays & stations

When: November · Slot: —

Eyes on the ball after a toss.

Equipment: 3 cones, hoop, soccer ball.

Set-up: Cones on the way to a hoop.

How we play

  1. At each cone: toss, clap, catch. At the hoop: one high toss and catch. Home.
Grades 1–2. No clap — toss and catch only.
Grades 3–4. One clap.
Grades 5–6. Two claps at the hoop.

Safety. Balls stay in the lane.
