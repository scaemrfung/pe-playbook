13. Icebergs

Tag & chase

When: February · Slot: 16–25

Hoop islands that shrink. Same family as Sharks and Dolphins.

Equipment: 8–12 hoops.

Set-up: Scatter hoops.

How we play

  1. Travel between icebergs on the call.
  2. Remove one hoop each round.
  3. Share an iceberg. Nobody sits out.
Grades 1–2. Walk. Two per hoop.
Grades 3–4. Jog.
Grades 5–6. One hoop, two feet only.

Safety. Feet in hoops, no diving.
