230. Racquet waiter

Fitness & landings

When: February, March · Slot: —

Balance then a short toss.

Equipment: Racquet or hoop, beanbag, target hoop.

Set-up: Lane to a cone and hoop.

How we play

  1. Bag on the racquet or hoop to the cone. Toss into the target. Three tries.
Grades 1–2. Carry with two hands.
Grades 3–4. One-hand waiter.
Grades 5–6. Walk around a cone before the toss.

Safety. No swinging at people.
