14. Kangaroos and Crocodiles

Tag & chase

When: December, September · Slot: 0–5 · Two teams · a centre line · two home lines

Hear your name. Turn. Get home. The other team tags.

Also called: Kangaroo Tag

Equipment: None · two home lines and a centre line

Set-up: Teams on a centre line, facing you. Homes behind each.

How we play

  1. Call ‘Kangaroos’ or ‘Crocodiles.’
  2. The named team turns and runs (or hops) home. The other team tags.
  3. Tagged players do 5 jumps and re-enter.

If this happens

  • False start: send that team back.
  • Wrong team runs: they are taggable.

How a round ends. Play several calls. No one sits out.

Cues

  • Wait for the name.
  • Tagged players jump 5 and re-enter next call.
  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • Soft two-finger or noodle tag on the back or shoulder — never the head.
  • Change pathway. Do not run someone over to make a tag.
  • If you are tagged, show it honestly and take the next job.

Skins — same game, different wrapper

  • Hop only (Kangaroo Tag).
  • What’s-the-time: jump the called number toward the caller, flee when they turn.

Teaching tips

  • Also called: Kangaroo Tag.
  • House rules: Freeze on the whistle. Tags are two-finger or noodle on the back or shoulder. A miss is a short task and you stay in. Extra players get a job (bumper, next wave, fetcher). No elimination.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Kangaroos and Crocodiles for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Tagged player re-enters or switches — nobody sits out.
  • Skill focus. Uses space, a fake, or a safe zone instead of a pile-up in Kangaroos and Crocodiles.
  • Skill focus. Takes a role and shares space in Kangaroos and Crocodiles; does not crowd a hospital or jail.
  • Character Development. Honest about a tag, a catch, or a line — no argument that stalls Kangaroos and Crocodiles.
  • Safety. Moves only when their name or shape wins.
  • Healthy Relationships. Helps a tagged classmate re-enter (high-five, stretch, hospital, or role swap).
Grades 1–2. Walk-tag. Teacher plus one student tagger. 30–45 second bursts. Re-enter with a high-five or the game’s hospital rule.
Grades 3–4. Jog-tag. 3–5 taggers. 60–90 second rounds. Soft two-finger or noodle tags. Rotate every round.
Grades 5–6. Add bib tails instead of a body tag.

Safety. Soft two-finger or noodle tags on the back or shoulder — never the head. No elimination: a sit is a stretch, a jail, a hospital, or a role swap, then back in. Freeze on the whistle. Watch a demo or clip before the first play if you have one. A miss is a short task and you stay in. Extra players get a job (bumper, next wave, fetcher).
