199. Ice Floe Crossing

Cooperative & circus

When: December · Slot: 8-15

Cross icy water stepping only inside hoops. Touching the floor outside means restart.

Equipment: Hoop each plus 1 extra per team

Set-up: Start and finish lines. Extra hoop per team.

How we play

  1. Slide or lift hoops.
  2. Touching the floor outside = restart.
  3. All people and hoops must cross.
Grades 1–2. More extra hoops.
Grades 3–4. Allow a hand down.
Grades 5–6. Smaller teams.

Safety. No elimination. Freeze on the whistle.
