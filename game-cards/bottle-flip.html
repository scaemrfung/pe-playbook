77. Bottle Flip

Target & send

When: December · Slot: 8-15 · Daly Exercise · slot 8-15.

Flip the bottle so it stands in box 1, then 2, then 3. No skipping. Daly Exercise game. Nobody sits the period.

Also called: Daly Exercise: Bottle Flip

Equipment: Part-filled bottles; floor markers or saucer cones for six boxes

Set-up: Mark six boxes in a line. A little water in each bottle.

How we play

  1. Flip in box 1.
  2. Upright = move to the next box.
  3. On its side = stay. Partner turn.
  4. First upright landing in box 6 wins.
  5. Freeze on the whistle. Reset kit, then the next round.

If this happens

  • Kit knocks a person: pause, widen space, restart.
  • Argument over a point: teacher call, play on.
  • Someone sits out after a miss: they rejoin on the next turn with a short task instead.

How a round ends. Play 3–6 minutes, or first team/player to the posted target, then rotate jobs.

Cues

  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • One job at a time. Extra players fetch or count.
  • A miss is a short task, then back in.

Skins — same game, different wrapper

  • Two or three boxes. Two-hand set-down counts.
  • Six boxes. One-hand flip.
  • Time cap or team relay.

Teaching tips

  • Source: Daly Exercise. Same house rules as the rest of the playbook.
  • Watch for crowding at one piece of kit. Add a second station before you add speed.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Bottle Flip for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Uses the taught action in Bottle Flip with control, not a rush that knocks kit or people.
  • Skill focus. Chooses space, timing, or a partner job in Bottle Flip instead of crowding.
  • Skill focus. Takes a role and shares kit in Bottle Flip.
  • Character Development. Honest about a miss or a line in Bottle Flip.
  • Safety. Freeze on the whistle. No throws at a face.
  • Healthy Relationships. Helps a classmate try again after a miss.
