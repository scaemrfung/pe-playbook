179. Rush Hour

Locomotor & listen

When: September, February · Slot: 0–5

A try-not-to-crash traffic game. Students are cars that change speed and skill on the call.

Equipment: None

Set-up: Students are cars in their own space. Review “look where you go” and no crashing.

How we play

  1. Call a locomotor skill and a speed: slow traffic, city, highway.
  2. Add rules: red light freeze, green light go, reverse, flat tire (hop), out of gas (sit or plank), carpool (join a partner).
  3. If two cars touch, both do 5 jumping jacks and rejoin traffic.
  4. Play 3–5 minutes as a warm-up.

More details · Grades K-3

Equipment: None

A simple “try not to crash” game. It works well with large groups. Idea credit: Jiang Xiaolei.

  1. Students are cars in personal space. Review “look where you go” and no crashing.
  2. Call a locomotor skill and a speed (slow traffic, city, highway).
  3. Add rules: red light freeze, green light go, reverse, flat tire (hop), out of gas (sit or plank), carpool (join a partner).
  4. If two cars touch, both do 5 jumping jacks and re-enter traffic.
  5. Play 3–5 minutes as a primary warm-up.

Source · Video

Grades 1–2. Teacher calls. Two or three commands only. Walk before you jog.
Grades 3–4. Add commands and speed. Let a student call one round.
Grades 5–6. Add fitness moves or a sequence to remember. Students lead.

Safety. Spread out to arm's-length space. Look where you go. Freeze on the whistle.
