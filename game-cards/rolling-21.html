102. Rolling 21

Target & send

When: December, January · Slot: 16-25

Bowling with a bust rule. Large hoop 3, small hoop 7. Exactly 21.

Equipment: Pairs: large hoop, small hoop, one ball

Set-up: Two hoops between the pair.

How we play

  1. Take turns. Large = 3, small = 7, miss = 0.
  2. Add the points aloud.
  3. Past 21 = return to 15.
Grades 1–2. Play to 15. Only the large hoop.
Grades 3–4. Exactly 21. Bust to 15.
Grades 5–6. Team of three sharing one total.

Safety. No elimination. Freeze on the whistle.
