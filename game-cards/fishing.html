165. Fishing

Locomotor & listen

When: November · Slot: 8-15

Hook cones with a noodle and bring them home.

Equipment: Pool noodle; scattered cones

Set-up: Scatter cones. Pair shares one noodle and a home hoop.

How we play

  1. One hooks a cone, returns, passes the noodle.
  2. Continue until cones are gone.
  3. Freeze on the whistle.
Grades 1–2. Colour-only fish.
Grades 3–4. Seated fisher plus running partner.
Grades 5–6. Must hook through the top hole.

Safety. No elimination. Freeze on the whistle.
