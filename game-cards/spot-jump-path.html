235. Spot-Jump Path

Fitness & landings

When: April, May, June · Slot: 16–25 · Spots, hoops, or a river line · clear landing

Jump the targets. Quiet stick. Quality before speed.

Also called: Spot-jump path · Helicopter / Snake rope · Helicopter · Frogs & Toads · Hurdle hops

Equipment: Spots, hoops, or a two-rope river · optional noodle

Set-up: Lane of spots or a two-rope river. Show two-foot take-off and a still landing.

How we play

  1. Jump target to target. Land inside, not on the rim.
  2. Miss or a noisy fall-back: go back one target.
  3. Walk back along the side. Next jumper.

If this happens

  • Toe over the take-off line: no jump.
  • Lane clash: pause and separate.

How a round ends. First team through, farthest jump, or most quiet sticks.

Cues

  • Two feet.
  • Quiet stick.
  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • Quiet landings. Bend ankles, knees, and hips.
  • Quality beats speed. Stop if a wrist, knee, or back complains.
  • Breathe out on the work; do not hold a breath to rush.

Skins — same game, different wrapper

  • Noodle swept on the floor around a circle (Helicopter).
  • Beat jumping.
  • Standing long jump, cone behind the heels.
  • What’s-the-time jumps toward a caller, then flee.
  • Jump The River: one two-rope river that grows.
  • Hurdle hops: low hurdles, same quiet stick.

Teaching tips

  • Also called: Spot-jump path; Jump The River; Helicopter / Snake rope; Helicopter; Frogs & Toads; Hurdle hops.
  • House rules: Freeze on the whistle. Tags are two-finger or noodle on the back or shoulder. A miss is a short task and you stay in. Extra players get a job (bumper, next wave, fetcher). No elimination.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Spot-jump path for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Two-foot take-off and a still land.
  • Skill focus. Paces the set so they can finish, not sprint and collapse.
  • Skill focus. Works beside a partner or in a hoop without bumping others out.
  • Character Development. Records a private number honestly, or skips the number and names effort.
  • Safety. One jumper on the path.
  • Healthy Relationships. Lets a classmate have a hoop or spot; no blocking a landing.
Grades 1–2. Half the distance or fewer reps. Knees-down options. Stop if wrists or backs complain. Feet-only if sticks are new.
Grades 3–4. Full lane or full set. Quiet landings. Partner counts.
Grades 5–6. Add a half-turn on the last spot.

Safety. Quiet landings. Stop a station if a wrist, knee, or back complains — switch to a walk option. Sticks stay below the knee. No slapshots. Blade on the floor when you stop. No elimination: a sit is a stretch, a jail, a hospital, or a role swap, then back in. Freeze on the whistle. Watch a demo or clip before the first play if you have one. Tags are two-finger or noodle on the back or shoulder. A miss is a short task and you stay in. Extra players get a job (bumper, next wave, fetcher).
