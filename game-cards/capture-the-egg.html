40. Capture the Egg

Invasion & team

When: May · Slot: 16–25

Carry a beanbag through traffic.

Equipment: Beanbags, pinnies.

Set-up: Two ends. A few taggers in the middle.

How we play

  1. Carry an egg across.
  2. If tagged, hand the egg to the tagger and switch jobs.
  3. Count fair crossings.
Grades 1–2. Walk. Wide channel.
Grades 3–4. Jog.
Grades 5–6. Two eggs at once.

Safety. Soft tag. No diving on the egg.
