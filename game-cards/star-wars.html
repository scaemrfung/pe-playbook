63. Star Wars

Invasion & team

When: November, January · Slot: 16-25

The beach ball is the only target. People are not targets.

Equipment: Beach ball, many soft balls, a bench at each end

Set-up: Beach ball on the centre line. Teams behind their lines.

How we play

  1. Throw soft balls at the beach ball.
  2. Stay behind your line.
  3. Beach ball touches the other bench = 1.
Grades 1–2. Must roll, not throw.
Grades 3–4. Throw at the beach ball only.
Grades 5–6. Three teams or a must-roll rule.

Safety. No elimination. Freeze on the whistle.
