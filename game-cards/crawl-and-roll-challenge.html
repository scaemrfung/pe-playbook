214. Crawl and Roll Challenge

Fitness & landings

When: April · Slot: 8-15 · Daly Exercise · slot 8-15.

Link a crawl into a roll across a mat lane. Daly Exercise game. Nobody sits the period.

Also called: Daly Exercise: Crawl and Roll Challenge

Equipment: Mats

Set-up: Mat lanes. Demo the exact sequence once.

How we play

  1. Crawl to the mat, perform the taught roll, stand, finish the lane.
  2. Freeze on the whistle.

If this happens

  • Kit knocks a person: pause, widen space, restart.
  • Argument over a point: teacher call, play on.
  • Someone sits out after a miss: they rejoin on the next turn with a short task instead.

How a round ends. Play 3–6 minutes, or first team/player to the posted target, then rotate jobs.

Cues

  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • One job at a time. Extra players fetch or count.
  • A miss is a short task, then back in.

Skins — same game, different wrapper

  • Log roll only.
  • Crawl only.
  • Add a balance at the end.

Teaching tips

  • Source: Daly Exercise. Same house rules as the rest of the playbook.
  • Watch for crowding at one piece of kit. Add a second station before you add speed.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Crawl and Roll Challenge for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Uses the taught action in Crawl and Roll Challenge with control, not a rush that knocks kit or people.
  • Skill focus. Chooses space, timing, or a partner job in Crawl and Roll Challenge instead of crowding.
  • Skill focus. Takes a role and shares kit in Crawl and Roll Challenge.
  • Character Development. Honest about a miss in Crawl and Roll Challenge.
  • Safety. Freeze on the whistle.
  • Healthy Relationships. Helps a classmate try again after a miss.
Grades 1–2. Log roll only.
Grades 3–4. Crawl only.
Grades 5–6. Add a balance at the end.

Safety. No elimination. Freeze on the whistle. A miss is a short task, then stay in.
