56. Pin Galore Soccer

Invasion & team

When: September · Slot: 16–25

Soccer where every pin is a goal. Knock one down and carry it back to your own row.

Equipment: Plastic pins, indoor soccer balls or Softex foam balls

Set-up: Each team lines plastic pins along its end line. Two or more indoor soccer balls or Softex foam balls.

How we play

  1. Play soccer with feet only.
  2. Score by knocking over an opponent’s pin instead of shooting into a net.
  3. Carry a knocked-down pin back and add it to your own row.
  4. The game ends when one team has every pin, or at time (most pins wins).

More details · Grades 1-8

Equipment: Plastic pins, indoor soccer balls or Softex foam balls

A soccer variation that increases participation. Instead of scoring in a goal, players try to knock over the other team’s pins. A player who knocks down a pin brings it to their own side and adds it to their team’s pins. The game ends when one team has all the pins or time runs out. Use it as a soccer lead-up game.

  1. Each team lines plastic pins on its end. Use indoor soccer balls or Softex foam balls.
  2. Play soccer (feet only). Instead of a net, you score by knocking over an opponent’s pin.
  3. When you knock a pin down, carry that pin back and add it to your own row.
  4. Game ends when one team has every pin, or when time expires (most pins wins).
  5. Use it as a soccer lead-up; every pin is a scoring chance.

Grades 1–2. Walk-through first. Small field, one object, teacher referees.
Grades 3–4. Full rules, larger area. Rotate roles every round.
Grades 5–6. Add strategy talk between rounds. Students set house rules.

Safety. Foam balls. Kicks stay low — no blasting from close range. Nobody stands in front of their own pins as a goalie wall.
