205. Reaction Speed

Cooperative & circus

When: January, March · Slot: 8-15

Hands start away from the ball. Clean drop, no fake until later.

Equipment: Trios: cone as a height marker, tennis ball

Set-up: Dropper holds the ball at chin height. Catcher ready.

How we play

  1. Dropper releases with no fake.
  2. Catcher tries to catch before the bounce.
  3. Five drops, then rotate.
Grades 1–2. Larger ball, lower drop. Allow one bounce.
Grades 3–4. Tennis ball, chin height.
Grades 5–6. Add pump-fakes after they can catch a clean drop.

Safety. No elimination. Freeze on the whistle. Soft tags.
