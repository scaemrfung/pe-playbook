166. Follow the Leader

Locomotor & listen

When: December, April · Slot: 0–5

Copy a locomotor or shape in a small line.

Equipment: Optional whistle, clap or music.

Set-up: Lines of 4.

How we play

  1. Leader travels or holds a shape.
  2. Switch leader every 20 seconds.
Grades 1–2. Teacher starts as leader.
Grades 3–4. Student leaders.
Grades 5–6. Must include a level change.

Safety. Leave space. No tumbling off mats.
