143. Jungle Run Relays

Relays & stations

When: May · Slot: 16–25

Relay with a hoop, a jump, and a cone.

Equipment: Cones, hoops, optional hurdles (low).

Set-up: A short obstacle path per team.

How we play

  1. Run the path, return, hand off.
  2. Next runner waits for the touch.
Grades 1–2. Walk-run. Step in the hoop.
Grades 3–4. Jog. Two-foot jump.
Grades 5–6. Add a baton.

Safety. Low obstacles. Pass on the outside.
