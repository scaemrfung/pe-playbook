195. Flip Flop

Cooperative & circus

When: December, April · Slot: 8-15

Half the class are tunnels and bridges; the other half travel, then swap.

Equipment: None (bodies as kit)

Set-up: Tunnels (hands-knees) and bridges (pike). Gaps between.

How we play

  1. Travellers crawl through and jump or step over.
  2. Swap and rebuild.
  3. Freeze on the whistle.
Grades 1–2. Tunnels only.
Grades 3–4. Walk around instead of over.
Grades 5–6. Theme the course.

Safety. No elimination. Freeze on the whistle.
