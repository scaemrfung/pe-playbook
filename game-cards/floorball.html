119. Floorball

Strike & field

When: November · Slot: 8-15

Score in the opponent's mini goal and defend your own.

Equipment: Floor hockey sticks, soft ball, mini goals

Set-up: Goals at each end. One ball.

How we play

  1. Tap the ball with the stick.
  2. After a goal, the conceding player restarts.
  3. Freeze on the whistle.
Grades 1–2. Hands instead of sticks.
Grades 3–4. Wider goals.
Grades 5–6. 3v3 with sidelines.

Safety. No elimination. Freeze on the whistle.
