216. Frogs on the Lily Pads

Fitness & landings

When: February, April · Slot: 5–16 or 16–25 · Whole class · 5 min · hoop path.

Students jump with two feet from hoop to hoop and stick each landing. Use it to practise gymnastics landings.

Equipment: Hoops.

Set-up: Paths of hoops. Agree the freeze signal before the first round.

How we play

  1. Show the quality cue first (quiet land, airplane arms, still head). Count quality reps, not speed.
  2. Jump pad to pad and stick.
  3. A miss means start that path again.
  4. Freeze on the whistle. Reset spots, then the next round.
  5. After two rounds, 20 seconds of a named stretch, then one more quality set.

Cues

  • Bend–jump–stick.
  • Miss = start that path again.
  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • Quiet landings. Bend ankles, knees, and hips.
  • Quality beats speed. Stop if a wrist, knee, or back complains.
  • Breathe out on the work; do not hold a breath to rush.

Variations

  • Two-to-one landings.
  • Carry a beanbag on the head.
  • Half distance or fewer reps for grades 1–2. Quality landings stay the same.
  • Partner count: one moves, one counts out loud, then swap.
  • Last round is ‘show your best three’ — not a race.

Teaching tips

  • Reset: freeze, walk back to start spots, change the key role, then GO. Two or three short rounds beat one long messy one in Frogs on the Lily Pads.
  • Watch for sloppy landings when they race. Sit the race, keep the quality set.
  • Sticks: teach grip and ‘blade down’ in the hallway line before the first dribble.
  • Year plan: February, April — match the month’s sport ball when you can.
  • Hoop lily pads in 3–4 paths. Jump hoop to hoop and hold the land for a count of 3.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Frogs on the Lily Pads for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Jump-stick or a still shape for 3 seconds.
  • Skill focus. Paces the set so they can finish, not sprint and collapse.
  • Skill focus. Works beside a partner or in a hoop without bumping others out.
  • Character Development. Records a private number honestly, or skips the number and names effort.
  • Safety. One at a time on a hoop or mat.
  • Healthy Relationships. Lets a classmate have a hoop or spot; no blocking a landing.
Grades 1–2. Half the distance or fewer reps. Knees-down options. Stop if wrists or backs complain. Feet-only if sticks are new.
Grades 3–4. Full lane or full set. Quiet landings. Partner counts.
Grades 5–6. Farther pads. Quiet landings.

Safety. Quiet landings. Stop a station if a wrist, knee, or back complains — switch to a walk option. Sticks stay below the knee. No slapshots. Blade on the floor when you stop. No elimination: a sit is a stretch, a jail, a hospital, or a role swap, then back in. Freeze on the whistle. Watch a demo or clip before the first play if you have one.
