92. Knock the Pin

Target & send

When: November, May, June · Slot: 8-15

Run the triangle, then throw to knock the centre pin.

Equipment: Cones in a triangle; centre cone in a hoop; softballs

Set-up: Start cones form a triangle around a hoop-and-cone target.

How we play

  1. Run around the outside of your cone, return, throw from the cone.
  2. Hit = point and reset.
  3. Freeze on the whistle.
Grades 1–2. Shorter triangle.
Grades 3–4. Underarm only.
Grades 5–6. Team total.

Safety. No elimination. Freeze on the whistle.
