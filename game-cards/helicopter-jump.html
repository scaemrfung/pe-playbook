217. Helicopter Jump

Fitness & landings

When: February, April, May · Slot: 8-15

Jump the sliding noodle and stick the landing.

Equipment: Foam pool noodle

Set-up: Large circle. One pupil in the middle with the noodle.

How we play

  1. Spinner slides the noodle on the floor.
  2. Jump and land in balance.
  3. Stumble or hit = 5s rest.
Grades 1–2. Step-over.
Grades 3–4. No elimination.
Grades 5–6. Two jumpers at a time.

Safety. No elimination. Freeze on the whistle.
