222. Jumping Jack Hoop Challenge

Fitness & landings

When: February, May · Slot: 8-15 · Daly Exercise · slot 8-15.

Students spin a hoop, do jumping jacks, then catch it, adding one jack after each clean catch. Use it as a personal challenge, not a race (Daly Exercise game).

Also called: Daly Exercise: Jumping Jack Hoop Challenge

Equipment: One hoop each; personal space

Set-up: Show a clean rim-spin and a two-hand catch.

How we play

  1. Spin the hoop upright on the floor.
  2. Do 1 jumping jack, then catch.
  3. Next try: 2, then 3. A drop returns you to your last clean number.
  4. Freeze on the whistle. Reset kit, then the next round.

If this happens

  • Kit knocks a person: pause, widen space, restart.
  • Argument over a point: teacher call, play on.
  • Someone sits out after a miss: they rejoin on the next turn with a short task instead.

How a round ends. Play 3–6 minutes, or first team/player to the posted target, then rotate jobs.

Cues

  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • One job at a time. Extra players fetch or count.
  • A miss is a short task, then back in.

Skins — same game, different wrapper

  • Marching instead of jacks.
  • Jacks. Quality over count.
  • Record a weekly personal best.

Teaching tips

  • Source: Daly Exercise. Same house rules as the rest of the playbook.
  • Watch for crowding at one piece of kit. Add a second station before you add speed.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Jumping Jack Hoop Challenge for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Uses the taught action in Jumping Jack Hoop Challenge with control, not a rush that knocks kit or people.
  • Skill focus. Chooses space, timing, or a partner job in Jumping Jack Hoop Challenge instead of crowding.
  • Skill focus. Takes a role and shares kit in Jumping Jack Hoop Challenge.
  • Character Development. Honest about a miss or a drop in Jumping Jack Hoop Challenge.
  • Safety. Freeze on the whistle.
  • Healthy Relationships. Helps a classmate try again after a miss or a drop.

Grades 3–4. Jacks. Quality over count.
