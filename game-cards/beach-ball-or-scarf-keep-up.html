185. Beach Ball or Scarf Keep-Up

Cooperative & circus

When: March, December, October · Slot: 16–25 · Circles of 4–8 · one beach ball or scarf per circle · optional noodles or rulers

Keep one beach ball or scarf up. Count hits. A drop restarts the count, not the person.

Also called: Balloon Keep-Up · Balloon Keep-Ups · Balloon Keep-Up station

Equipment: One beach ball or juggling scarf per circle · optional noodles or rulers

Set-up: Circles with arm’s-length space. One beach ball or scarf each.

How we play

  1. Name the shared goal out loud — you are not chasing a winner between circles unless you say so.
  2. Toss the beach ball or scarf in. Soft taps only.
  3. Count hits aloud. Floor = restart that circle’s count.
  4. Play 3–6 minutes.

If this happens

  • Hard swat: sit one restart, then rejoin.
  • Sticks clash: widen the circle.
  • Ball or scarf leaves the circle: retrieve and restart that count.

How a round ends. Highest consecutive count, or first circle to 20.

Cues

  • Call it.
  • A drop restarts the count, not the player.
  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • Use names. Ask before you grab a hoop, rope, or ball.
  • If it drops, restart together — no blame.
  • Quiet voices so the group can hear the next job.

Skins — same game, different wrapper

  • Hands only.
  • Noodle/ruler only.
  • Second beach ball or scarf.
  • Seated chairs.

Teaching tips

  • Also called: Balloon Keep-Up; Balloon Keep-Ups; Balloon Keep-Up station.
  • House rules: Freeze on the whistle. A miss is a short task and you stay in. Extra players get a job (bumper, next wave, fetcher). No elimination.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Beach Ball or Scarf Keep-Up for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Catch, then throw — call it.
  • Skill focus. Plans a simple order or role with teammates before rushing.
  • Skill focus. Takes turns and solves a small problem without grabbing.
  • Character Development. Restarts a drop without blaming a classmate.
  • Safety. Moves at a speed the group can control. Consent for partner shapes — a student may say no.
  • Healthy Relationships. Uses a name or ‘mine’ before the send.
Grades 1–2. Small groups of 4–6. Teacher in the group if the task needs an extra pair of hands.
Grades 3–4. Whole-class or groups of 8. Plan for 20 seconds, then try.
Grades 5–6. Class record. Weak hand only.

Safety. No heading hard balls. Latex allergy: use a beach ball. No elimination: a sit is a stretch, a jail, a hospital, or a role swap, then back in. Freeze on the whistle. Watch a demo or clip before the first play if you have one. A miss is a short task and you stay in. Extra players get a job (bumper, next wave, fetcher).
