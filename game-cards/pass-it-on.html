203. Pass It On

Cooperative & circus

When: December, February · Slot: 16–25

Teams lie in a line and move every object from one end to the other using only their feet.

Equipment: Hoops, a mix of soft items (balls, beanbags, noodles)

Set-up: Teams lie on their backs in a line from one end of the gym. Objects start at one end; a hoop at the far end.

How we play

  1. Pass only with your feet — no hands.
  2. Move every object down the line into the hoop at the far end.
  3. An item that touches a hand or the floor restarts from the beginning.
  4. First team to move everything wins. Mix item sizes to change the difficulty.

More details · Grades 2-8

Equipment: Hoops, various items

A team-building game. Teams pass objects from one end of their line to the other using only their feet. Make even teams. Each team lies on their backs in a line, head to toe. Place a hoop with objects at the feet of the first player and an empty hoop at the head of the last player. On the signal, teams pass all of their objects to the far hoop using only their feet. Play as many times as you like.

  1. Teams lie on their backs in a line from one end of the gym toward the other.
  2. Objects start at one end. Players may pass ONLY with their feet — no hands.
  3. Move every object down the line into a hoop or pile at the far end.
  4. First team to move all objects wins. If an item touches a hand or the floor off-plan, restart that item.
  5. Use it for team building; mix item sizes to change the difficulty.

Source · Video

Grades 1–2. Small groups of 4–5. Teacher shows it first. No timer.
Grades 3–4. Groups of 6–8. Time trial against your own best.
Grades 5–6. Whole class or team vs team. Add a rule (no talking, fewer touches).

Safety. Agree the freeze signal and a safe way to stop. No pulling or dragging a teammate. Soft items only; no kicking an object into a face.
