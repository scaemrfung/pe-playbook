88. Hand Tennis

Target & send

When: November, January, March · Slot: 8-15

Win the rally with hand hits, one bounce per side.

Equipment: Soft ball; a line or bench as net

Set-up: Mark a small court. One server.

How we play

  1. Underhand serve to land on the other side.
  2. Return with the hand.
  3. More than one bounce or out = point.
Grades 1–2. Allow two bounces.
Grades 3–4. Bat or racket instead of a hand.
Grades 5–6. Doubles.

Safety. No elimination. Freeze on the whistle.
