159. Colour Hunt

Locomotor & listen

When: May · Slot: 8-15

Find and collect the colour the teacher calls.

Equipment: Coloured cones or markers

Set-up: Scatter colours. Pupils start in a home base.

How we play

  1. Call a colour and a travel skill.
  2. Collect that object and return.
  3. Progress: a sequence of colours to remember.
Grades 1–2. Picture cards.
Grades 3–4. Pair memory list.
Grades 5–6. Hidden colours under cones.

Safety. No elimination. Freeze on the whistle.
