42. Collect the Treasure

Invasion & team

When: October, January · Slot: 16–25 · 4 teams · 5–6 min · beanbags in the middle.

Teams carry treasure home one piece at a time, and a tagged player walks the piece back to the middle. Use it to practise moving into space and simple attack and defence jobs.

Equipment: Beanbags, 4 hoops.

Set-up: Treasure in the middle. Team hoops in corners. Pinnies on one team. Show the two attacking directions before GO. Agree the freeze signal before the first round.

How we play

  1. Carry one treasure home at a time.
  2. If tagged, walk it back to the middle.
  3. No standing in your hoop to guard.
  4. Play 4–6 minute periods. Extra players are bumpers, walls, or the next wave — they stay in a job.
  5. Freeze on the whistle. Reset spots, then the next round.

Cues

  • One treasure at a time.
  • Tagged = walk it back.
  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • Head up. Pass or move — do not crowd the ball.
  • Find a passing lane, not a pile-up.
  • Soft send; the ball stays below the chest unless the sport needs a high set.

Variations

  • Must crab-walk.
  • Steal from other nests after the middle is empty.
  • Must complete one pass before a shot or a score.
  • Add a second foam ball if more than 24 students so more players get a touch.
  • Extra players are walking walls — they may deflect a ball with feet but may not score.

Teaching tips

  • Reset: freeze, walk back to start spots, change the key role, then GO. Two or three short rounds beat one long messy one in Collect the Treasure.
  • Watch for one student holding the ball. Cue a pass or a 3-second pivot.
  • Year plan: October, January — match the month’s sport ball when you can.
  • Beanbags in a centre hoop. Teams carry one at a time to a home hoop.
  • Tagged players walk the treasure back to the middle. No guarding the hoop.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Collect the Treasure for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Sends, receives, or travels with control using the taught skill (foot, hand, or implement).
  • Skill focus. One object or one rescue at a time.
  • Skill focus. Walks the treasure back after a tag, without arguing.
  • Character Development. Honest score and honest tags; tries again after a miss.
  • Safety. Heads up. No body checks. Equipment stays below the agreed height.
  • Healthy Relationships. Calls a teammate’s name; includes a player who has not touched the treasure.
Grades 1–2. Small space, big targets, walking or jogging. Teacher may be a walking bumper. Hands may help a trap if the ball is soccer.
Grades 3–4. Full game as written, foam or real sport ball as the unit says. One pass before a score if the class crowds.
Grades 5–6. Two treasures allowed if you pair up.

Safety. No body checks. No elimination: a sit is a stretch, a jail, a hospital, or a role swap, then back in. Freeze on the whistle. Watch a demo or clip before the first play if you have one.
