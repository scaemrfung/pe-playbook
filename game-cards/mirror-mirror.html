175. Mirror Mirror

Locomotor & listen

When: September, February · Slot: 0–5 or 16–25

Two groups run opposite ways to music. When it stops, one group freezes in a pose and the other mirrors it.

Equipment: Music

Set-up: Two equal groups. Group A outside the basketball-court line, Group B inside.

How we play

  1. Music on: the outside group runs one way, the inside group runs the other way.
  2. Music off: the outside group freezes in any pose. Each inside player stands 1–2 metres in front of a frozen player and mirrors the pose.
  3. Swap roles. An extra player joins a group if numbers are odd.
  4. For older students, use fitness poses (plank, lunge, squat).

More details · Grades 1-5

Equipment: Music

Use it as a stand-alone game, a warm-up or a fitness activity. Divide players into 2 equal groups, one outside the basketball court line and one inside. When the music plays, the outside group runs one way and the inside group runs the other way. When the music stops, the outside group freezes in any pose. Each inside player stands in front of a frozen player (1–2 metres apart) and copies the pose. Then the groups switch. With an odd number, let one player join either group. For older students, use fitness poses such as a plank, side plank, lunge or squat. Idea credit: Anne Guilmaine.

  1. Two equal groups. Group A stands outside the basketball court line; Group B stands inside.
  2. Music on: outside group runs one direction, inside group runs the opposite direction.
  3. Music off: the outside group FREEZES in any pose. Inside players each stand 1–2 metres in front of a frozen player and mirror the pose.
  4. Swap roles (outside becomes inside). If numbers are odd, allow one extra player to join a group.
  5. For older students require fitness poses (plank, lunge, squat).

Source · Video

Grades 1–2. Teacher calls. Two or three commands only. Walk before you jog.
Grades 3–4. Add commands and speed. Let a student call one round.
Grades 5–6. Add fitness moves or a sequence to remember. Students lead.

Safety. Spread out to arm's-length space. Look where you go. Freeze on the whistle. Inside group runs on the inside lane only.
