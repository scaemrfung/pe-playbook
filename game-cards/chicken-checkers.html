6. Chicken Checkers

Tag & chase

When: December, September · Slot: 16–25 · Hoops or cones as bases · fewer bases than people

On ‘switch,’ leave your base and claim a new one. No base = you are it, or you do the short task.

Also called: Buzz Off / hoop free zones · Buzz Off · Hoop Dog

Equipment: Hoops or cones as bases

Set-up: Bases spread. One player per base to start if you can. Show the switch call.

How we play

  1. Call ‘switch.’ Everyone must leave and claim a new base.
  2. Two on one base: both leave.
  3. Player left without a base becomes the tagger, or does 5 jacks and stays in.

If this happens

  • Dive onto a cone: no claim.
  • Camping through the call: they must leave.

How a round ends. Rotate the leftover player. Play 4–6 switches.

Cues

  • One chicken per hoop.
  • No sliding a chicken into a person.
  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • Soft two-finger or noodle tag on the back or shoulder — never the head.
  • Change pathway. Do not run someone over to make a tag.
  • If you are tagged, show it honestly and take the next job.

Skins — same game, different wrapper

  • Stone Guard: raider steals a stone.
  • Tigers keep feet in hoops and tag from there (Hoop Dog).
  • Rest hoop you may step into (Buzz Off).

Teaching tips

  • Also called: Buzz Off / hoop free zones; Buzz Off; Hoop Dog.
  • House rules: Freeze on the whistle. Tags are two-finger or noodle on the back or shoulder. A miss is a short task and you stay in. Extra players get a job (bumper, next wave, fetcher). No elimination.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Chicken Checkers for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. No sliding a piece into a person.
  • Skill focus. One object per trip.
  • Skill focus. Takes a role and shares space in Chicken Checkers; does not crowd a hospital or jail.
  • Character Development. Honest about a tag or a catch — no argument that stalls Chicken Checkers.
  • Safety. Soft two-finger or noodle tag on the back or shoulder — never the head. Freeze on the whistle.
  • Healthy Relationships. Helps a tagged classmate re-enter (high-five, stretch, hospital, or role swap).
Grades 1–2. Walk-tag. Teacher plus one student tagger. 30–45 second bursts. Re-enter with a high-five or the game’s hospital rule.
Grades 3–4. Jog-tag. 3–5 taggers. 60–90 second rounds. Soft two-finger or noodle tags. Rotate every round.
Grades 5–6. Must use a first name and a kind word to swap.

Safety. One extra person per hoop during the swap. No shoving out. No elimination: a sit is a stretch, a jail, a hospital, or a role swap, then back in. Freeze on the whistle. Watch a demo or clip before the first play if you have one. Tags are two-finger or noodle on the back or shoulder. A miss is a short task and you stay in.
