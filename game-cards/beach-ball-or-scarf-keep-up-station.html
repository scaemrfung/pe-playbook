156. Beach Ball or Scarf keep-up station

Locomotor & listen

When: March · Slot: —

Strike without a catch.

Equipment: One juggling scarf or beach ball per pair.

Set-up: Pairs in a square.

How we play

  1. Hit, do not catch. Count hits. A drop restarts the count, not the player.
Grades 1–2. Two-hand tap.
Grades 3–4. Either hand.
Grades 5–6. Alternate hands.

Safety. Space between pairs.
