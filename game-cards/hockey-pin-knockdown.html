89. Hockey pin knockdown

Target & send

When: January · Slot: —

One controlled shot after a dribble.

Equipment: Tunnel (two lines of cones, or a hoop in a hula hoop stand), pin, stick, hockey ball.

Set-up: Tunnel to a pin.

How we play

  1. Dribble the tunnel. One push at the pin. Dribble home.
Grades 1–2. Kick the hockey ball if no sticks.
Grades 3–4. One shot.
Grades 5–6. Receive a partner pass before the shot.

Safety. No high sticks. Fetch from the side.
