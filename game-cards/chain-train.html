81. Chain Train

Target & send

When: December, February · Slot: 16-25 · Daly Exercise · slot 16-25.

Hit your first hoop, then steal another group's hoop. Every group keeps one. Daly Exercise game. Nobody sits the period.

Also called: Daly Exercise: Chain Train

Equipment: 4 hoops and 1 bean bag per group of 4

Set-up: Four hoops in a short line. Last hoop cannot be raided.

How we play

  1. Throw the bag into your first hoop.
  2. Hit = take one hoop from another group.
  3. A group down to one hoop is safe.
  4. Freeze on the whistle. Reset kit, then the next round.

If this happens

  • Kit knocks a person: pause, widen space, restart.
  • Argument over a point: teacher call, play on.
  • Someone sits out after a miss: they rejoin on the next turn with a short task instead.

How a round ends. Play 3–6 minutes, or first team/player to the posted target, then rotate jobs.

Cues

  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • One job at a time. Extra players fetch or count.
  • A miss is a short task, then back in.

Skins — same game, different wrapper

  • No stealing. Add from a central pile.
  • Steal allowed. Must keep one hoop.
  • Must hit the newest hoop before you steal.

Teaching tips

  • Source: Daly Exercise. Same house rules as the rest of the playbook.
  • Watch for crowding at one piece of kit. Add a second station before you add speed.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Chain Train for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Uses the taught action in Chain Train with control, not a rush that knocks kit or people.
  • Skill focus. Chooses space, timing, or a partner job in Chain Train instead of crowding.
  • Skill focus. Takes a role and shares kit in Chain Train.
  • Character Development. Honest about a miss or a line in Chain Train.
  • Safety. Freeze on the whistle. Soft tags. No throws at a face.
  • Healthy Relationships. Helps a classmate try again after a miss.
Grades 1–2. No stealing. Add from a central pile.
Grades 3–4. Steal allowed. Must keep one hoop.
Grades 5–6. Must hit the newest hoop before you steal.

Safety. No elimination. Freeze on the whistle. Soft two-finger or noodle tags on the back or shoulder — never the head. A miss is a short task, then stay in.
