186. Birthday Lineup

Cooperative & circus

When: December, October · Slot: 16–25 · Whole class · silent.

Students line up silently by birthday, from January to December. Use it to practise communicating without talking.

Equipment: None.

Set-up: One long wall or line. Agree the freeze signal before the first round.

How we play

  1. Silent. Show the month with fingers, then the day.
  2. January is the left end of the gym, December the right.
  3. When the line is still, check from one end. Celebrate the mix-ups, then try once more.
  4. Freeze on the whistle. Reset spots, then the next round.
  5. A drop or a tangle is a restart from the last good shape, not a loss.

Cues

  • Fingers for the month.
  • Walk to the line.
  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • Use names. Ask before you grab a hoop, rope, or balloon.
  • If it drops, restart together — no blame.
  • Quiet voices so the group can hear the next job.

Variations

  • Teacher may whisper for 1–2.
  • Smaller groups of 6 if the whole-class version tangles.
  • Silent round: only gestures, then talk again to plan.
  • Add one extra object (second balloon, extra hoop) when the first task is easy.

Teaching tips

  • Reset: freeze, walk back to start spots, change the key role, then GO. Two or three short rounds beat one long messy one in Birthday Lineup.
  • Watch for one student doing all the work. Give every group a ‘must-touch’ rule.
  • Year plan: December, October — match the month’s sport ball when you can.
  • January left, December right. Check once. Try again.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Birthday Lineup for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Uses the needed skill (pass, step-through, carry, balance) so the group can finish.
  • Skill focus. Plans a simple order or role with teammates before rushing.
  • Skill focus. Takes turns and solves a small problem without grabbing.
  • Character Development. Stays silent through the check.
  • Safety. Moves at a speed the group can control. Consent for partner shapes — a student may say no.
  • Healthy Relationships. Uses gestures, not a shove.
Grades 1–2. Teacher may whisper a month to a stuck pair.
Grades 3–4. Whole-class or groups of 8. Plan for 20 seconds, then try.
Grades 5–6. Add a silent ‘same week’ huddle.

Safety. Ask before you pull a hoop, rope, or teammate. Stop if hair, glasses, or a wrist is caught. No elimination: a sit is a stretch, a jail, a hospital, or a role swap, then back in. Freeze on the whistle. Watch a demo or clip before the first play if you have one.
