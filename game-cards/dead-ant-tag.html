7. Dead Ant Tag

Tag & chase

When: September, December · Slot: 0–5 or 16–25

Tagged players lie on their back like a dead ant. Four teammates each hold an arm or a leg to bring them back.

Equipment: None · optional pool noodles for taggers

Set-up: Open gym with a clear boundary. Choose 3–6 taggers; everyone else runs.

How we play

  1. A tagged player lies on their back with arms and legs in the air like a dead ant.
  2. To free them, four untagged players each hold one arm or one leg. The ant stands and all five are free.
  3. A rescuer who is tagged on the way becomes a dead ant too.
  4. Rotate taggers often. Freeze on the whistle, reset, next round.

More details · Grades K-5

Equipment: None

A simple tag game. A tagged player lies on their back with arms and legs up, like a dead ant. To free them, four untagged players each hold an arm or a leg. Taggers cannot guard or tag the helpers. Works well with large groups. Have everyone spread out and choose a couple of taggers.

  1. Choose 3–6 taggers. Everyone else runs.
  2. A tagged player lies on their back with arms and legs in the air like a dead ant.
  3. To free them, FOUR untagged players must each hold one arm or one leg. Then the ant stands and all five are free.
  4. If a rescuer gets tagged on the way, they become a dead ant too.
  5. Rotate taggers often.

Source · Video

Grades 1–2. Walk-tag first. Teacher plus one student tagger. 30–45 second bursts.
Grades 3–4. Jog-tag. 3–5 taggers. Rotate every round.
Grades 5–6. Full speed with two-finger tags. Add one tactic or a second rescue rule.

Safety. Soft two-finger tags on the back or shoulder, never the head. Clear boundary and freeze signal first. Rotate taggers so the same students are not always chasing. Rescuers hold wrists or ankles gently — no pulling.
