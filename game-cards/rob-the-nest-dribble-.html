60. Rob the Nest (dribble)

Invasion & team

When: October · Slot: 16–25

Dribble collect. Same idea as Robin’s Nest, feet only.

Equipment: 4 hoops, soccer balls.

Set-up: Four teams, four nests. Balls in the middle.

How we play

  1. Dribble a ball into your nest.
  2. First to 3, or count on the whistle.
  3. Later round: you may take from another nest. No standing in the hoop.

Video. Rob the Nest (Prime Coaching Sport)

Clips are demos. Our house rules still apply (no elimination, soft tag; dodgeballs stay on the Dodgeball page).

Grades 1–2. Hands may trap.
Grades 3–4. Feet only.
Grades 5–6. Steal allowed.

Safety. No kicking people.
