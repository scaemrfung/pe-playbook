157. Boom Clap Snatch

Locomotor & listen

When: December, November · Slot: 8-15 · Daly Exercise · slot 8-15.

Move only on the right word: Boom, Clap, Snatch. Daly Exercise game. Nobody sits the period.

Also called: Daly Exercise: Boom Clap Snatch

Equipment: One beanbag or cone per pair

Set-up: Pairs face each other. Object in the middle. Hands on knees.

How we play

  1. Boom = smack the floor.
  2. Clap = clap.
  3. Snatch = grab. Early grab gives the point away.
  4. Freeze on the whistle. Reset kit, then the next round.

If this happens

  • Kit knocks a person: pause, widen space, restart.
  • Argument over a point: teacher call, play on.
  • Someone sits out after a miss: they rejoin on the next turn with a short task instead.

How a round ends. Play 3–6 minutes, or first team/player to the posted target, then rotate jobs.

Cues

  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • One job at a time. Extra players fetch or count.
  • A miss is a short task, then back in.

Skins — same game, different wrapper

  • Two words only.
  • All three words. Mix the order.
  • Add dummy words.

Teaching tips

  • Source: Daly Exercise. Same house rules as the rest of the playbook.
  • Watch for crowding at one piece of kit. Add a second station before you add speed.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Boom Clap Snatch for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Uses the taught action in Boom Clap Snatch with control, not a rush that knocks kit or people.
  • Skill focus. Chooses space, timing, or a partner job in Boom Clap Snatch instead of crowding.
  • Skill focus. Takes a role and shares kit in Boom Clap Snatch.
  • Character Development. Honest about a miss in Boom Clap Snatch.
  • Safety. Freeze on the whistle. Soft tags. No throws at a face.
  • Healthy Relationships. Helps a classmate try again after a miss.
Grades 1–2. Two words only.
Grades 3–4. All three words. Mix the order.
Grades 5–6. Add dummy words.

Safety. No elimination. Freeze on the whistle. Soft two-finger or noodle tags on the back or shoulder — never the head. A miss is a short task, then stay in.
