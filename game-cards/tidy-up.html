28. Tidy Up

Tag & chase

When: September, June · Slot: 8-15

Chase a moving bucket and return balls one at a time.

Equipment: Scattered tennis balls; one bucket

Set-up: Scatter balls. One holder walks or jogs with the bucket overhead.

How we play

  1. On go, collect one ball, chase the holder, place it in the bucket, repeat.
  2. Freeze on the whistle.
Grades 1–2. Two buckets.
Grades 3–4. Walking holder for infants.
Grades 5–6. Colour balls for two teams.

Safety. No elimination. Freeze on the whistle. Soft tags - never the head.
