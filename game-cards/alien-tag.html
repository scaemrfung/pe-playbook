1. Alien Tag

Tag & chase

When: December, February · Slot: 16-25

Tagged aliens drop the waist hoop and do 5 jumping jacks to rejoin.

Equipment: Noodles; hoop per runner

Set-up: Hoops at waists. Chasers with noodles.

How we play

  1. Tag, drop hoop, 5 jacks, pick hoop up, rejoin.
  2. Freeze on the whistle.
Grades 1–2. Marching instead of jacks.
Grades 3–4. More chasers.
Grades 5–6. No hoops - bibs instead.

Safety. No elimination. Freeze on the whistle. Soft tags - never the head.
