151. Toe-tap hoop relay

Relays & stations

When: October · Slot: —

Dribble plus a still foot-skill.

Equipment: Cones, hoop, soccer ball.

Set-up: Weave to a hoop.

How we play

  1. Weave to the hoop. Ten toe-taps, switching feet. Dribble home.
Grades 1–2. Five taps.
Grades 3–4. Ten taps.
Grades 5–6. Ten taps, then a sole-roll.

Safety. Ball stays in the hoop during taps.
