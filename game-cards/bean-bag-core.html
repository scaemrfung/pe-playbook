211. Bean Bag Core

Fitness & landings

When: February, April, January · Slot: 8-15 · Daly Exercise · slot 8-15.

From a plank, empty your hoop into someone else's. Daly Exercise game. Nobody sits the period.

Also called: Daly Exercise: Bean Bag Core

Equipment: One hoop and bean bags per player

Set-up: Hoops close enough to reach. Default plank is knees-down.

How we play

  1. Stay in plank. Move one bag at a time.
  2. Feet and knees do not walk the hoop.
  3. 30-45 seconds. Rest. Repeat.
  4. Freeze on the whistle. Reset kit, then the next round.

If this happens

  • Kit knocks a person: pause, widen space, restart.
  • Argument over a point: teacher call, play on.
  • Someone sits out after a miss: they rejoin on the next turn with a short task instead.

How a round ends. Play 3–6 minutes, or first team/player to the posted target, then rotate jobs.

Cues

  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • One job at a time. Extra players fetch or count.
  • A miss is a short task, then back in.

Skins — same game, different wrapper

  • Standing athletic stance.
  • Knees-down plank. 30 seconds.
  • Full plank. Non-dominant hand.

Teaching tips

  • Source: Daly Exercise. Same house rules as the rest of the playbook.
  • Watch for crowding at one piece of kit. Add a second station before you add speed.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Bean Bag Core for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Uses the taught action in Bean Bag Core with control, not a rush that knocks kit or people.
  • Skill focus. Chooses space, timing, or a partner job in Bean Bag Core instead of crowding.
  • Skill focus. Takes a role and shares kit in Bean Bag Core.
  • Character Development. Honest about a miss in Bean Bag Core.
  • Safety. Freeze on the whistle.
  • Healthy Relationships. Helps a classmate try again after a miss.
Grades 1–2. Standing athletic stance.
Grades 3–4. Knees-down plank. 30 seconds.
Grades 5–6. Full plank. Non-dominant hand.
