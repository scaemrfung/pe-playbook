80. Catch the Ice Cream

Target & send

When: September, October, January, March, June · Slot: 8-15 · Daly Exercise · slot 8-15.

Throw a bean bag into a partner's cone without a hand trap. Daly Exercise game. Nobody sits the period.

Also called: Daly Exercise: Catch the Ice Cream

Equipment: Cone and bean bag per pair

Set-up: Pairs stand a short distance apart, each with a cone.

How we play

  1. Gentle toss to the cone.
  2. Receiver uses the cone only.
  3. Step back if consistent.
  4. Freeze on the whistle. Reset kit, then the next round.

If this happens

  • Kit knocks a person: pause, widen space, restart.
  • Argument over a point: teacher call, play on.
  • Someone sits out after a miss: they rejoin on the next turn with a short task instead.

How a round ends. Play 3–6 minutes, or first team/player to the posted target, then rotate jobs.

Cues

  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • One job at a time. Extra players fetch or count.
  • A miss is a short task, then back in.

Skins — same game, different wrapper

  • Larger cone.
  • Seated catchers.
  • Tennis ball progression.

Teaching tips

  • Source: Daly Exercise. Same house rules as the rest of the playbook.
  • Watch for crowding at one piece of kit. Add a second station before you add speed.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Catch the Ice Cream for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Uses the taught action in Catch the Ice Cream with control, not a rush that knocks kit or people.
  • Skill focus. Chooses space, timing, or a partner job in Catch the Ice Cream instead of crowding.
  • Skill focus. Takes a role and shares kit in Catch the Ice Cream.
  • Character Development. Honest about a miss or a drop in Catch the Ice Cream.
  • Safety. Freeze on the whistle. Soft tags. No throws at a face.
  • Healthy Relationships. Helps a classmate try again after a miss or a drop.
Grades 1–2. Larger cone.
Grades 3–4. Seated catchers.
Grades 5–6. Tennis ball progression.

Safety. No elimination. Freeze on the whistle. Soft two-finger or noodle tags on the back or shoulder — never the head. A miss is a short task, then stay in.
