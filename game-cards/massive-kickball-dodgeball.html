124. Massive Kickball Dodgeball

Strike & field

When: June, December · Slot: 16–25

Kickball meets dodgeball for up to about 50 players. Kick, run to a mat, and dodge the fielders' throws.

Equipment: 2 foam balls, exercise mats as bases

Set-up: Two teams. Exercise mats as bases or islands around the gym. Two foam balls.

How we play

  1. A kicker kicks one ball and runs to a mat.
  2. Fielders may throw a foam ball to hit the runner below the waist. A runner on a mat is safe.
  3. A hit runner jogs back to the kicking line and joins the end of the kicking queue — no sitting out.
  4. Keep both balls live. Switch kicking and fielding after a set number of kickers.

More details · Grades 4-8

Equipment: 2 foam balls, exercise mats

A large-group game (up to 50 players) that combines kickball and dodgeball. Use it when you have a very large group and want something different.

  1. Two teams. Use exercise mats as bases/islands. Two foam balls.
  2. A kicker kicks one ball and runs to a mat. Fielders may throw a foam ball to hit the runner below the shoulders.
  3. A hit runner is out. A runner who reaches a mat is safe.
  4. Keep both balls live so the field never stalls. Switch kicking/fielding after a set number of kickers.
  5. Works with up to about 50 players.

Source · Video

Grades 1–2. Not for grades 1–2 — use Poison Ball (roll only) or a target game instead.
Grades 3–4. Rolls or underhand throws below the knees. Short 2–3 minute rounds with a full reset.
Grades 5–6. Play as written with house rules. Students can referee a line or count hits.

Safety. Foam balls only. Throws below the waist; no head shots and no close-range throws. A hit is a short stretch or a jail with a clear way back — nobody sits the period. Fetch balls along the side. Dodgeball is optional programming: use it only where it fits the group.
