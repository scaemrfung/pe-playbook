99. Prison Ball

Target & send

When: December, January · Slot: 16–25

A hit sends you to the prison behind the other team. Hit someone from prison and you come home.

Equipment: Foam balls, cones for the prison lines

Set-up: Two teams, one half each. Cone a prison zone behind each end line.

How we play

  1. If you are hit below the waist, go to the prison behind the other team.
  2. From prison you may throw at the team in front of you. A hit brings you home and sends that player to prison.
  3. Balls come from the front and from behind — check all directions.
  4. Play timed games.
  5. House rule: below-the-waist hits with foam balls. A hit is a 10-second stretch or a trip to jail with a clear way back — nobody sits for long. Full rules sit on the Dodgeball page.

More details · Grades 4-8

Equipment: Foam Balls

A dodgeball-type game for two teams. Each team has a prison area at the far end of the gym, behind the other team. A player who is hit goes to the prison behind the other team. They stay there until they throw a ball from prison and hit someone; then they return home, and the player they hit goes to prison. Players must watch in front, behind and to the sides because balls come from all directions.

  1. Two teams, each half. Behind each end is a PRISON zone.
  2. If you are hit with a foam ball (below shoulders), go to the prison BEHIND the other team.
  3. From prison you may throw at the team in front of you. If you hit someone, you return home and they go to prison.
  4. Balls fly from front and behind, so players must check all directions.
  5. Play timed games. Foam balls only.

Source · Video

Grades 1–2. Not for grades 1–2 — use Poison Ball (roll only) or a target game instead.
Grades 3–4. Rolls or underhand throws below the knees. Short 2–3 minute rounds with a full reset.
Grades 5–6. Play as written with house rules. Students can referee a line or count hits.

Safety. Foam balls only. Throws below the waist; no head shots and no close-range throws. A hit is a short stretch or a jail with a clear way back — nobody sits the period. Fetch balls along the side. Dodgeball is optional programming: use it only where it fits the group.
