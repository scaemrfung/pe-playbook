213. Bench Balance

Fitness & landings

When: April · Slot: 8-15

Walk the bench and transfer cones from one side hoop to the other.

Equipment: Bench, side hoops, cones

Set-up: Bench per team. Hoops both sides. Cones in one hoop.

How we play

  1. Mount, walk, move one cone across, dismount.
  2. Next player.
  3. Freeze on the whistle.
Grades 1–2. Floor line instead of a bench.
Grades 3–4. Hand hold.
Grades 5–6. Carry on a racket.

Safety. No elimination. Freeze on the whistle.
