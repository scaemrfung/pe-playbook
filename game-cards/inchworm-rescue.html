220. Inchworm Rescue

Fitness & landings

When: April · Slot: 16-25 · Daly Exercise · slot 16-25.

Feet stay planted. Walk the hands out and back with one ball. Daly Exercise game. Nobody sits the period.

Also called: Daly Exercise: Inchworm Rescue

Equipment: Two hoops per pupil; five balls in the far hoop

Set-up: Feet in home hoop. Five balls wait in hoop 2.

How we play

  1. Walk hands out, grab one ball, walk back, drop it in hoop 1.
  2. One ball per trip.
  3. Repeat until all five are home.
  4. Freeze on the whistle. Reset kit, then the next round.

If this happens

  • Kit knocks a person: pause, widen space, restart.
  • Argument over a point: teacher call, play on.
  • Someone sits out after a miss: they rejoin on the next turn with a short task instead.

How a round ends. Play 3–6 minutes, or first team/player to the posted target, then rotate jobs.

Cues

  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • One job at a time. Extra players fetch or count.
  • A miss is a short task, then back in.

Skins — same game, different wrapper

  • Fewer balls. A knee down is legal.
  • Five balls. Feet stay planted.
  • Time cap.

Teaching tips

  • Source: Daly Exercise. Same house rules as the rest of the playbook.
  • Watch for crowding at one piece of kit. Add a second station before you add speed.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Inchworm Rescue for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Uses the taught action in Inchworm Rescue with control, not a rush that knocks kit or people.
  • Skill focus. Chooses space, timing, or a partner job in Inchworm Rescue instead of crowding.
  • Skill focus. Takes a role and shares kit in Inchworm Rescue.
  • Character Development. Honest about a miss in Inchworm Rescue.
  • Safety. Freeze on the whistle. Soft tags. No throws at a face.
  • Healthy Relationships. Helps a classmate try again after a miss.
Grades 1–2. Fewer balls. A knee down is legal.
Grades 3–4. Five balls. Feet stay planted.
Grades 5–6. Time cap.

Safety. No elimination. Freeze on the whistle. Soft two-finger or noodle tags on the back or shoulder — never the head. A miss is a short task, then stay in.
