141. Jump to Hoop Race

Relays & stations

When: February, May · Slot: 16-25 · Daly Exercise · slot 16-25.

Jump hoop-to-hoop; next runner waits for a sit at the end. Daly Exercise game. Nobody sits the period.

Also called: Daly Exercise: Jump to Hoop Race

Equipment: One hoop per player as stepping-stones

Set-up: Hoops in a lane. Team behind hoop 1.

How we play

  1. Jump to the finish and sit.
  2. Sitting starts the next teammate.
  3. First team all seated wins.
  4. Freeze on the whistle. Reset kit, then the next round.

If this happens

  • Kit knocks a person: pause, widen space, restart.
  • Argument over a point: teacher call, play on.
  • Someone sits out after a miss: they rejoin on the next turn with a short task instead.

How a round ends. Play 3–6 minutes, or first team/player to the posted target, then rotate jobs.

Cues

  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • One job at a time. Extra players fetch or count.
  • A miss is a short task, then back in.

Skins — same game, different wrapper

  • Step-in allowed.
  • Larger hoops.
  • Travel back as well.

Teaching tips

  • Source: Daly Exercise. Same house rules as the rest of the playbook.
  • Watch for crowding at one piece of kit. Add a second station before you add speed.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Jump to Hoop Race for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Uses the taught action in Jump to Hoop Race with control, not a rush that knocks kit or people.
  • Skill focus. Chooses space, timing, or a partner job in Jump to Hoop Race instead of crowding.
  • Skill focus. Takes a role and shares kit in Jump to Hoop Race.
  • Character Development. Honest about a miss in Jump to Hoop Race.
  • Safety. Freeze on the whistle.
  • Healthy Relationships. Helps a classmate try again after a miss.
