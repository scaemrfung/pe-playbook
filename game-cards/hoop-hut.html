196. Hoop Hut

Cooperative & circus

When: February, December · Slot: 16-25 · Daly Exercise · slot 16-25.

Build a six-hoop hut from a picture and carry it to a cone. Daly Exercise game. Nobody sits the period.

Also called: Daly Exercise: Hoop Hut

Equipment: Six hoops per group; a picture; a cone 10-15 m away

Set-up: Show a finished hut first.

How we play

  1. Group builds to match the picture.
  2. When it stands, lift and walk toward the cone.
  3. Collapse = carry back, rebuild.
  4. Freeze on the whistle. Reset kit, then the next round.

If this happens

  • Kit knocks a person: pause, widen space, restart.
  • Argument over a point: teacher call, play on.
  • Someone sits out after a miss: they rejoin on the next turn with a short task instead.

How a round ends. Play 3–6 minutes, or first team/player to the posted target, then rotate jobs.

Cues

  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • One job at a time. Extra players fetch or count.
  • A miss is a short task, then back in.

Skins — same game, different wrapper

  • Fewer hoops. Slide instead of a full lift.
  • Six hoops. Slow walk.
  • Picture only. Time cap.

Teaching tips

  • Source: Daly Exercise. Same house rules as the rest of the playbook.
  • Watch for crowding at one piece of kit. Add a second station before you add speed.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Hoop Hut for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Uses the taught action in Hoop Hut with control, not a rush that knocks kit or people.
  • Skill focus. Chooses space, timing, or a partner job in Hoop Hut instead of crowding.
  • Skill focus. Takes a role and shares kit in Hoop Hut.
  • Character Development. Honest about a miss in Hoop Hut.
  • Safety. Freeze on the whistle.
  • Healthy Relationships. Helps a classmate try again after a miss.
Grades 1–2. Fewer hoops. Slide instead of a full lift.
Grades 3–4. Six hoops. Slow walk.
Grades 5–6. Picture only. Time cap.
