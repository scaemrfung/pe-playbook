90. Hoop Target Bowling

Target & send

When: October, January, June · Slot: 8-15

Land an overarm throw in a hoop, seated or standing.

Equipment: Floor hoops, soft balls

Set-up: Hoops at mixed distances. Throwers in front.

How we play

  1. Overarm into the hoop.
  2. Coach the up-back and down-through arm path.
  3. Freeze on the whistle.
Grades 1–2. Shorter distance, larger hoop.
Grades 3–4. Two-hand pass first.
Grades 5–6. Wall target if floor hoops are hard to see.

Safety. No elimination. Freeze on the whistle.
