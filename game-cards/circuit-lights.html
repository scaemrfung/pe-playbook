82. Circuit Lights

Target & send

When: December · Slot: 8-15

Slide the bag into cones to turn the lights on.

Equipment: Bean bag, spot, 6 cones

Set-up: Six cones as unlit lights. Pair shares a bean bag.

How we play

  1. Slide the bag.
  2. Land in a cone = stand that cone up.
  3. Miss = partner's turn.
Grades 1–2. Larger cones.
Grades 3–4. Any contact counts.

Safety. No elimination. Freeze on the whistle.
