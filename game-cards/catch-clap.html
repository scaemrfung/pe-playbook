78. Catch Clap

Target & send

When: September, March · Slot: 8-15

Clap once, then catch. Fakes and drops are misses.

Equipment: One ball

Set-up: Teacher in the middle, class evenly spaced.

How we play

  1. Throw to a pupil.
  2. They must clap then catch.
  3. Fake throw plus clap, drop, or catch with no clap = miss, then rejoin.
Grades 1–2. Two claps.
Grades 3–4. Pupil becomes thrower.
Grades 5–6. Score personal catches.

Safety. No elimination. Freeze on the whistle.
