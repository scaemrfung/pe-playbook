32. Waspital

Tag & chase

When: January · Slot: 0–5

Everyone-is-it tag with a stretch hospital.

Equipment: 1–2 hoops.

Set-up: Hoops are the waspital.

How we play

  1. Everyone is it. Soft two-finger tag.
  2. Two tags = walk to the hoop, stretch, re-enter.
Grades 1–2. Walk-jog.
Grades 3–4. Jog.
Grades 5–6. Students lead the hospital stretch.

Safety. Cap at 5 minutes. No head tags.
