224. Long Jump Challenge

Fitness & landings

When: February, April, May · Slot: 8-15

Beat the team mark from the same line.

Equipment: 1 cone per team; jump line

Set-up: Clear landing area. Shared jump line.

How we play

  1. Two-foot take-off, balanced landing, cone behind the heels.
  2. Cone moves only if the next jump is longer.
  3. Freeze on the whistle.
Grades 1–2. Mat landing.
Grades 3–4. Measure every jump independently.
Grades 5–6. One-foot category.

Safety. No elimination. Freeze on the whistle.
