188. Bounce & Scoop

Cooperative & circus

When: September, January, March, June · Slot: 8-15 · Daly Exercise · slot 8-15.

Scoop a one-bounce feed in a cone. Daly Exercise game. Nobody sits the period.

Also called: Daly Exercise: Bounce & Scoop

Equipment: Tennis balls and a cone

Set-up: Feeder has the balls. Partner has the cone.

How we play

  1. Bounce one at a time.
  2. Scoop after one bounce.
  3. Caught balls stay.
  4. Freeze on the whistle. Reset kit, then the next round.

If this happens

  • Kit knocks a person: pause, widen space, restart.
  • Argument over a point: teacher call, play on.
  • Someone sits out after a miss: they rejoin on the next turn with a short task instead.

How a round ends. Play 3–6 minutes, or first team/player to the posted target, then rotate jobs.

Cues

  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • One job at a time. Extra players fetch or count.
  • A miss is a short task, then back in.

Skins — same game, different wrapper

  • Larger ball and bucket.
  • Allow two bounces.
  • Bounce to a hoop.

Teaching tips

  • Source: Daly Exercise. Same house rules as the rest of the playbook.
  • Watch for crowding at one piece of kit. Add a second station before you add speed.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Bounce & Scoop for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Uses the taught action in Bounce & Scoop with control, not a rush that knocks kit or people.
  • Skill focus. Chooses space, timing, or a partner job in Bounce & Scoop instead of crowding.
  • Skill focus. Takes a role and shares kit in Bounce & Scoop.
  • Character Development. Honest about a miss in Bounce & Scoop.
  • Safety. Freeze on the whistle. No throws at a face.
  • Healthy Relationships. Helps a classmate try again after a miss.
Grades 1–2. Larger ball and bucket.
Grades 3–4. Allow two bounces.
Grades 5–6. Bounce to a hoop.
