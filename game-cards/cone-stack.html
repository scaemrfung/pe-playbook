192. Cone Stack

Cooperative & circus

When: February, December · Slot: 16-25

Place a cone in the hoop and still catch the bib.

Equipment: Pairs: one bib, one hoop, a stack of cones

Set-up: Thrower outside the hoop. High friendly toss.

How we play

  1. Toss the bib up.
  2. Partner places one cone and catches the bib.
  3. Swap. Build the tallest clean stack.
Grades 1–2. Place the cone without needing the catch.
Grades 3–4. Place and catch. One cone per toss.
Grades 5–6. Two cones per toss if the toss is high enough.

Safety. No elimination. Freeze on the whistle.
