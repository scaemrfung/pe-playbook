48. Four Corner Flags

Invasion & team

When: September, October · Slot: 16–25 · Four teams · 8–12 min · one hoop and 2–3 flags per corner.

Four teams steal flags one at a time from other hoops and are safe in their own quadrant. Use it to practise attacking and defending space.

Equipment: 16 beanbags (4 colours), 4 hoops, 4 pinnies colours, cones.

Set-up: Gym in four quadrants. Each team’s flags start in its hoop. Agree the freeze signal before the first round.

How we play

  1. Steal one flag at a time from another hoop and return it home.
  2. You are safe in your own quadrant. Tagged in another quadrant: walk the flag back and return home.
  3. No standing over your own hoop (no puppy-guarding).
  4. Play 4–6 minute periods. Extra players are bumpers, walls, or the next wave — they stay in a job.
  5. Freeze on the whistle. Reset spots, then the next round.

Cues

  • Safe in your own quarter.
  • One flag at a time. Walk a jail rescue.
  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • Head up. Pass or move — do not crowd the ball.
  • Find a passing lane, not a pile-up.
  • Soft send; the ball stays below the chest unless the sport needs a high set.

Variations

  • Two-team Capture the Flag: one flag each, prison, walk-home rescue.
  • Capture the cone: steal standing cones instead of pinnies.
  • Must complete one pass before a shot or a score.
  • Add a second foam ball if more than 24 students so more players get a touch.
  • Extra players are walking walls — they may deflect a ball with feet but may not score.

Teaching tips

  • Reset: freeze, walk back to start spots, change the key role, then GO. Two or three short rounds beat one long messy one in Four Corner Flags.
  • Watch for one student holding the ball. Cue a pass or a 3-second pivot.
  • Year plan: September, October — match the month’s sport ball when you can.
  • Split the gym into four. Each team has a hoop (flag nest) and a jail cone.
  • Start with 2 flags of each other colour in every nest, or 3 like-coloured flags to steal.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Four Corner Flags for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Sends, receives, or travels with control using the taught skill (foot, hand, or implement).
  • Skill focus. One object or one rescue at a time.
  • Skill focus. Walks the flag back after a tag, without arguing.
  • Character Development. Honest score and honest tags; tries again after a miss.
  • Safety. Heads up. No body checks. Equipment stays below the agreed height.
  • Healthy Relationships. Calls a teammate’s name; includes a player who has not touched the flag.
Grades 1–2. Small space, big targets, walking or jogging. Teacher may be a walking bumper. Hands may help a trap if the ball is soccer.
Grades 3–4. Full game as written, foam or real sport ball as the unit says. One pass before a score if the class crowds.
Grades 5–6. Outdoor map-optional version in May.

Safety. No body checks. No elimination: a sit is a stretch, a jail, a hospital, or a role swap, then back in. Freeze on the whistle. Watch a demo or clip before the first play if you have one.
