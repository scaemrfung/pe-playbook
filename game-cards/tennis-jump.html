237. Tennis Jump

Fitness & landings

When: April, May · Slot: 8-15 · Daly Exercise · slot 8-15.

Jump so the rolling ball ends up inside the cone between your feet. Daly Exercise game. Nobody sits the period.

Also called: Daly Exercise: Tennis Jump

Equipment: Pairs: one cone, one tennis ball

Set-up: Receiver stands the cone between their feet.

How we play

  1. Partner rolls the ball gently.
  2. Jump and land so the ball is trapped in the cone.
  3. Clean trap = 1. Swap.
  4. Freeze on the whistle. Reset kit, then the next round.

If this happens

  • Kit knocks a person: pause, widen space, restart.
  • Argument over a point: teacher call, play on.
  • Someone sits out after a miss: they rejoin on the next turn with a short task instead.

How a round ends. Play 3–6 minutes, or first team/player to the posted target, then rotate jobs.

Cues

  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • One job at a time. Extra players fetch or count.
  • A miss is a short task, then back in.

Skins — same game, different wrapper

  • Larger cone. Rolled bean bag.
  • Tennis ball. First to 5 traps.
  • Longer roll.

Teaching tips

  • Source: Daly Exercise. Same house rules as the rest of the playbook.
  • Watch for crowding at one piece of kit. Add a second station before you add speed.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Tennis Jump for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Uses the taught action in Tennis Jump with control, not a rush that knocks kit or people.
  • Skill focus. Chooses space, timing, or a partner job in Tennis Jump instead of crowding.
  • Skill focus. Takes a role and shares kit in Tennis Jump.
  • Character Development. Honest about a miss in Tennis Jump.
  • Safety. Freeze on the whistle. No throws at a face.
  • Healthy Relationships. Helps a classmate try again after a miss.
