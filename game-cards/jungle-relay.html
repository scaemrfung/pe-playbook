142. Jungle Relay

Relays & stations

When: December, February · Slot: 16–25 · Even teams · a turn cone · optional bag or cone for the back

Each runner uses a real animal movement. Disguised sprinting does not count.

Also called: Jungle Run Relays · Animal Crawl · Turtle Race · Rabbit Hole

Equipment: Turn cone 8–15 m away · optional bag or cone for the back

Set-up: Show 6 legal animals. Separate lanes. Cone 8–15 m away.

How we play

  1. First player animal-moves to the cone and back, tags.
  2. Next player must pick a different animal unless you have allowed repeats.
  3. Whole team goes.

If this happens

  • Disguised sprint: send them back to do the animal.
  • Bag or cone falls off the back: replace and continue from there.

How a round ends. First team home, or fewest drops.

Cues

  • Complete the course.
  • Hand-off, not a shout.
  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • Wait behind the cone. Go on the high-five.
  • Stay in your lane. Pass on the right if you meet someone.
  • Quality over sprint — reset if the skill falls apart.

Skins — same game, different wrapper

  • Cone on the mid-back (Turtle).
  • Crawl under a hoop-on-cones (Rabbit Hole).
  • Class as tunnels and bridges, other half travels.
  • Animals: pick from a posted list.

Teaching tips

  • Also called: Jungle Run Relays; Animals; Animal Crawl; Turtle Race; Rabbit Hole.
  • House rules: Freeze on the whistle. Tags are two-finger or noodle on the back or shoulder. A miss is a short task and you stay in. Extra players get a job (bumper, next wave, fetcher). No elimination.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Jungle Run Relays for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Waits behind the line for the tag.
  • Skill focus. Starts on the tag, not early; chooses a path that does not cut off another team.
  • Skill focus. Hands off — does not throw at a teammate.
  • Character Development. No extra steps past the line; a drop is picked up and the leg continues.
  • Safety. Runs in a lane. No diving at the line. Equipment carried, not thrown at people.
  • Healthy Relationships. Encourages the next runner; does not shame a drop.
Grades 1–2. Walk or skip. Shorter lane. Redo a trip if the skill falls apart — no extra penalty laps.
Grades 3–4. Jog. Full lane. High-five hand-off. One quality redo if a pin or ball is dropped.
Grades 5–6. Skip one way, jog back — or weaker foot/hand home. A student starter on the whistle.

Safety. Pass on the right. No diving at a hoop, pin, or beanbag. No elimination: a sit is a stretch, a jail, a hospital, or a role swap, then back in. Freeze on the whistle. Watch a demo or clip before the first play if you have one. Tags are two-finger or noodle on the back or shoulder. A miss is a short task and you stay in. Extra players get a job (bumper, next wave, fetcher).
