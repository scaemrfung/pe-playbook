11. Frog Tag

Tag & chase

When: December · Slot: 16-25

Tagged players crouch; a leapfrog frees them.

Equipment: Pool noodles

Set-up: Two tigers with noodles. Court marked.

How we play

  1. Gentle tag.
  2. Crouch as a frog.
  3. Untagged players leapfrog to free.
Grades 1–2. Step-over instead of leapfrog.
Grades 3–4. More taggers.
Grades 5–6. No noodles - hand tags.

Safety. No elimination. Freeze on the whistle. Soft tags - never the head.
