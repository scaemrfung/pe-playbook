91. Human Catapult

Target & send

When: January, March · Slot: 8-15 · Daly Exercise · slot 8-15.

The sit-up is the throw. Accuracy after a sit-up is the skill. Daly Exercise game. Nobody sits the period.

Also called: Daly Exercise: Human Catapult

Equipment: Mat, soft ball, cone behind each partner

Set-up: Partners sit back-to-back. Cone behind each, out of the face line.

How we play

  1. A sit-up throws at B's cone.
  2. Knockdown = 1. Reset.
  3. Swap. Freeze on the whistle.

If this happens

  • Kit knocks a person: pause, widen space, restart.
  • Argument over a point: teacher call, play on.
  • Someone sits out after a miss: they rejoin on the next turn with a short task instead.

How a round ends. Play 3–6 minutes, or first team/player to the posted target, then rotate jobs.

Cues

  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • One job at a time. Extra players fetch or count.
  • A miss is a short task, then back in.

Skins — same game, different wrapper

  • Seated chest-pass. No sit-up required.
  • Sit-up then throw.
  • Non-dominant throw.

Teaching tips

  • Source: Daly Exercise. Same house rules as the rest of the playbook.
  • Watch for crowding at one piece of kit. Add a second station before you add speed.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Human Catapult for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Uses the taught action in Human Catapult with control, not a rush that knocks kit or people.
  • Skill focus. Chooses space, timing, or a partner job in Human Catapult instead of crowding.
  • Skill focus. Takes a role and shares kit in Human Catapult.
  • Character Development. Honest about a miss or a line in Human Catapult.
  • Safety. Freeze on the whistle. No throws at a face.
  • Healthy Relationships. Helps a classmate try again after a miss.
