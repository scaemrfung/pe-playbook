189. Bridges & Rivers

Cooperative & circus

When: April · Slot: 0–5

On the call, half the class makes bridges or rivers and the other half crawls under or jumps over.

Equipment: None · optional music

Set-up: Half the class are structures, half are travellers. Scattered in open space.

How we play

  1. On “BRIDGES,” structures make a stable bridge (hands and feet down, back flat). Travellers crawl under.
  2. On “RIVERS,” structures lie or crouch low. Travellers jump over.
  3. Teacher or a student caller switches the call. Play to music.
  4. Swap jobs so everyone travels and everyone holds.

More details · Grades K-4

Equipment: None

Players form bridges or rivers and jump or crawl depending on the call. It works well with music. Make half the players bridges and half rivers. When the music starts (or on a signal), everyone moves around. When it stops, call “bridges” or “rivers.” On “bridges,” bridges make a bridge with their bodies and rivers crawl under as many as they can. On “rivers,” rivers lie flat and bridges jump over as many as they can. Restart the music and repeat. Switch roles after a while.

  1. On “BRIDGES,” some students make a stable bridge (hands and feet on the floor, back flat). Others are rivers and crawl under.
  2. On “RIVERS,” some students lie or crouch as a low river. Others jump over.
  3. Teacher (or a student caller) switches the call. Play to music.
  4. Rotate who is the structure and who is travelling so everyone jumps and everyone holds.
  5. Cue safe landings and no collapsing on a crawler.

Source · Video

Grades 1–2. Small groups of 4–5. Teacher shows it first. No timer.
Grades 3–4. Groups of 6–8. Time trial against your own best.
Grades 5–6. Whole class or team vs team. Add a rule (no talking, fewer touches).

Safety. Stable bridges only. Safe landings. Nobody collapses onto a crawler; jump over the legs, not the head.
