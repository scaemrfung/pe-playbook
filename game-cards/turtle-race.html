239. Turtle Race

Fitness & landings

When: April · Slot: 8-15

Crawl with a cone on the back; first across with it still on wins.

Equipment: 1 cone per racer

Set-up: Start and finish. Turtle position, cone on mid-back.

How we play

  1. On go, crawl.
  2. Cone off = stop, replace, continue.
  3. Freeze on the whistle.
Grades 1–2. Bean bag instead.
Grades 3–4. Head-carry walk.
Grades 5–6. Partner replaces the cone.

Safety. No elimination. Freeze on the whistle.
