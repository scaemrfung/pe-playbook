128. Thunderball

Strike & field

When: June · Slot: 16–25 · Whole class · 6 min · wiffle or t-ball, 30 cones.

Players hit or kick the ball through three rows of cones for 1, 2 or 3 points while the defence stops it. Use it to practise striking and fielding.

Equipment: Wiffle or t-ball, bat or kick, 30 cones in 3 rows.

Set-up: Three rows of cones across the gym. Hitting team on one end. Defence in the lanes. Agree the freeze signal before the first round.

How we play

  1. Each hitter tries to send the ball through the rows.
  2. Past row 1 = 1 point, row 2 = 2, row 3 = 3.
  3. Defence stops the ball before the next row. Everyone hits, then swap.
  4. Balls come back along the side. Restart on a teacher call, not a scramble through the middle.
  5. Freeze on the whistle. Reset spots, then the next round.

Cues

  • Hit through the rows, not at people.
  • Defence stops the ball before it crosses.
  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • Ready position in the field — feet still, hands out.
  • Hit, then run the path. Do not watch your own hit.
  • Fielders send to the base or hoop, not at a runner.

Variations

  • One hitter at a time; next batter ready.
  • Tee or a bounced teacher-toss for grades 1–2.
  • Three-pitch maximum, then a run-out anyway so the line keeps moving.
  • Fielders rotate one spot after each batter.

Teaching tips

  • Reset: freeze, walk back to start spots, change the key role, then GO. Two or three short rounds beat one long messy one in Thunderball.
  • Watch for a long batting line. Use a continuous format or a 3-pitch max.
  • Year plan: June — match the month’s sport ball when you can.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Thunderball for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Sends the ball through a row, not at a person.
  • Skill focus. Knows when to send through a gap in the rows.
  • Skill focus. Takes batting order and a fielding spot; rotates so the same student is not always pitcher.
  • Character Development. Honest row count; no arguing a call.
  • Safety. Hitters wait behind the line; no full bats indoors.
  • Healthy Relationships. Calls ‘mine’ on a fly; helps a defender cover the next row.
Grades 1–2. Kick off a cone. One row only.
Grades 3–4. Live send (kick, strike, or throw). Three chances, then a run so the line moves.
Grades 5–6. Live toss. Defence must pass once before they can stop it.

Safety. Hitters wait behind a line. No full hard bats indoors — wiffle or t-ball only. No elimination: a sit is a stretch, a jail, a hospital, or a role swap, then back in. Freeze on the whistle. Watch a demo or clip before the first play if you have one.
