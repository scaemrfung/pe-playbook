117. Continuous Kick Ball

Strike & field

When: June · Slot: 16–25 · Two teams · 10 min · playground ball, 3–4 hoops as bases.

Several runners can be on base at once, and play stops when the kicker’s partner places the ball back on the cone. Use it as a kickball game that keeps more runners moving.

Equipment: Playground or t-ball, saucer cone, bases or hoops.

Set-up: Two teams. Each kicker has a partner on the fielding team. Ball starts on a cone. Agree the freeze signal before the first round.

How we play

  1. Kick the ball off the cone and run. Several runners may be on base at once.
  2. Anyone may field, but only the kicker’s partner may place the ball back on the cone to stop play.
  3. Runners caught between bases when the ball is replaced are out of that run — they rotate, they do not sit the inning.
  4. A catch in the air is an out for that kicker only; other runners keep going until the cone is reset.
  5. Balls come back along the side. Restart on a teacher call, not a scramble through the middle.
  6. Freeze on the whistle. Reset spots, then the next round.

Cues

  • Everyone kicks.
  • Throw to the base, not the runner.
  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • Ready position in the field — feet still, hands out.
  • Hit, then run the path. Do not watch your own hit.
  • Fielders send to the base or hoop, not at a runner.

Variations

  • One-base and back (Quick Baseball).
  • Tee instead of a roll.
  • Tee or a bounced teacher-toss for grades 1–2.
  • Three-pitch maximum, then a run-out anyway so the line keeps moving.
  • Fielders rotate one spot after each batter.

Teaching tips

  • Reset: freeze, walk back to start spots, change the key role, then GO. Two or three short rounds beat one long messy one in Continuous Kick Ball.
  • Watch for a long batting line. Use a continuous format or a 3-pitch max.
  • Year plan: June — match the month’s sport ball when you can.
  • Pitcher rolls. Kicker kicks and runs. There are no strikeouts — a miss is a re-roll or a walk to first after two misses.
  • Fielders get an ‘out’ by catching a fly, or by getting the ball to the base the runner is going to.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Continuous Kick Ball for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Everyone gets a kick or hit.
  • Skill focus. Ball goes to a base or hoop, not at the runner.
  • Skill focus. Takes batting order and a fielding spot; rotates so the same student is not always pitcher.
  • Character Development. Honest out or safe; no arguing a force.
  • Safety. Looks up after contact. Bat or kicker away from the waiting line.
  • Healthy Relationships. Calls ‘mine’ on a fly; helps a runner who is unsure of the next hoop.
Grades 1–2. Everyone runs to first only. Teacher helps reset the cone.
Grades 3–4. Live send (kick, strike, or throw). Three chances, then a run so the line moves.
Grades 5–6. Full diamond. Force at two bases.

Safety. No slide tackles, no body checks. The ball is not thrown at a face. No elimination: a sit is a stretch, a jail, a hospital, or a role swap, then back in. Freeze on the whistle. Watch a demo or clip before the first play if you have one.
