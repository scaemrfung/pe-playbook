57. Protect the House

Invasion & team

When: September, November, January · Slot: 8-15

Knock the noodle off the house cone; defenders intercept.

Equipment: Cone circle, centre cone, balanced noodle, softballs

Set-up: Noodle balanced on the centre cone. Two defenders inside.

How we play

  1. Throw to dislodge the noodle.
  2. Defenders block.
  3. Reset and rotate defenders often.
Grades 1–2. More defenders.
Grades 3–4. Must roll.
Grades 5–6. Two houses at once.

Safety. No elimination. Freeze on the whistle.
