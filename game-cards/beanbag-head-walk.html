212. Beanbag head walk

Fitness & landings

When: December, April · Slot: —

Still head, airplane arms.

Equipment: Beanbag and a hoop per team.

Set-up: Lane to a hoop.

How we play

  1. Bag on the head. Walk to the hoop. Drop it in. Jog home without the bag.
Grades 1–2. Hold the bag with one finger if it slips.
Grades 3–4. No hands.
Grades 5–6. Walk backward home.

Safety. No running with a bag on the head.
