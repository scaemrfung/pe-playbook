54. Hurley Burley

Invasion & team

When: October, January · Slot: 16–25 · Grades 3–6 · 8 min · one sport ball.

Players may kick, bounce, throw or run a few steps, but a soft tip means they must pass right away. Use it to practise quick decisions in an invasion game.

Equipment: Soccer or playground ball, two goals, pinnies.

Set-up: Two teams. Teacher tosses in. Pinnies on one team. Show the two attacking directions before GO. Agree the freeze signal before the first round.

How we play

  1. You may kick, bounce, throw, or run a few steps.
  2. If tipped (soft two-finger), you must pass immediately.
  3. Throw-in = 1, strike-in = 2, kick-in = 3.
  4. Play 4–6 minute periods. Extra players are bumpers, walls, or the next wave — they stay in a job.
  5. Freeze on the whistle. Reset spots, then the next round.

Cues

  • A tip means you must pass at once.
  • Kick-in goal 3, hit 2, throw 1.
  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • Head up. Pass or move — do not crowd the ball.
  • Find a passing lane, not a pile-up.
  • Soft send; the ball stays below the chest unless the sport needs a high set.

Variations

  • Must complete one pass before a shot or a score.
  • Add a second foam ball if more than 24 students so more players get a touch.
  • Extra players are walking walls — they may deflect a ball with feet but may not score.

Teaching tips

  • Reset: freeze, walk back to start spots, change the key role, then GO. Two or three short rounds beat one long messy one in Hurley Burley.
  • Watch for one student holding the ball. Cue a pass or a 3-second pivot.
  • Year plan: October, January — match the month’s sport ball when you can.
  • Any legal send: kick, bounce, or throw. If the ball is only tipped, that player must pass immediately.
  • Score into a cone goal. Keep a simple 3–2–1 tally.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Hurley Burley for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Chooses kick, bounce, or throw.
  • Skill focus. A tip means pass at once.
  • Skill focus. Takes a role (attacker, defender, wall, sideline) and rotates without fuss.
  • Character Development. Honest score; tries again after a miss.
  • Safety. Heads up. No slide tackles or body checks. Equipment stays below the agreed height.
  • Healthy Relationships. Calls a teammate’s name; includes a player who has not touched the ball.
Grades 1–2. Hands and kick only. Walking.
Grades 3–4. Full game as written, foam or real sport ball as the unit says. One pass before a score if the class crowds.
Grades 5–6. Three-pass rule before a shot.

Safety. No slide tackles, no body checks. The ball is not thrown at a face. No elimination: a sit is a stretch, a jail, a hospital, or a role swap, then back in. Freeze on the whistle. Watch a demo or clip before the first play if you have one.
