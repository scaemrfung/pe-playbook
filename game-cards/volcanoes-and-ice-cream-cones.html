184. Volcanoes and Ice-Cream Cones

Locomotor & listen

When: September, December · Slot: 0–5 or 16–25 · Two teams · many scattered cones

Flip every cone to your shape before the whistle.

Also called: On/Off

Equipment: Many scattered cones

Set-up: Scatter cones mixed upright (ice-cream / volcano up) and upside down. Team Up vs Team Down. No guarding a cone with your body.

How we play

  1. On go, flip cones to your state. Hands only.
  2. Whistle. Hands on heads. Count each shape.
  3. Switch jobs and play again.

If this happens

  • Kicked cone: put it back as it was.
  • Held so the other team cannot touch it: let go.
  • Count dispute: teacher counts.

How a round ends. Most cones in your state after 45–90 seconds.

Cues

  • Flip, don’t kick.
  • No guarding a cone.
  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • Move the way I named (walk, skip, gallop) — not a race.
  • Stop in a balanced shape: two feet, knees soft.
  • Give neighbours a hoop of space.

Skins — same game, different wrapper

  • Progression: ball on top vs ball underneath.

Teaching tips

  • Also called: On/Off (year-round name).
  • House rules: Freeze on the whistle. A miss is a short task and you stay in. Extra players get a job (bumper, next wave, fetcher). No elimination.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Volcanoes and Ice-Cream Cones for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Finds a group or a spot without diving.
  • Skill focus. Finds an open pathway on the go signal; does not cut a neighbour in Volcanoes and Ice-Cream Cones.
  • Skill focus. Leftovers stay in — they join the teacher or the next round.
  • Character Development. Freezes honestly on the signal even if it is fun to keep running.
  • Safety. Heads up, soft landings, freeze on the whistle. No pushing to a line.
  • Healthy Relationships. Moves around people, not through them; helps a classmate who missed the cue.
Grades 1–2. Walk first. Two commands only (go and freeze). Teacher models every shape.
Grades 3–4. Add skip, gallop, and a shape at the freeze. Students may suggest one command.
Grades 5–6. A student DJ or caller for two minutes. Teacher keeps freeze and safety.

Safety. Give a hoop of space. Freeze means still feet — not a last-second shove. No elimination: a sit is a stretch, a jail, a hospital, or a role swap, then back in. Freeze on the whistle. Watch a demo or clip before the first play if you have one. A miss is a short task and you stay in. Extra players get a job (bumper, next wave, fetcher).
