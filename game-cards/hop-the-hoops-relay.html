138. Hop-the-hoops relay

Relays & stations

When: September, February, May · Slot: —

One-foot hop with a planned landing.

Equipment: 6 hoops per team.

Set-up: Hoops almost touching in a line.

How we play

  1. Hop on the right foot to the end. Hop on the left foot home.
Grades 1–2. Step if a hop is too much.
Grades 3–4. Hop both ways.
Grades 5–6. Two-foot jump out, hop back.

Safety. Quiet land. No pushing a hoop into the next jumper.
