154. Animals

Locomotor & listen

When: September, April · Slot: 0–5

Pick an animal from the habitat the teacher calls and move and sound like it. A K–2 warm-up.

Equipment: None

Set-up: Scattered in open space.

How we play

  1. Call a habitat theme: jungle, arctic, farm, ocean.
  2. Each student picks an animal from that habitat and moves and sounds like it for about a minute.
  3. Change the theme and repeat.
  4. Encourage different levels (high bird, low snake) and speeds.

More details · Grades K-2

Equipment: None

A simple warm-up. Choose a theme, such as the jungle. Each student picks an animal from that theme (a monkey or parrot) and moves like it, with sounds, for about a minute. Then change the theme, for example to the Arctic (a penguin or seal).

  1. Call a habitat theme (jungle, arctic, farm, ocean).
  2. Each student picks an animal from that habitat and moves + makes the sound for about one minute.
  3. Change the theme and repeat. Encourage different levels (high bird, low snake) and speeds.
  4. K–2 warm-up; no equipment.
