120. Hit and Run

Strike & field

When: June · Slot: 16–25 · Stations or a small diamond · 8 min.

Players hit or kick, then run to a hoop and back while fielders throw to the hoop, not at the runner. Use it to practise striking, running and fielding.

Equipment: Tee or kick, t-ball, one or two hoops.

Set-up: Hitting line. Fielders spread. Agree the freeze signal before the first round.

How we play

  1. Hit or kick. Run to a hoop and back.
  2. Fielders throw to the hoop, not at the runner.
  3. Everyone hits.
  4. Balls come back along the side. Restart on a teacher call, not a scramble through the middle.
  5. Freeze on the whistle. Reset spots, then the next round.

Cues

  • Contact, then look up.
  • Field to a hoop.
  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • Ready position in the field — feet still, hands out.
  • Hit, then run the path. Do not watch your own hit.
  • Fielders send to the base or hoop, not at a runner.

Variations

  • Opposite-side hits.
  • Must slide-stop at the hoop (no dive).
  • Tee or a bounced teacher-toss for grades 1–2.
  • Three-pitch maximum, then a run-out anyway so the line keeps moving.
  • Fielders rotate one spot after each batter.

Teaching tips

  • Reset: freeze, walk back to start spots, change the key role, then GO. Two or three short rounds beat one long messy one in Hit and Run.
  • Watch for a long batting line. Use a continuous format or a 3-pitch max.
  • Year plan: June — match the month’s sport ball when you can.
  • Hit or kick, run a short marked path.
  • Fielders throw to a hoop, never at the runner.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Hit and Run for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Everyone gets a kick or hit.
  • Skill focus. Ball goes to a base or hoop, not at the runner.
  • Skill focus. Takes batting order and a fielding spot; rotates so the same student is not always pitcher.
  • Character Development. Honest out or safe; no arguing a force.
  • Safety. Looks up after contact. Bat or kicker away from the waiting line.
  • Healthy Relationships. Calls ‘mine’ on a fly; helps a runner who is unsure of the next hoop.
Grades 1–2. Tee or a bounced toss. Run to one base or hoop and stay. Teacher fields the first few.
Grades 3–4. Live send (kick, strike, or throw). Three chances, then a run so the line moves.
Grades 5–6. Fielders call ‘mine.’ Rotate the strike job every three batters. Add a force-out at one base.

Safety. No body checks. The ball is not thrown at a face. No elimination: a sit is a stretch, a jail, a hospital, or a role swap, then back in. Freeze on the whistle. Watch a demo or clip before the first play if you have one.
