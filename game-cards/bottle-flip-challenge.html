5. Bottle Flip Challenge

Tag & chase

When: September, December · Slot: 16-25 · Daly Exercise · slot 16-25.

First upright cone-flip claims the centre ball and becomes the tagger. Daly Exercise game. Nobody sits the period.

Also called: Daly Exercise: Bottle Flip Challenge

Equipment: One cone per pupil; centre hoop; one soft ball

Set-up: Stand around a hoop. Soft ball inside. Each pupil holds a cone.

How we play

  1. On go, everyone flips their cone.
  2. First upright cone takes the ball and becomes it.
  3. Held-ball tag only. Tagged player does 5 jacks and stays in.
  4. Freeze on the whistle. Reset kit, then the next round.

If this happens

  • Kit knocks a person: pause, widen space, restart.
  • Argument over a point: teacher call, play on.
  • Someone sits out after a miss: they rejoin on the next turn with a short task instead.

How a round ends. Play 3–6 minutes, or first team/player to the posted target, then rotate jobs.

Cues

  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • One job at a time. Extra players fetch or count.
  • A miss is a short task, then back in.

Skins — same game, different wrapper

  • Flip onto a mat. Walking tag.
  • Jog tag. Two winners become two taggers.
  • Full reset each round.

Teaching tips

  • Source: Daly Exercise. Same house rules as the rest of the playbook.
  • Watch for crowding at one piece of kit. Add a second station before you add speed.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Bottle Flip Challenge for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Uses the taught action in Bottle Flip Challenge with control, not a rush that knocks kit or people.
  • Skill focus. Chooses space, timing, or a partner job in Bottle Flip Challenge instead of crowding.
  • Skill focus. Takes a role and shares kit in Bottle Flip Challenge.
  • Character Development. Honest about a miss or a tag in Bottle Flip Challenge.
  • Safety. Freeze on the whistle. Soft tags. No throws at a face.
  • Healthy Relationships. Helps a classmate re-enter after a miss or a tag.
Grades 1–2. Flip onto a mat. Walking tag.
Grades 3–4. Jog tag. Two winners become two taggers.
Grades 5–6. Full reset each round.

Safety. No elimination. Freeze on the whistle. Soft two-finger or noodle tags on the back or shoulder — never the head. A miss is a short task, then stay in.
