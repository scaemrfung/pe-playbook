45. End Ball

Invasion & team

When: October, January · Slot: 16–25 · Two teams of 6–10 · 2 × 4 min · sport ball, two end zones.

The carrier cannot run and must pass, and a clean catch by your end-zone catcher scores. Use it as a passing game in the same family as Captain Ball.

Equipment: Football or playground ball, pinnies, cones for two end zones.

Set-up: Each team names one catcher who stays in the far end. Pinnies on one team. Show the two attacking directions before GO. Agree the freeze signal before the first round.

How we play

  1. Carrier cannot run and must pass in 5 seconds.
  2. A clean catch in the end zone scores. Rotate the catcher.
  3. Defend by intercepting, not by grabbing the holder.
  4. Play 4–6 minute periods. Extra players are bumpers, walls, or the next wave — they stay in a job.
  5. Freeze on the whistle. Reset spots, then the next round.

Cues

  • Pivot, don’t run.
  • 5-second hold. Pass to space.
  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • Head up. Pass or move — do not crowd the ball.
  • Find a passing lane, not a pile-up.
  • Soft send; the ball stays below the chest unless the sport needs a high set.

Variations

  • Must complete 3 passes before a score (The Perfect Pass).
  • Walking only for Grade 1–2.
  • Must complete one pass before a shot or a score.
  • Add a second foam ball if more than 24 students so more players get a touch.
  • Extra players are walking walls — they may deflect a ball with feet but may not score.

Teaching tips

  • Reset: freeze, walk back to start spots, change the key role, then GO. Two or three short rounds beat one long messy one in End Ball.
  • Watch for one student holding the ball. Cue a pass or a 3-second pivot.
  • Year plan: October, January — match the month’s sport ball when you can.
  • End zones are 2–3 metres deep (mats or cones).
  • Score with a catch in the far end zone. The carrier cannot run — pivot only.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in End Ball for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Carrier pivots — no running with the ball.
  • Skill focus. Looks for a teammate before a score.
  • Skill focus. Takes a role (attacker, defender, wall, sideline) and rotates without fuss.
  • Character Development. Honest score; tries again after a miss.
  • Safety. Heads up. No slide tackles or body checks. Equipment stays below the agreed height.
  • Healthy Relationships. Calls a teammate’s name; includes a player who has not touched the ball.
Grades 1–2. Walking. Teacher may be catcher.
Grades 3–4. Three-pass rule before a score.
Grades 5–6. Student captains or officials. Offside-lite or a must-pass rule. Keep score without ranking classmates.

Safety. No slide tackles, no body checks. The ball is not thrown at a face. No elimination: a sit is a stretch, a jail, a hospital, or a role swap, then back in. Freeze on the whistle. Watch a demo or clip before the first play if you have one.
