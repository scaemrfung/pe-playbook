22. Sharks and Dolphins

Tag & chase

When: December, September · Slot: 16–25 · 2 sharks · noodles · marked sea

Sharks tag. Dolphins freeze, then get rescued.

Also called: Sharks and Dolphins (chute) · Shark and Fish

Equipment: 2 noodles · marked sea · optional parachute

Set-up: Sea boundary. Two sharks. Show the freeze (T or agreed shape) and the rescue.

How we play

  1. Sharks tag with noodles.
  2. Tagged dolphin freezes.
  3. A free dolphin rescues them (under the arms, high-five, or chute peek).
  4. Out of bounds = frozen.

If this happens

  • Freeze shape not clear: no rescue until it is.
  • Shark guards one frozen player all game: send them hunting.
  • Rough rescue: high-five only.

How a round ends. Sharks win if all are frozen at the whistle. Otherwise most free dolphins.

Cues

  • Cross on the call — do not wait on the line.
  • Safe once both feet are over the end line or in a hoop.
  • Freeze on the whistle — then eyes on the teacher.
  • Soft two-finger or noodle tag on the back or shoulder — never the head.
  • Change pathway. Do not run someone over to make a tag.
  • If you are tagged, show it honestly and take the next job.

Skins — same game, different wrapper

  • Parachute cat-and-mouse.
  • More sharks.
  • Shark and Fish: same freeze-and-rescue.

Teaching tips

  • Also called: Sharks and Dolphins (chute); Shark and Fish.
  • House rules: Freeze on the whistle. Tags are two-finger or noodle on the back or shoulder. A miss is a short task and you stay in. Extra players get a job (bumper, next wave, fetcher). No elimination.

Alberta PEW outcomes

Pick one or two look-fors per class.

  • Active Living. Stays in Sharks and Dolphins for the set time; can name breath or enjoyment after — not a ranking.
  • Movement Skill Development. Crosses on the call, not after a wait.
  • Skill focus. Uses space, a fake, or a safe zone instead of a pile-up in Sharks and Dolphins.
  • Skill focus. Takes a role and shares space in Sharks and Dolphins; does not crowd a hospital or jail.
  • Character Development. Honest about a tag or a catch — no argument that stalls Sharks and Dolphins.
  • Safety. Tagged player re-enters next wave — no last-one-standing.
  • Healthy Relationships. Helps a tagged classmate re-enter (high-five, stretch, hospital, or role swap).
Grades 1–2. Walk-tag. Teacher plus one student tagger. 30–45 second bursts. Re-enter with a high-five or the game’s hospital rule.
Grades 3–4. Jog-tag. 3–5 taggers. 60–90 second rounds. Soft two-finger or noodle tags. Rotate every round.
Grades 5–6. Add a rescue: a free dolphin high-fives a newly tagged shark to bring them back once.

Safety. Feet only in hoops — no diving. One or two per hoop for older grades. No elimination: a sit is a stretch, a jail, a hospital, or a role swap, then back in. Freeze on the whistle.
