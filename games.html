Big-Group Games

Numbered and sorted by type. Includes the Daly Exercise games that were not already in the playbook (Bottle Flip, Hoop Hut, and the rest). Same-game titles live on one card. Written for a 30-minute class. No elimination. Soft tags. Sport games use the real ball.

Some games adapted from physedgames.com

Large-group games · 67 games, all on the cards below
  • All 67 large-group games are on the cards below. Open “More details” on a card for the original overview, steps, source, and video.
  • Large group in this catalogue often means doubled classes (about 40–60) if you have the space. Shrink boundaries and tagger counts for a single class.
  • Safety defaults for throwing and kicking games: foam or soft balls, tags below the shoulders, no head shots, two-finger or noodle tags.
  • Dodgeball is optional programming. Prefer Detective, Net, Bench, or Prison Ball when you want more students moving; full house rules are on the Dodgeball page.

1. Alien Tag

Tag & chase

Tagged aliens drop the waist hoop and do 5 jumping jacks to rejoin.

Equipment: Noodles; hoop per runner

2. Angry Neighbour

Tag & chase

Neighbours with pool noodles chase players off the yard, and tagged players go to a house mat, then re-enter.

Equipment: pool noodles, exercise mats

3. Backyard Dog Tag

Tag & chase

Each dog guards a small yard while visitors try to sneak in, and steal the bone if you use one, without being tagged.

Equipment: Cones, 4 foam balls

4. Blob Tag

Tag & chase

Tagged players join the blob.

Equipment: Optional noodles or cone hats · spare kit at the side

5. Bottle Flip Challenge

Tag & chase

First upright cone-flip claims the centre ball and becomes the tagger.

Equipment: One cone per pupil; centre hoop; one soft ball

6. Chicken Checkers

Tag & chase

On ‘switch,’ leave your base and claim a new one.

Equipment: Hoops or cones as bases

7. Dead Ant Tag

Tag & chase

Tagged players lie on their back like a dead ant.

Equipment: None · optional pool noodles for taggers

8. Duck Duck Dash

Tag & chase

Run this standing-circle chase like Duck, Duck, Goose, where both players stay in after the chase.

Equipment: None.

9. Everybody’s It

Tag & chase

Anyone may tag.

Equipment: Optional one soft ball or spot markers · optional clothespins

10. Freedom Catch

Tag & chase

Taggers freeze runners, and a teammate frees a frozen player by completing a throw and catch to them.

Equipment: Cones, foam balls

11. Frog Tag

Tag & chase

Tagged players crouch; a leapfrog frees them.

Equipment: Pool noodles

12. Frozen Tag

Tag & chase

Tag freezes you in a shape.

Equipment: Optional noodles · optional hoops for Hospital

13. Icebergs

Tag & chase

Hoop islands that shrink.

Equipment: 8–12 hoops.

14. Kangaroos and Crocodiles

Tag & chase

Hear your name.

Equipment: None · two home lines and a centre line

15. Line Tag

Tag & chase

Travel only on painted lines.

Equipment: Painted gym lines · optional noodles

16. Massive 10 Second Tag

Tag & chase

Everyone is a tagger during short bursts of about 10 seconds, and tagged players take a knee until you bring them back in.

Equipment: none

17. Mystery Number Tag

Tag & chase

Hand out folded notes so a few students secretly become taggers when you say GO, then reshuffle the notes every 1–2 minutes.

Equipment: sticky notes, or pieces of paper

18. Octopus

Tag & chase

Runners cross the gym past the octopus, and tagged players plant their feet and become seaweed taggers.

Equipment: Two end lines.

19. Package Tag

Tag & chase

Tagged player sits as a package (hands in a bow).

Equipment: Pool noodles

20. Pony Roundup

Tag & chase

Herders tag galloping ponies with a noodle, and free ponies join hands to rescue a captured pony from a stable.

Equipment: Hoops, pool noodles

21. Shadow Tag

Tag & chase

Players tag by stepping on a shadow or a floor spot, never a body.

Equipment: Poly spots if you have no sun.

22. Sharks and Dolphins

Tag & chase

Sharks tag.

Equipment: 2 noodles · marked sea · optional parachute

23. Slap Rover

Tag & chase

A visitor walks over, slaps one hand on the other team’s line, and races home before the slapped player can tag them.

Equipment: Cones for two team lines.

24. Squirrel’s Tail

Tag & chase

Four squirrel teams steal nuts from the middle or pull tails, and a player without a tail pays two nuts to buy it back.

Equipment: Fabric tails, hoops, beanbags (nuts), 4-colour pinnies.

25. Survivor Tag

Tag & chase

Tagged players do a named skill, such as 5 jumps or a stretch, then re-enter.

Equipment: 2–3 pinnies.

26. Tail Tag

Tag & chase

Players pull a bib tail with two fingers, then hand it back so the game keeps going.

Equipment: Bibs or flags.

27. Ten-Second Tag

Tag & chase

Send a new small group of taggers in every 10 seconds, and tagged runners do 5 jumping jacks, then keep running.

Equipment: Centre line of cones, whistle.

28. Tidy Up

Tag & chase

Chase a moving bucket and return balls one at a time.

Equipment: Scattered tennis balls; one bucket

29. Tripod Tag

Tag & chase

In groups of four, three players hold hands and turn to protect a named teammate from the outside tagger.

Equipment: None.

30. Tunnel Freeze

Tag & chase

Tagged players freeze in a wide stance until a classmate frees them.

Equipment: None.

31. Wake-Up Bear

Tag & chase

The class chants until the bear wakes on a secret count, then the bear chases runners toward the walls.

Equipment: A circle of cones. No blindfold.

32. Waspital

Tag & chase

Everyone-is-it tag with a stretch hospital.

Equipment: 1–2 hoops.

33. Wolf’s Den

Tag & chase

Runners cross a middle zone past the wolves, and tagged players help tag for one crossing, then rejoin.

Equipment: Two end lines and a 3-m middle strip.

34. 3-Ball Soccer

Invasion & team

Play soccer with three balls at once so more students get touches.

Equipment: 3 soccer balls

35. 4-Goal Soccer

Invasion & team

Four teams each defend one net and may score in any other net.

Equipment: Soccer balls, 4 goals

36. 5 Soccer Circle Ideas

Invasion & team

Use these five soccer circle activities, from simple to more advanced, to practise passing and dribbling.

Equipment: Soccerballs, cones

37. Bench Ball

Invasion & team

Teams throw a foam ball to teammates standing on their bench, and each catch lets another teammate climb on.

Equipment: 2 benches, dodgeballs

38. Booger Ball

Invasion & team

A tagged ball-carrier must pass and cannot shoot on that possession.

Equipment: 1 basketball or playground ball, pinnies, a net and/or hoop.

39. Captain Ball

Invasion & team

Teams pass the ball to their captain, who stays in a hoop.

Equipment: Soccer or playground ball, pinnies, 2 hoops.

40. Capture the Egg

Invasion & team

Carry a beanbag through traffic.

Equipment: Beanbags, pinnies.

41. Capture The Flag Outdoors

Invasion & team

The standard Capture the Flag on a big outdoor field: hide your flag, steal theirs, bring it home.

Equipment: 2 flags, cones, a large outdoor area

42. Collect the Treasure

Invasion & team

Teams carry treasure home one piece at a time, and a tagged player walks the piece back to the middle.

Equipment: Beanbags, 4 hoops.

43. Cornerball

Invasion & team

The carrier must pass, and a catch with a foot in a corner hoop scores.

Equipment: 4 hoops, playground ball, pinnies.

44. Crab Football

Invasion & team

Players crab-walk and push or carry the ball to the line in short rounds.

Equipment: Playground ball, two end lines.

45. End Ball

Invasion & team

The carrier cannot run and must pass, and a clean catch by your end-zone catcher scores.

Equipment: Football or playground ball, pinnies, cones for two end zones.

46. End Wall Soccer

Invasion & team

Each team’s goal is the whole end wall, so the game works with a large group.

Equipment: indoor soccer ball or Softex foam ball, pinnies

47. End Zone Ball

Invasion & team

Players roll, not throw, the ball to teammates in the end zone while the other team tries to intercept.

Equipment: 4–8 footballs or soccer balls, pinnies, optional mats as obstacles.

48. Four Corner Flags

Invasion & team

Four teams steal flags one at a time from other hoops and are safe in their own quadrant.

Equipment: 16 beanbags (4 colours), 4 hoops, 4 pinnies colours, cones.

49. Fruit Salad

Invasion & team

On a called fruit, those players switch corners while the middle player tries to claim an empty one.

Equipment: Four corner spots.

50. Go for Goal

Invasion & team

Small teams of 2–3 pass once before shooting at cone goals, and waiting players act as walls or the next wave.

Equipment: Soccer balls, cone goals, pinnies.

51. Guard the Gates

Invasion & team

Attackers dribble or pass through cone gates while defenders try to win the ball with their feet only.

Equipment: Cone gates, soccer balls, pinnies.

52. Heist the Treasure

Invasion & team

Players steal treasure from a middle pile one piece at a time and walk it back if tagged.

Equipment: Beanbags, 4 hoops, optional 2 taggers.

53. Home Alone

Invasion & team

Each homeowner guards a cone key in a hoop while thieves try to steal it without being tagged.

Equipment: Hoops, cones

54. Hurley Burley

Invasion & team

Players may kick, bounce, throw or run a few steps, but a soft tip means they must pass right away.

Equipment: Soccer or playground ball, two goals, pinnies.

55. Numbers Hockey

Invasion & team

Call two or three numbers, and those players play to one goal while the rest wait at the side.

Equipment: Floor-hockey sticks or hands, hockey ball, two cone goals.

56. Pin Galore Soccer

Invasion & team

Soccer where every pin is a goal.

Equipment: Plastic pins, indoor soccer balls or Softex foam balls

57. Protect the House

Invasion & team

Knock the noodle off the house cone; defenders intercept.

Equipment: Cone circle, centre cone, balanced noodle, softballs

58. Reverse Soccer

Invasion & team

Field players may only tap or pass with their hands, and keepers save with their feet.

Equipment: Soccer ball, pinnies, two cone goals.

59. Rob the Nest

Invasion & team

Get objects into your nest.

Equipment: Centre pile of objects · team hoops as nests

60. Rob the Nest (dribble)

Invasion & team

Dribble collect.

Equipment: 4 hoops, soccer balls.

61. Sideline Soccer

Invasion & team

Each team lines its sideline as a wall of goalies while five players from each side play on the floor, then rotate every 2 minutes.

Equipment: Foam Balls

62. Slappers!

Invasion & team

Four teams strike bouncy balls with an open hand into any other team’s corner net.

Equipment: 4 nets, 4 bouncy balls (ex racquetball ball)

63. Star Wars

Invasion & team

The beach ball is the only target.

Equipment: Beach ball, many soft balls, a bench at each end

64. Steal the Bacon

Invasion & team

Your number is called.

Equipment: One object in the middle · two numbered lines

65. Stones

Invasion & team

Two teams cross to steal stones, and a teammate walks a tagged player home.

Equipment: Beanbags (stones), hoops, pinnies.

66. Switch

Invasion & team

On “Switch,” corner players change spots while the middle player tries to claim an empty corner.

Equipment: 4 cones, hoops, or mats as corners. Optional centre pylon.

67. Team Tag

Invasion & team

The tag team passes a ball to get close and tags with the ball in hand, and tagged players join the tag team.

Equipment: 1 playground ball or pinnie, bag of extra pinnies.

68. The Perfect Pass

Invasion & team

Teams must complete 3 (or 5) passes before they can score, and an interception resets the count.

Equipment: Soccer or playground ball, pinnies, two end lines or hoops.

69. Through the Gates

Invasion & team

Partners send a ball through a cone gate, then trap it and send it back.

Equipment: Cone pairs as gates, soccer balls.

70. Traffic Lights (kick)

Invasion & team

Students dribble on green, trap on yellow and stop with a foot on the ball on red.

Equipment: Soccer balls, one per student if you can.

71. Wall Soccer

Invasion & team

Small teams play while everyone else stands along their end wall as the goal.

Equipment: Soccer ball, pinnies, cones.

72. Wild Ball Soccer

Invasion & team

Play soccer with several soft objects at once, such as a foam football, a large foam die and a beach ball.

Equipment: Various soft or foam balls

73. Backboard Dodgeball

Target & send

Dodgeball on the basketball court.

Equipment: Basketball backboards, foam dodgeballs

74. Battleball

Target & send

Official-style team dodgeball with refs, where sportsmanship is rule number one.

Equipment: 4 foam balls, cones

75. Battleships

Target & send

Four teams throw playground balls to knock down other teams’ pins while protecting their own.

Equipment: 4 hoops, 4 pins, playground balls, cones for zones.

76. Bench Dodgeball

Target & send

Get your own teammates off the bench: a legal hit lets one of them back in.

Equipment: Two benches, foam dodgeballs

77. Bottle Flip

Target & send

Flip the bottle so it stands in box 1, then 2, then 3.

Equipment: Part-filled bottles; floor markers or saucer cones for six boxes

78. Catch Clap

Target & send

Clap once, then catch.

Equipment: One ball

79. Catch the Cone

Target & send

Flip a small cone so it lands inside a larger one.

Equipment: Two cones per player

80. Catch the Ice Cream

Target & send

Throw a bean bag into a partner's cone without a hand trap.

Equipment: Cone and bean bag per pair

81. Chain Train

Target & send

Hit your first hoop, then steal another group's hoop.

Equipment: 4 hoops and 1 bean bag per group of 4

82. Circuit Lights

Target & send

Slide the bag into cones to turn the lights on.

Equipment: Bean bag, spot, 6 cones

83. Cone Raider

Target & send

Hit a partner's cone and it becomes yours.

Equipment: 4-5 cones each; one tennis ball per pair

84. Detective Dodgeball

Target & send

If you are hit, remember who hit you.

Equipment: Foam dodgeballs

85. FLY BACK

Target & send

A runner takes a disc from the safe zone and throws it to teammates, who keep it only if they catch it.

Equipment: Discs or balls, cones, pinnies.

86. Flying Saucers

Target & send

Students send a disc or a long underhand throw at hoops placed at two or three distances.

Equipment: Discs or rings, hoops, cones.

87. Frisbee Flingers

Target & send

Teams complete disc throws and catches, and a catch in a hoop scores.

Equipment: Discs, hoops.

88. Hand Tennis

Target & send

Win the rally with hand hits, one bounce per side.

Equipment: Soft ball; a line or bench as net

89. Hockey pin knockdown

Target & send

One controlled shot after a dribble.

Equipment: Tunnel (two lines of cones, or a hoop in a hula hoop stand), pin, stick, hockey ball.

90. Hoop Target Bowling

Target & send

Land an overarm throw in a hoop, seated or standing.

Equipment: Floor hoops, soft balls

91. Human Catapult

Target & send

The sit-up is the throw.

Equipment: Mat, soft ball, cone behind each partner

92. Knock the Pin

Target & send

Run the triangle, then throw to knock the centre pin.

Equipment: Cones in a triangle; centre cone in a hoop; softballs

93. Low-ball

Target & send

Students send dodgeballs at feet or shins only, and a hit player does a 10-second stretch, then returns.

Equipment: 4–6 dodgeballs, centre line.

94. Net Dodgeball

Target & send

Dodgeball with a net and a goalie at each end.

Equipment: Foam dodgeballs, two nets or cone goals

95. Overarm throw-and-catch

Target & send

Students throw overarm to a catcher in a hoop, then swap roles.

Equipment: Playground ball, hoop.

96. Poison Ball

Target & send

Throwers outside the circle aim below the waist, and a hit dodger switches places with the thrower.

Equipment: Dodgeballs, a cone circle.

97. Popcorn Flip

Target & send

Bounce a tennis ball onto an upside-down cone so it flips upright.

Equipment: One saucer cone and one tennis ball each

98. Powerball

Target & send

Throw dodgeballs at beach balls to push them over the other team's goal line.

Equipment: 3 beach balls (or 10" playground balls), foam dodgeballs

99. Prison Ball

Target & send

A hit sends you to the prison behind the other team.

Equipment: Foam balls, cones for the prison lines

100. Protect the Castle

Target & send

Attackers knock the castle.

Equipment: Pins or cones · soft balls · 2–4 defender bibs

101. Punt to the line

Target & send

Students drop-kick a football toward a line 15–20 m away and keep kicking until it crosses.

Equipment: Football or playground ball, a line.

102. Rolling 21

Target & send

Bowling with a bust rule.

Equipment: Pairs: large hoop, small hoop, one ball

103. Skittle Ball

Target & send

Knock a pin, then either count it or collect it.

Equipment: Standing pins or cones · balls · a fetch line

104. Slide Sling Save

Target & send

Students slide beanbags at the other team’s feet, and a hit player returns by catching a ball thrown by a teammate.

Equipment: Beanbags, dodgeballs, two jail hoops.

105. Standard Dodgeball

Target & send

The classic two-team version, played here with house rules so nobody sits the period.

Equipment: Foam dodgeballs

106. Survivor Dodgeball

Target & send

A below-the-head hit means the player switches teams or does a 10-count stretch and stays in.

Equipment: Many dodgeballs.

107. Team Bowling

Target & send

Land in the target.

Equipment: Hoops or pins with values · balls or bags · a throw line

108. Three-Court Dodgeball

Target & send

Three mini dodgeball games at once.

Equipment: Foam balls, cones

109. Tidy the Bedroom

Target & send

Your half is tidy when the whistle goes.

Equipment: Equal light objects · centre line · optional hoops

110. Tunnel kick knockdown

Target & send

Students dribble through a tunnel, take one controlled kick at a pin, then dribble home.

Equipment: Cone tunnel, one pin or stacked cone, soccer ball.

111. Warzone Dodgeball

Target & send

Dodgeball with mats and benches as cover.

Equipment: Foam dodgeballs, mats and benches as walls

112. Aces

Strike & field

Players carry cards to the other team’s Card Master to win more cards, and players holding one card may tag.

Equipment: 3 decks of cards, 2 hoops, pinnies.

113. Barkball

Strike & field

Players hit or kick, then run to the far line and back while fielders tag with the ball held in two hands.

Equipment: Playground or t-ball, bat or kick, an end line.

114. Castleball

Strike & field

Stay on your half.

Equipment: Equal castles each side · soft balls
