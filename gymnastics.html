Gymnastics skills checklist

Download skills check list Download Grade 6 booklet

The skills check list is the locomotor / positions / rolls / balances tick sheet. The Grade 6 booklet is a routine planner and 40-point rubric. April stays floor-only — skip benches, beams, pyramids, and neck-loading (headstand, diving rolls, back handspring) unless you are trained and have mats.

Tick I (introduced), P (practising), or C (consistent). One student per printed page, or watch 6–8 students per class. You can also print this page.

  • Control and a still ending beat speed.
  • Yellow rows need a taught progression and a mat. Skip them if you have not taught the skill.
  • No neck-loading. Rocks stay on the shoulder-blades. Headstand and a diving forward roll are Grade 5–6 only, and only if you are trained.
  • Partner skills need consent. A student may say no.
