How to use this plan

  • Follow the month you are in. Combined headings on the original plan (Oct/Nov, Feb/March) are split so each month still has four teaching weeks.
  • December and June are written as three weeks to match a typical Alberta calendar. Extra days: repeat the last week’s favourite games.
  • Read the grade-band line that matches your class. Mix down or up for split grades.
  • Indoor default. Take throwing, running, T-ball, and orienteering outside when weather allows.
  • Skating is an optional swap in January and February — one day if ice time appears.
  • If the gym is booked, keep the warm-up and cool-down even on a shortened day.

House rules for every game

  • No true elimination. Sitting out is a stretch, a hospital, or a 10-count — then back in.
  • Soft tag. Thrown objects stay below the waist. Foam or gator-skin indoors.
  • Grades 1–2 walk more, use fewer taggers, and skip grabbing games (Squirrel’s Tail, Aces) until Grade 3+.
  • If a game needs a rubber chicken, a foam ball works.
  • Teacher-formed teams. Back-to-back partners. No public picking.

Objectives (from the yearly plan)

  • Appreciate and understand warm-up and stretching before activity.
  • Acquire skills through a variety of developmentally appropriate movement activities.
  • Understand, experience, and appreciate the health benefits of physical activity.
  • Interact positively with others.
  • Assume personal responsibility to lead an active way of life.
  • Name major muscle groups.

Whole-child / spiritual (use if it fits your school): notice how the body works together; talk about taking care of the body as a gift.

Evaluation

  • Monthly fitness updates — the same simple checks, recorded privately, never posted as a ranking.
  • Participation — entering games, trying the skill, helping tidy.
  • Cooperation — fair start, kind language, accepting a tag or a drop.
  • Skill development — watch the cues in each lesson, not just the score.

  • Report on the PEW learning outcome, not on who won the game.
  • Fitness numbers stay private. Never post a class ranking or read times aloud.
  • A short checklist once a week is enough. You do not need to score every 30-minute class.
  • Confirm the exact learning-outcome wording for your grade on LearnAlberta Physical Education and Wellness K–6 before report cards.

Safety

  • Soft tags, no sliding, foam or indoor balls only unless you are outside.
  • Offer a retriever, referee, or coach role when a student cannot do a movement that day.
  • Ropes, hoops, bats, and sticks live in a pile — never stepped on, never thrown at people.
