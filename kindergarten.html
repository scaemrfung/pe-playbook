Kindergarten PE

One 30-minute class a week · 38 lessons, September to June · Mr. Fung · Physical Education and Wellness K–6 (Kindergarten)

Focus: simple games every child can play. Mostly tag games and movement games. Short rules. Nobody is out: a tagged child does a quick task and rejoins.

A week belongs to the month it starts in, and is named by its Monday. If your Kindergarten class day is a day off that week, skip the lesson or carry it to the next week.

How every Kindergarten class runs

0–5 minWarm-up
5–12 minExplore the move
12–25 minGame
25–30 minCool-down and check-in
  • Entry: children walk in and sit on the centre line (or a poly spot). Take attendance there.
  • Stop signal: one drum beat or whistle, and you say “Freeze.” Children stop, hands on knees, eyes on you. Practise it in every warm-up.
  • Boundaries: set a cone line about 2 m in from every wall. Children stop at the cones, never at the wall. The tall walls have letters on them; use them for directions (“Walk to the B wall”).
  • Tag rules: two-finger tag on the shoulder or back. Walk-tag first, then jog. Change taggers every 45–90 seconds. A tagged child does 5 jumps or gets a friend’s high-five and keeps playing.
  • Show, then go: model every game with 2–3 children before the class plays. Give one rule at a time.
  • Safety: soft equipment only. Check the class medical alert list in the sub binder (including latex allergies before balloon lessons). Follow SPHEReS guidance and school policy. Water breaks as needed.
  • Support: walk instead of jog, a bigger target, a partner or a picture card for the rules. Challenge: a smaller target, the other hand or foot, or a new way to move. Follow ISP supports listed in the class binder.
  • Break plan: a child who needs a break sits on the quiet spot by the door and rejoins when ready.
  • Short on time: cut the “Explore the move” part to 3 minutes. Time left: replay the warm-up game with a new way to move.
  • Sub notes: the class list, the medical alert list and the equipment room key are in the sub binder. Leave a short note on what you covered. Plan B (no gym): play Simon Says Stay-In and Silly Bananas in the classroom, walking only.

Lesson 1 · Week of Mon Sept 7 — Freeze, go and my own space

Sept 8–11 · Sept 7 off (Labour Day) · School week 2
Outcomes
Movement Skill Development: Children explore spatial awareness in a variety of physical activity contexts.
Safety: Children recognize boundaries in various situations.
PEW K–6 · Kindergarten · LearnAlberta (word for word)
Equipment
20–25 poly spots, a drum or whistle
0–5
Warm-up. Each child walks to a poly spot. Teach two signals: “Go” means walk. One drum beat means “Freeze like a statue.” Practise 5 times.
5–12
Explore the move. Space bubble: arms out, turn slowly, touch nobody. Walk, tiptoe and march between the spots. On the drum, freeze in your own bubble. Praise children who find empty space.
12–25
Game. Jumping Beans with two beans only: walking bean and frozen bean. Add jumping bean when most children freeze within 3 seconds.
25–30
Cool-down and check-in. Sit on a spot. Three slow breaths. Ask: “Show me your space bubble. Why do we need one?”
Cues
Eyes up · Find empty space · Freeze like a statue
Safety
Set a cone line about 2 m in from every wall. Walls are never a finish line.
Check
K01. Freeze and find my own space (observation)

PowerTeacher Pro suggestions

  1. Open PowerTeacher Pro. Click Create, then Assignment.
  2. Click Select by Classes and pick the Kindergarten PE class. You can pick several and use Per Class for different due dates.
  3. Type the assignment name from the table. Pick the Category (check in PowerTeacher Pro; for example Observation if offered) and the Score Type (Collected Only, or the EIPS level grade scale if your school set one up; check in PowerTeacher Pro). Set the due date to your class day that week.
  4. Leave Count in Traditional Final Grade off unless your school says otherwise. K–6 is reported on standards, not a percent.
  5. Open the Standards tab. Click Show All. Tick the Kindergarten PE standard (“Children analyze and apply skills related to physical education.”; the name may differ in your list) and the learning outcome if your list has it. Tick Count in Grade.
  6. Description (optional, families see it): paste the “Meeting” look-for from the checklist. Click Save.
  7. To mark: Grading › Scoresheet. In the standards column, enter Not Yet Meeting, Meeting or Mastering Expectations, or INS, ISP or N/A. Click Save.
