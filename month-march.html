March

Month focus: Volleyball all four weeks · Newcomb to bump/set · send/receive games

Alberta PEW focus: Movement Skill Development, Active Living, Safety, Healthy Relationships

Equipment: Volleyballs, trainers or beach balls, a tape net or rope, cones

Fitness update: Timed keep-up or skip. Private record.

Catch is allowed in 1–2. Bump/set progress in 3–6. Send/receive games (Newcomb, keep-it-up) keep everyone in.

Show differentiation for

All grades G1 G2 G3 G4 G5 G6

Showing all grade bands (1–2 / 3–4 / 5–6).

Outcomes and assessment

Alberta PEW organizing ideas: Healthy Relationships · Movement Skill Development · Active Living · Safety

Parachute work (timing as a group) and circus skills (toss-catch, balance, showmanship). No aerials.

Grades 1–2

  • Lift the chute on a count with the class.
  • Toss and catch a scarf.
  • Walk a line with a beanbag on the head or hold a flamingo.
  • Watch an act and clap at the end.

Grades 3–4

  • Perform dome, popcorn, and a change-places under the chute.
  • Link toss-catch and a still pose into a 20–30 second act.
  • Name shoulders or core as working muscles.
  • Name one safety rule that kept the chute round.

Grades 5–6

  • Order a class chute sequence and count the lift.
  • Build a 30–45 second act with a start pose and still ending.
  • MC or peer-coach a station.
  • Explain why rest between lifts matters.
