Outcomes and assessment

Alberta PEW organizing ideas: Movement Skill Development · Active Living · Safety

Rope and hoop skills. The monthly fitness signature is a private 30-second skip.

Grades 1–2

  • Swing a rope as a rainbow and jump a floor rope or river.
  • Enter a long rope with teacher turners, or run through.
  • Roll or spin a hoop with control.
  • Keep rope and hoop safety (down before talk; never at people).

Grades 3–4

  • Complete a short string of two-foot self-turned jumps.
  • Enter, jump, and exit a long rope.
  • Travel while rolling a hoop; toss a hoop to a cone.
  • Connect rope or hoop work to heart-rate talk.

Grades 5–6

  • Set a private jump or spin record and try a new variation (jog-step, criss-cross, weak-hand spin).
  • Turn a rope for a partner.
  • Host a station or interval.
  • Compare only to their own January skip, not to classmates.
