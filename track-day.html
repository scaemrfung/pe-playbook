Track and Field Day

Grade 4–6 Track and Field Day. First week of June. May on the year plan is school practice. This page is the event sheet — descriptions, rules, and scoring. Combined-group events put two countries at the station at the same time. Beanbags replace water balloons. High-jump mat and hurdles are available. Discus and shot put are Grade 6, outside only.

Original event booklet May prep · June on the year plan

How this day works

  • Held the first week of June. May is school practice on the year plan. This page is the station sheet for the meet.
  • Students compete in countries. Combined-group events put two countries at the station at once.
  • Grade 4–5 throw the hoop (ring fling) and the ball. Grade 6 throws discus and shot put — outside only.
  • Beanbags replace water balloons. High-jump mat and hurdles are on site. Sack race is the only event where runners may come off.

How to score

Unless an event has its own table, countries score 4 / 3 / 2 / 1 for 1st–4th. Individual marks use the band tables below (best of the allowed tries). Ties split the points. Do not post a class ranking from PE — these points are for Track Day countries only.

  • A fault uses up that try. Remaining tries still stand.
  • Add country points across events. Highest total wins the day.
  • Ties on an event split the points (e.g. 4+3 → 3.5 each).

Event 1 · Grades 4–5 (Grade 6 uses discus)

Ring fling (hula hoop)

A side-arm hoop throw using the same action as discus. Best of three throws against distance cones.

Equipment. Hula hoops, throw line, cones at 1 m, 8 m, 16 m, 20 m

Set-up. Throwing sector with a clearly marked line. Students wait to the side, not in front of the thrower.

Tries. 3 throws

Rules

  1. Each student gets three throws. Best throw counts.
  2. Throw with a side-arm from the same side as the throwing arm (discus action, not a frisbee flick across the body).
  3. Feet stay behind the line until the hoop has left the hand.
  4. Measure to the first cone the hoop reaches on the fly or first bounce, as the station judge decides before the rotation starts — use the same rule for everyone.

Faults

  • Step-over before release = that throw does not count.
  • Wrong-side or overhand throw = no throw. Reset and use the remaining tries.
  • Fetching before the judge calls ‘clear’ = that throw is void.

How to score

Best throw vs cones. If two students share a band, the farther cone wins the place; then apply 4–3–2–1 to the country.

Mark Points
Past 20 m4
Past 16 m3
Past 8 m2
Past 1 m1
Short of 1 m / fault0

Safety. One thrower. Sector empty. Fetch on the judge’s call only.

Event 1 · Outside · Grade 6 only

Discus

School discus, side-arm, same side as the throwing arm. Practise and compete outside only.

Equipment. School discus or marked practice disc, throw line, cones at 1 m, 4 m, 6 m, 10 m

Set-up. Outside. Students start two steps behind the throw line. Wide sector. No indoor discus.

Tries. 3 throws

Rules

  1. Each student gets three throws. Best throw counts.
  2. Stand two steps behind the line. Do not cross it before release.
  3. Side-arm throw from the same side as the throwing arm.
  4. Walk the discus back; never throw it to the line.

Faults

  • Crossing the line before release = no throw.
  • Overhand or baseball throw = no throw (safety stop; remaining tries only after a re-teach).
  • Throwing back toward the waiting line = disqualified from the remaining tries.

How to score

Best throw vs cones.

Mark Points
Past 10 m4
Past 6 m3
Past 4 m2
Past 1 m1
Short of 1 m / fault0

Safety. Outside only. One thrower. Sector empty. Implement carried vertically.

Event 2 · Outside · Grades 4–6

Javelin throw

Overhand throw with a run-up allowed. School javelin or turbo-javelin / foam trainer.

Equipment. Javelin or turbo-javelin, throw line, cones at 1 m, 4 m, 7 m, 9 m, run-up lane

Set-up. Run-up lane into a throw line. Pointed end down when walking. One thrower in the sector.

Tries. 3 throws

Rules

  1. Students may run up to the line.
  2. Overhand only.
  3. Three tries. Best throw counts.
  4. Release before the line. Point stays down on the walk-back.

Faults

  • Step-over before release = no throw.
  • Side-arm or underhand = no throw.
  • Throwing the implement back to the line = remaining tries cancelled.

How to score

Best throw vs cones.

Mark Points
Past 9 m4
Past 7 m3
Past 4 m2
Past 1 m1
Short of 1 m / fault0

Safety. One thrower. Fetch on the call. Carry vertically, tip down.

Event 3 · Grades 4–6

Bounce-pass relay

Each country splits into two files. A basketball is bounce-passed across the gap. Loud count. Two timed trials.

Equipment. One basketball per country, two parallel lines

Set-up. Two files facing each other. The ball must bounce between the two lines. Team members stay behind their line except to fetch.

Tries. 2 timed trials

Rules

  1. On go, bounce-pass back and forth. After you pass, run to the end of your file.
  2. The person who bounced the ball retrieves it if the catcher misses — not the catcher.
  3. Stay behind the line except to fetch a runaway ball.
  4. Count completed passes out loud. Two trial runs. Best count counts.

Faults

  • Chest-pass with no bounce = that pass does not add to the count.
  • Catcher fetching a miss = that pass does not count; reset and continue.
  • Leaving the line early (other than a fetch) = restart that trial.

How to score

Compare best counts across countries. 1st–4th.

Mark Points
1st (highest count)4
2nd3
3rd2
4th1

Safety. No sliding for a loose ball. Files stay behind the line.

Event 4 · Combined group · Grades 4–6

Shuttle run

10 m beanbag shuttle from a face-down start. Combined-group: two countries at the station.

Equipment. Two lines 10 m apart, 3 beanbags per runner (2 far, 1 near)

Set-up. Contestants lie face down, nose above the start line, hands at their sides. Two bags behind the far line, one beside the starter — not in the hands. Combined-group corridors so two countries run at once.

Tries. 1–2 timed runs

Rules

  1. On go, jump up. Do not pick up the near beanbag on the first take-off.
  2. Run 10 m, pick up one far bag, carry it to the start, exchange with the near bag.
  3. Run back, exchange at the far end, return to the start with a bag.
  4. Reset for the next runner: two far, one near.

Faults

  • Grabbing a bag on the first rise = restart.
  • Throwing bags instead of exchanging = restart.
  • Starting before ‘go’ = false start; one restart, then a time stands.

How to score

Fastest legal time for the country (use the named runner, or a country average if every member runs). Then 1st–4th.

Mark Points
1st (fastest legal time)4
2nd3
3rd2
4th1

Safety. No diving on bags. Lanes clear. Combined groups stay in their own 10 m corridor.

Event 5 · Grades 4–6

Hurdles

Four-hurdle sprint. 5 m approach, 4 m between hurdles, 5 m to finish = 26 m. Two runs. Best time counts.

Equipment. 4 hurdles (facing so they tip forward), marks, stopwatch

Set-up. One runner at a time. Flight as marked. Next runner waits off the approach.

Tries. 2 runs

Rules

  1. Run all four hurdles. Two runs; keep the faster legal time.
  2. Aim to go over, not through — over is faster and kinder to shins.
  3. No time penalty for a knock-down. The time still counts.
  4. Start on the judge’s signal. Run through the finish.

Faults

  • Running around a hurdle = no run.
  • Starting before the signal = one restart, then the time stands.
  • Next runner on the flight before the landing is clear = wait; do not start.

How to score

Booklet time bands on the faster run.

Mark Points
Under 8 seconds4
8–9 seconds3
9–10 seconds2
More than 11 seconds1

Safety. One at a time. Hurdles tip forward. Clear the landing before the next runner.

Event 6 · Combined group · Grades 4–6

Hula hoop twirl

Waist twirl for time. Combined-group: one competitor from each country starts together. Three chances to beat 40 seconds.

Equipment. One hoop per competitor, stopwatch

Set-up. Space so hoops do not clash. A student from another country may time.

Tries. 3 chances

Rules

  1. On go, twirl the hoop on the waist.
  2. Time stops when the hoop hits the ground or the body other than the waist.
  3. Three chances. Best time counts.
  4. Stop on the whistle even if the hoop is still up.

Faults

  • Hands holding the hoop up after the start = time stops.
  • Starting before go = that try is void.

How to score

Booklet time marks on the best try.

Mark Points
40 seconds or more4
15 seconds3
5 seconds2
1 second (got it spinning)1
Did not start / drop at once0

Safety. Space between hoopers. Stop on the whistle.

Event 7 · Grades 4–6

Pin bowling

Bowl at three pins. Knees bent, ball released close to the ground. One point per pin, three rolls, maximum 9.

Equipment. 3 pins (or cones), one bowling or playground ball per team

Set-up. Each country bowls in turn. One teammate sets the three pins, then steps off the lane.

Tries. 3 rolls each

Rules

  1. Bend the knees and release the ball near the ground — a bowl, not a toss.
  2. Each person has 3 rolls. Pins are reset between rolls.
  3. One point for each pin knocked down, up to 9 for the person.
  4. Country score is the total of its members, or the named bowler if you are sampling one per country.

Faults

  • Tossing or bouncing the ball = that roll scores 0.
  • Rolling while the setter is still on the lane = roll does not count.

How to score

Add pin points (max 9 per person). Rank countries by total, then 4–3–2–1. If only one bowler per country, use that 0–9 as the mark.

Mark Points
7–9 pins4
5–6 pins3
3–4 pins2
1–2 pins1
0 pins0

Safety. Wait until pins are set and the setter is off the lane.

Event 8 · Combined group · Grades 4–6

100 metre dash

Lane sprint. Heats of up to 8. Combined-group: two countries may share a heat. 1st and 2nd from each heat to a final.

Equipment. Lanes or cones, start and finish lines

Set-up. As many runners per heat as you have lanes (max 8). Rest the winners from the last heat before the final.

Tries. Heat + final for qualifiers

Rules

  1. Standing or crouch start. On your mark, set, go.
  2. Stay in the lane. Run through the finish — do not lunge sideways.
  3. Pick 1st and 2nd from each heat for the final.
  4. Everyone who does not final still has completed the event for participation; only the final is scored for country points.

Faults

  • False start = one restart for that heat, then the next false start is last place in that heat.
  • Leaving the lane and impeding = disqualified from the heat.

How to score

Final places 1st–4th among countries (use each country’s best finalist).

Mark Points
1st in the final4
2nd3
3rd2
4th1

Safety. Look forward. No blocking. Cool-down walk after the final.

Event 9 · Grades 4–6

High jump

Jump for height onto the school high-jump mat. Bar starts at 50 cm. On Track Day a miss ends the event (time will not allow several attempts).

Equipment. High-jump mat, uprights, bar or elastic, measuring stick

Set-up. Mat flush to the bar. Waiting line sits off the run-up. One jumper. One practice jump only if time allows.

Tries. Day: one miss ends. Optional one practice before the counted jump.

Rules

  1. Set the bar at 50 cm and have everyone jump.
  2. Those who miss are finished on Track Day.
  3. Scissors is the default. Fosbury flop only if it has been taught and the landing is on the back.
  4. Land on the mat on the back or side — never the head or neck.

Faults

  • Bar knocked = miss (finished on the day).
  • Take-off from two feet if you have called scissors-only = miss.
  • Landing on the head/neck = stop the student; no further jumps.

How to score

Booklet height bands on the last bar cleared.

Mark Points
Higher than 140 cm4
130 cm3
Cleared, but less than 110 cm2
Attempted 50 cm, miss1

Safety. One jumper. Mat flush. No diving onto the head. Students waiting sit off the run-up.

Event 10 · Grades 4–6

Dry-land canoeing

Sit on two scooter boards and paddle with plungers. 10 m around a pylon and back. The whole country is timed.

Equipment. Two scooter boards and two plungers per racer, 10 m out-and-back, pylon

Set-up. Loading/unloading zone behind the start. Only the student on the boards is in that zone. All four countries go at the same time if space allows.

Tries. Whole country once (restart a foot-fault)

Rules

  1. Sit on the boards. Push with plungers. Feet are not allowed.
  2. If a foot is used, that student starts over.
  3. Travel 10 m, around the pylon, back to the start; next teammate goes.
  4. Time the entire country. Clock stops when the last student is back in the zone.

Faults

  • Foot on the floor = that rider restarts.
  • Standing on scooters = restart.
  • A helper in the loading zone (other than the rider) = 5-second penalty, or restart if it happens at the changeover.

How to score

Booklet team-time bands.

Mark Points
Under 2 minutes4
2–2:303
2:30–3:002
More than 3 minutes1

Safety. No standing on scooters. Hands and plungers only. Helmets if your school requires them for scooters.

Event 11 · Combined group · Grades 4–6

Skipping

Self-turn skip for count. Combined-group: one skipper from each colour at a time. A student from another country counts. Three tries.

Equipment. One rope per skipper

Set-up. Rope space. Counter sits or stands where they can see the rope pass the feet.

Tries. 3 tries

Rules

  1. Count each time both feet (or the jumping foot) clear the rope.
  2. Three tries. Highest count counts.
  3. A miss ends that try. Start the next try on the judge’s nod.
  4. Marching in place does not add to the Track Day count (it is fine in PE class, not here).

Faults

  • Double-bouncing with the rope not passing = do not count those extra bounces.
  • Counter from the skipper’s own country = recount with a different colour.

How to score

Booklet count ranges on the best try.

Mark Points
100 or more4
51–993
21–502
6–201
0–50

Safety. Rope space. Stop on the whistle. No whipping a rope at others.

Event 12 · Combined group · Grades 4–6

Beanbag toss (balloon substitute)

Partner toss across a gap that steps back after each full rotation. Water balloons are not used — beanbags only. Last country with a clean catch at the longest gap wins.

Equipment. One beanbag per country, two files, cones that step back

Set-up. Each country splits into two facing files. After you throw, go to the back of your file. After everyone has tossed, move the pylons one step farther. Odd numbers: extra person alternates. This is not a race.

Tries. Until one country remains

Rules

  1. Soft underhand toss to your partner’s hands.
  2. If the bag hits the floor, that country is out of that distance round.
  3. Teams that still have a clean catch after a full rotation step back and continue.
  4. Keep moving farther until one country remains.

Faults

  • Overhand or a hard bullet toss = that toss is a drop.
  • A catch off the body/foot = drop.
  • Stepping over the cone to shorten the gap = drop.

How to score

Last country standing = 1st. Reverse order of elimination for 2nd–4th.

Mark Points
Last country with a clean catch4
2nd last out3
3rd last out2
First country out1

Safety. Beanbags only. No overhand. Eyes on the bag. Extra time to toss is allowed.

Event 13 · Grades 4–6

Standing long jump

Two-foot take-off from a board, no run-up. Three jumps. Stick the landing.

Equipment. Take-off board or tape, measuring tape, cones at 0.25 / 0.5 / 0.75 / 1.0 m

Set-up. Toes behind the board. Landing area clear. Measure from the nearest mark in the sand/mat back to the board.

Tries. 3 jumps

Rules

  1. This is standing long jump — no run-up.
  2. Each student gets three jumps. Best legal jump counts.
  3. If they step over the board, that jump is disqualified.
  4. Stick the landing; the nearest heel (or hand, if it touches down) is the mark.

Faults

  • Step-over / hitch-kick off the board = no jump.
  • Falling back so a hand or seat is closer to the board = measure from that nearer mark, which may drop a band.

How to score

Best legal jump vs booklet distances.

Mark Points
1.0 m or more4
0.75 m3
0.5 m2
0.25 m1
Short of 0.25 m / fault0

Safety. One jumper. Soft knees. Landing area clear.

Event 14 · Grades 4–6

Running long jump

Running jump, not standing. Three jumps. Board rule.

Equipment. Runway, board, pit or mat, cones at 1.5 · 2.5 · 3.5 · 4.5 m

Set-up. One jumper on the runway. Next waits until the pit is raked/clear.

Tries. 3 jumps

Rules

  1. Controlled run-up. Take off from the board.
  2. Three jumps. Best legal jump counts.
  3. If they step over the board, that jump is disqualified.
  4. Land and walk forward out of the pit.

Faults

  • Foul (step-over) = no jump.
  • Running through without a take-off = that try is used.
  • Next jumper on the runway too early = wait; do not jump.

How to score

Best legal jump vs booklet distances.

Mark Points
4.5 m or more4
3.5 m3
2.5 m2
1.5 m1
Short of 1.5 m / fault0

Safety. One on the runway. Pit clear before the next jumper.

Event 15 · Combined group · Grades 4–6

Tug-of-war

Each country pulls every other country. Equal numbers. First team to move the centre 3 m wins that pull.

Equipment. Tug rope with a centre mark, 3 m win marks each side

Set-up. Combined-group. Sides need an equal number of pullers. Outdoor shoes. Judge on the centre mark.

Tries. Each pairing once

Rules

  1. On go, pull. First country to take the centre 3 m wins that pairing.
  2. Each country should pull against all other countries.
  3. Hands only on the rope. Sit or fall: the judge may restart if it is unsafe.
  4. Stop on the whistle even mid-pull.

Faults

  • Wrapping the rope around a waist or wrist = forfeit that pull.
  • Pulling before go = restart, then a second false start loses the pull.

How to score

Most pairing wins = 1st country, then 4–3–2–1. Tie: one extra pull or split the points.

Mark Points
Most pairing wins (1st)4
2nd3
3rd2
4th1

Safety. No wrapping the rope on a person. Stop on the whistle. Check the ground for holes.

Event 16 · Grades 4–6

Soccer kick

Place-kick from the marker through pylon gates. Three tries. Booklet points 5 / 4 / 3 by gate.

Equipment. Soccer ball, kick line, two or more pylon gates

Set-up. Kick line. Gates in a line so the last set is the 5-point gate. Fetch from the side.

Tries. 3 kicks

Rules

  1. Place the ball. Three tries. Best kick counts.
  2. Kick from the pylon marker through the gates.
  3. A ball through the last set of pylons is 5 points; nearer gates 4 then 3 as marked on the field.
  4. No slide-tackle run-up into other kickers.

Faults

  • Kicking from in front of the line = 0 for that try.
  • Volleying at waiting students = remaining tries cancelled.

How to score

Best kick using the booklet 5–4–3, then convert to country 4–3–2–1 if you need a place, or sum raw points if every member kicks.

Mark Points
Through last pylons (5 pts)4 country pts if ranking
Middle gate (4 pts)3
Near gate (3 pts)2
Miss1 for attempting / 0 if no kick

Safety. Kick away from waiting lines. Fetch from the side.

Event 17 · Grades 4–5 (Grade 6 uses shot put)

Overhand throw with a run-up allowed. Three tries. Booklet uses separate girl/boy cones; a class may use one mixed set if you want practice without a gender split — on Track Day use the booklet marks.

Event 17 · Outside · Grade 6 only

Shot put

Push the shot from the shoulder. Never throw it like a baseball — that can injure the shoulder. Outside only.

Equipment. Age-appropriate school shot, throw line, cones

Set-up. Outside. Boys’ cones 0–1–3 m. Girls’ 0–1–2 m. Shot rests at the neck.

Tries. 3 puts

Rules

  1. Shot at the neck/shoulder. Push, do not throw.
  2. Three tries. Do not cross the line.
  3. Best put counts.
  4. If form looks like a baseball throw, the judge stops the put; remaining tries only after a reset.

Faults

  • Baseball throw = that put is void; re-teach before the next try.
  • Crossing the line = no put.

How to score

Best put vs booklet cones.

Mark Points
Boys past 3 m / girls past 2 m4
Boys / girls past 1 m2
Legal put, short of 1 m1
Fault / baseball throw0

Safety. Outside. One putter. Sector empty. Push from the shoulder only.

Event 18 · Grades 4–6

Sack races

Jump in a gunny sack to the finish. This is the only event where students may take off their runners. Must be in the sack when they cross the finish.

Equipment. Gunny sacks, start and finish lines

Set-up. Heats if numbers are large. Check the ground for holes. Sit to put shoes back on before the next station.

Tries. 1–2 races

Rules

  1. Step in. Hold the sack. Jump to the finish.
  2. Must be in the sack at the finish line — or redo.
  3. Heats, then a final if you want country places.

Faults

  • Finish with the sack off or around one ankle only = redo or last in that heat.
  • Leaving the lane and impeding = last in that heat.

How to score

Final places 1st–4th among countries (best heat/finalist).

Mark Points
1st4
2nd3
3rd2
4th1

Safety. Check the ground. No crashing the finish. Shoes back on before the next station.
