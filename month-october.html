October

Month focus: Football (Weeks 1–2) · parachute and circus (Weeks 3–4)

Alberta PEW focus: Movement Skill Development, Movement Tactics, Safety, Healthy Relationships

Equipment: Footballs, flag belts or pinnies, parachute, scarves, beanbags, plates or rings

Fitness update: Throw-and-catch pulse check. Private wall-pass later in the month.

Football uses the real ball (foam is fine for 1–2). Weeks 3–4 are parachute and circus so October is not all collision games.

Show differentiation for

All grades G1 G2 G3 G4 G5 G6

Showing all grade bands (1–2 / 3–4 / 5–6).

Outcomes and assessment

Alberta PEW organizing ideas: Movement Skill Development · Active Living · Safety

October is football: hike, catch, throw, flag or two-hand touch, end-zone. Real footballs. No tackling.

Grades 1–2

  • Demonstrate an underhand send and a two-hand or scoop receive.
  • Kick a still soccer or football to a target or partner.
  • Show ready position (eyes on the sender, body behind the ball).
  • Move safely when many balls are in the gym.

Grades 3–4

  • Modify a kick or throw to improve accuracy (plant foot, opposite-foot step).
  • Utilize a send from one activity in another (gate pass → small game).
  • Begin simple tactics: pass before a shot; move after you send.
  • Apply below-waist throws only in dodgeball, and use dodgeballs for those games.

Grades 5–6

  • Select a send (kick vs throw) that fits the task.
  • Implement a tactic in 3v1 or 3v3 (lead the receiver, intercept without contact).
  • Use the non-dominant foot or hand for a short round.
  • Explain why accuracy is safer than power indoors.
