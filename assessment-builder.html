Assessment builder

Make your own PE checklist in a few minutes. Pick a grade, choose the outcome for each check from the dropdown (or type your own), paste your students, then print it or download it for Excel or Google Sheets.

Privacy: everything happens in your browser. Student names are not saved or sent anywhere. They disappear when you close or refresh this page, or click Clear names. Keep downloaded files in your own school Drive.

Outcomes are the four Physical Education learning outcomes for each grade, word for word from LearnAlberta. Wellness outcomes (character, healthy eating, relationships, growth and development, financial wellness) are assessed in Health class. Want a ready-made file instead? See the sample checklists.

Look-fors and outcomes

    PowerTeacher Pro steps

    1. Open PowerTeacher Pro. Click Create, then Assignment.
    2. Click Select by Classes and pick your PE class.
    3. Type the assignment name from the table below. Pick the category and score type (check in PowerTeacher Pro). Set the due date to the class day you assess.
    4. Leave Count in Traditional Final Grade off, unless your school says otherwise. K–6 is reported on standards, not a percent.
    5. Open the Standards tab. Click Show All. Tick your grade's PE standard and tick Count in Grade.
    6. Optional: paste the look-for into the description. Click Save.
    7. To mark: Grading > Scoresheet. In the standards column, enter the full level name from your checklist. Click Save.

    Not confirmed: category names, score types and the exact standard names in your gradebook. Check in PowerTeacher Pro or ask your school PowerSchool lead.
