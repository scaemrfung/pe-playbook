December

Month focus: Tag, cooperative, invasion and festival games · three weeks if the calendar is short

Alberta PEW focus: Active Living, Personal and Social Development, Character, Safety, Healthy Relationships

Equipment: Pinnies, noodles, hoops, foam balls, benches, flags — as each game card lists

Fitness update: Private skip or shuttle on the last class. No ranking.

December is games, not a new sport. House rules: no elimination, soft tags. Use the Games library. Extra days: repeat a favourite.

Showing all grade bands (1–2 / 3–4 / 5–6).

Outcomes and assessment

Alberta PEW organizing ideas: Movement Skill Development · Active Living · Character Development

December is floor hockey: blade down, tiny touches, push-pass, low shot. No skating. Three weeks if Advent is short.

Grades 1–2

  • Start and stop on a drum or freeze cue.
  • Show high / medium / low shapes.
  • Travel a straight or curved pathway without colliding.

Grades 3–4

  • Match locomotor changes to an 8-count.
  • Remember a short A-B-A phrase (travel, shape, travel).
  • Work with a partner or small group to keep a beat.

Grades 5–6

  • Build and perform 16–32 counts with a formation change.
  • Lead a phrase or take the drum.
  • Persevere when a sequence is forgotten — reset and try again.
