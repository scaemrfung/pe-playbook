January

Month focus: Basketball (Weeks 1–2) · scoop send/receive (Weeks 3–4)

Alberta PEW focus: Movement Skill Development, Movement Tactics, Safety, Healthy Relationships

Equipment: Basketballs, hoops or buckets, plastic scoops, wiffle or foam balls, pinnies

Fitness update: Wall-pass or skip check. Private record — not a rank.

Basketball uses the real ball. Scoops are a second send/receive tool (cradle, toss, catch) — not a full lacrosse unit.

Show differentiation for

All grades G1 G2 G3 G4 G5 G6

Showing all grade bands (1–2 / 3–4 / 5–6).

Outcomes and assessment

Alberta PEW organizing ideas: Movement Skill Development · Safety · Healthy Relationships · Active Living

January continues floor hockey: tactics, 3v3, inclusion. No ice. One class may use a stay-in dodgeball game.

Grades 1–2

  • Solve a simple partner task (move an object together).
  • Follow a playground turn-taking rule (four-square serve, rope wait).
  • Bounce a basketball or push-pass a hockey ball to a partner.
  • Keep a hockey stick blade on the floor.

Grades 3–4

  • Demonstrate teamwork in a group of four.
  • Walk-dribble with eyes up.
  • Chest or bounce pass to a moving partner.
  • Push-pass through a gate; blade stays down.

Grades 5–6

  • Jog-dribble and switch hands.
  • Pass, then move to space in a small-sided game.
  • Receive and send a hockey ball in two touches.
  • Show perseverance on a personal-best station (not vs peers).
