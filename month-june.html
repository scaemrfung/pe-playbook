June

Month focus: Week 1: Track and Field Day · Weeks 2–4: T-ball / baseball

Alberta PEW focus: Movement Skill Development, Active Living, Character, Safety

Equipment: Week 1: kit on the Track Day page. Weeks 2–4: tees, bats, balls, hoop bases.

Fitness update: Final private check after the meet: skip, short run, or jump-stick.

Track and Field Day is the first week of June (event page for rules and scoring). Weeks 2–4 are T-ball/baseball — everyone bats, throw to a hoop at first, not at the runner.

Show differentiation for

All grades G1 G2 G3 G4 G5 G6

Showing all grade bands (1–2 / 3–4 / 5–6).

Outcomes and assessment

Alberta PEW organizing ideas: Movement Skill Development · Active Living · Character Development · Healthy Relationships

June is track and field: meet rehearsal, relays, field events, then a year-end festival. Optional T-ball closer.

Grades 1–2

  • Rotate two events calmly and cheer a classmate.
  • Catch a beanbag, run through first, hit off a tee.
  • Name one favourite game and one way to stay active in summer.

Grades 3–4

  • Complete three meet events with fair starts.
  • Field a grounder and throw to a hoop at first.
  • Play modified T-ball so everyone bats.
  • Write or say one personal goal from the year.

Grades 5–6

  • Official or lead a younger heat if asked.
  • Decide where to throw and when to run.
  • Host a festival station.
  • Compare private September and June checks only with themselves.
