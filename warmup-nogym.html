Warm Up Games - No Gym, No Problem

Quick warm-ups when the gym isn’t available, you’re stuck in a classroom or hallway, or you need a fast start before the main lesson. Games are tagged by Classroom, Gym, Outside, and Hallway — use the filters to match your space.

Soft tags where possible. Prefer stay-in / no-elimination endings (freezes, role swaps, fitness side tasks, time limits) so nobody sits the period out. Follow your school’s safety rules.

Classroom, gym, outside, and hallway warm-ups in one list.
