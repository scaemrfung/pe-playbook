May

Month focus: Track and Field Day practice for Grades 4–6 · play versions for 1–2

Alberta PEW focus: Movement Skill Development, Active Living, Safety, Character Development

Equipment: Hurdles, high-jump mat, hoops, beanbags (not balloons), pins, sacks, javelin trainers; Grade 6 discus and shot outside

Fitness update: Private timed run or skip late in the month. No class ranking.

May is school practice so June Track Day is familiar. Use the Track Day page for descriptions, rules, faults, and scoring. Beanbags replace water balloons. High-jump mat and hurdles are available.

Outcomes and assessment

Alberta PEW organizing ideas: Active Living · Movement Skill Development · Safety

May Weeks 1–2 are volleyball (Newcomb to bump/set). Weeks 3–4 are track and field. Slide Jump Rope for Heart if your school runs it.

Grades 1–2

  • Pace a walk-jog and stick a standing jump.
  • Send to a zone (underhand).
  • Keep a rope or marching rhythm for a short song.
  • Find a few picture controls with an adult or partner.

Grades 3–4

  • Use a standing or three-point start and stay in a lane.
  • Hand off a baton and run a small relay.
  • Complete a numbered orienteering loop with a partner.
  • Connect skipping to why the heart likes continuous work.

Grades 5–6

  • Talk about race pace vs sprint and set a private meet goal.
  • Act as a student official or timer.
  • Plan a Score-O route and return by the whistle.
  • Record skip and a timed run privately.
