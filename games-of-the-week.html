September Soccer, Terry Fox and football 5 weeks

Week 1 · Aug 31–Sept 4

  1. Say My Name Rock, Paper, Scissors New Games A quick way to learn everyone’s name in the first week. Equipment: None (music optional)
  2. Here, There, Everywhere Big-Group Games Teaches the stop and travel signals you will use all year. Equipment: None
  3. Four Corners Big-Group Games A whole-class game where everyone stays in, good for setting gym routines. Equipment: Four labelled corners (numbers or colours)

Week 2 · Sept 8–11 4-day week: Mon Sept 7 off (Labour Day)

  1. Sharks and Dolphins Big-Group Games A tag warm-up with rescues, so nobody sits out. Equipment: 2 noodles · marked sea · optional parachute
  2. Through the Gates Big-Group Games Partner passing and trapping, the first soccer skill of the unit. Equipment: Cone pairs as gates, soccer balls.
  3. 4-Goal Soccer Big-Group Games Four nets keep a big class busy with lots of touches. Equipment: Soccer balls, 4 goals

Week 3 · Sept 14–18

  1. Pair Running Big-Group Games Builds steady running for the Terry Fox run on Friday. Equipment: None
  2. Avoid the Mines New Games Everyone dribbles at once with their head up. Equipment: Many cones as ‘mines’; one ball per student
  3. Pin Galore Soccer Big-Group Games Every pin is a goal, so passing and shooting pay off fast. Equipment: Plastic pins, foam soccer balls

Week 4 · Sept 21–25

  1. Kangaroos and Crocodiles Big-Group Games A reaction tag that gets legs ready for football. Equipment: None · two home lines and a centre line
  2. Crab Football Big-Group Games A low-key first football game that builds core strength. Equipment: Playground ball, two end lines.
  3. End Zone Ball Big-Group Games Get the ball to a teammate in the end zone: the big idea of football. Equipment: 4–8 footballs or soccer balls, pinnies, optional mats as obstacles.

Week 5 · Sept 28–Oct 1 3-day week: Wed Sept 30 off (Truth and Reconciliation Day), Fri Oct 2 off (PL day)

  1. Wolf’s Den Big-Group Games Dodging past defenders, like a ball carrier finding space. Equipment: Two end lines and a 3-m middle strip.
  2. 4 vs 1 New Games Small-group keep-away for quick passes and getting open. Equipment: Cones for a small grid; one ball per group of 5
  3. Four Corner Flags Big-Group Games Steal the flags and bring them home: attack and defend at once. Equipment: 16 beanbags (4 colours), 4 hoops, 4 pinnies colours, cones.
October Football 4 weeks

Week 6 · Oct 5–9

  1. Predators and Prey Tag New Games Fast chasing and dodging to start football class. Equipment: 1 pinnie or noodle for the bear; 2 hoops for the wolf pack; 2 mats as exercise stations (label one “Harder”, one “Easier”); cones for boundaries
  2. The Perfect Pass Big-Group Games Teams complete passes before scoring, so everyone touches the ball. Equipment: Soccer or playground ball, pinnies, two end lines or hoops.
  3. Football Frenzy New Games A busy whole-class game with foam footballs. Equipment: 6 gym mats, 6 foam footballs, bean bags, 1 basket per team, a volleyball net (optional)

Week 7 · Oct 13–16 4-day week: Mon Oct 12 off (Thanksgiving)

  1. Zone Tag New Games Running through zones past a tagger, like finding a gap. Equipment: 8–12 cones or floor markers to mark four zones; pinnies for the four taggers (optional); a whistle or music to start and stop
  2. Capture the Flag Relay New Games Throw-and-catch practice at three distances. Equipment: Per team: 1 cone for the team base, 3 hoops (near, middle and far), 15 flags or beanbags in a pile at the far end of the lane, 1 ball. Use foam or…
  3. Flasketball New Games Pass a foam football with no running, then shoot at the basket. Equipment: 2–3 foam or junior footballs; basketball hoops; pinnies in 3–4 colours; floor tape or spot markers to show scoring zones

Week 8 · Oct 19–23

  1. Jellyfish New Games A tag game where players help each other back in. Equipment: 3 hoops to start, plus 3–6 spare hoops; court lines or boundary cones
  2. Catch & Step Big-Group Games Partner throwing and catching that gets harder as you succeed. Equipment: Type of ball, or water balloons
  3. End Ball Big-Group Games Pass only, and score with a clean catch in the end zone. Equipment: Football or playground ball, pinnies, cones for two end zones.

Week 9 · Oct 26–30

  1. Tic Tac Run New Games A react-and-chase warm-up with a coin-flip twist. Equipment: 9 double-sided beanbags (mark X on one side and O on the other); floor tape for a large tic-tac-toe board; cones or divider mats for a neutral…
  2. Punt to the line Big-Group Games Practise the drop-kick, the last football skill of the unit. Equipment: Football or playground ball, a line.
  3. Elementary Ultimate Frisbee New Games Pass and move into space, the same idea as football. Equipment: Soft frisbees; pinnies; cone end zones
November Floor hockey 4 weeks

Week 10 · Nov 2–6

  1. Secret Agent Tag New Games A new tag game where nobody knows who the taggers are. Equipment: None. Court lines or boundary cones.
  2. Hockey Dribbling Shapes New Games Everyone stick-handles at once, from simple to tricky. Equipment: 1 floor-hockey stick and 1 soft ball per student (or 1 per pair taking turns), about 30 low cones, 4 tall cones.
  3. Numbers Hockey Big-Group Games Short, safe floor hockey games with sticks kept low. Equipment: Floor-hockey sticks or hands, hockey ball, two cone goals.

November Break (Nov 9–13): no school, no games this week.

Week 11 · Nov 16–20

  1. Freedom Catch Big-Group Games Tag where a throw and catch frees frozen players. Equipment: Cones, foam balls
  2. Gates & Hurdles Dribble New Games Dribble through gates and under hurdles for points. Equipment: 1 stick and 1 soft ball per student, 20–30 cones (10–15 gates), 6–10 low hurdles (or pool noodles on cones).
  3. Floorball Big-Group Games A small-sided game that uses the hockey ideas of attack and defence. Equipment: Tennis rackets, soft ball, mini goals

Week 12 · Nov 23–27

  1. Shadow Tag Big-Group Games A no-contact warm-up for quick changes of direction. Equipment: Poly spots if you have no sun.
  2. Zigzag & Shoot New Games Dribble around cones, then shoot at a target. Equipment: 1 stick and 1 soft ball per lane (2–3 students per lane), 3 small cones per lane, 1 folded mat or turned-over bench per lane.
  3. Star Wars Big-Group Games Teams aim at one big target ball, never at people. Equipment: Yoga ball, many soft balls, a bench at each end

Week 13 · Nov 30–Dec 4

  1. Everybody’s It Big-Group Games Everyone tags and keeps playing, a busy warm-up. Equipment: Optional one soft ball or spot markers · optional clothespins
  2. Move Up Hockey New Games Each hit moves the target farther away: passing accuracy. Equipment: Per lane: 1 stick, 1 soft ball, 4 spot markers, 1 empty plastic bottle or small cone (Cup Stack: 4 stackable cups or small cones).
  3. Bean Bag Battle New Games A team tossing battle to finish the hockey unit. Equipment: Per battle (two teams): 1 laundry basket, milk crate or bin per team; 1 beanbag per team; 1 cone per team for the throwing line; 7 hoops in a row…

Week 14 · Dec 7–11

  1. Alien Tag Big-Group Games A noodle tag warm-up with a quick jumping-jack return. Equipment: Noodles; hoop per runner
  2. Hoop Hut Knock Down New Games Build a hut together, then knock it down with a soft ball. Equipment: 6 hoops per team; soft balls
  3. Hidden Fleet New Games Lob soft balls over the net to find the hidden ships. Equipment: Volleyball net and standards; 1–2 parachutes or tarps draped over the net (or upright folding mats as a wall); 18 hoops (9 per team); 18 bowling…

Week 15 · Dec 14–18

  1. Frog Tag Big-Group Games Leapfrog rescues keep everybody moving. Equipment: Pool noodles
  2. Cone Raider Big-Group Games Partner throwing at cones: hit one and it becomes yours. Equipment: 4-5 cones each; one tennis ball per pair
  3. Eagles vs Seahawks New Games A big team game of chasing and collecting. Equipment: Beanbags as fish; hoop ‘lake’ in the centre; hoop nests

Christmas Break (Dec 21–Jan 1): no school, no games this week.

January Basketball 4 weeks

Week 16 · Jan 4–8

  1. Waspital Big-Group Games Everybody-is-it tag with a stretch hospital to start the term. Equipment: 1–2 hoops.
  2. Dribble the gates Big-Group Games Dribble through cone gates with finger pads and head up. Equipment: 4 cone gates, basketball.
  3. Captain Ball Big-Group Games Pass to your captain: the first basketball team game. Equipment: Soccer or playground ball, pinnies, 2 hoops.

Week 17 · Jan 11–15

  1. Dice Wave Dribble New Games A roll of the die sends groups dribbling across the gym. Equipment: Large foam die; one ball per student (soccer, basketball, or hockey)
  2. Wall chest-pass relay Big-Group Games Chest passes off the wall in a quick relay. Equipment: Hoop, soccer or basketball, wall space.
  3. Noodle Tag Basketball New Games Score while noodle taggers send you back. Equipment: Basketballs; noodles for defenders; hoops

Week 18 · Jan 18–22

  1. Rob the Nest Big-Group Games Dribble to collect objects, one per trip. Equipment: Centre pile of objects · team hoops as nests
  2. Throw & Go Big-Group Games Pass, then move, so the next pass happens on the run. Equipment: 1 ball per pair or circle
  3. Cornerball Big-Group Games Pass to a teammate in a corner hoop to score: getting open. Equipment: 4 hoops, playground ball, pinnies.

Week 19 · Jan 25–28 4-day week: Fri Jan 29 off (PL day)

  1. Team Tag Big-Group Games Pass the ball to get close enough to tag. Equipment: 1 playground ball or pinnie, bag of extra pinnies.
  2. Side to Side Big-Group Games Partner passing with side jumps between hoops. Equipment: Line of hoops; 1 ball per pair
  3. Bench Ball Big-Group Games Catch a pass on the bench to bring a teammate up. Equipment: 2 benches, dodgeballs
February Ropes and jumping 4 weeks

Week 20 · Feb 1–3 3-day week: Thu Feb 4 off (Teachers' Convention), Fri Feb 5 off (Teachers' Convention)

  1. Slippery Snakes Big-Group Games Jump or step over a wiggling rope: a gentle start to ropes. Equipment: 1–2 long ropes.
  2. Jump The River Big-Group Games Jump the gap between two ropes, and it widens after each success. Equipment: 2 skipping ropes (or tape lines)
  3. Rope Hoop Scoop New Games Partners use skipping ropes to carry a hoop home. Equipment: 24 skipping ropes (2 per pair); 24–36 hoops (mixed colours if you have them); 12 cones (one home cone per pair); optional colour-to-points chart…

Week 21 · Feb 8–12

  1. Musical Hoops Warm Up Games Travel to the music, then share a hoop. Nobody sits out. Equipment: CD player, upbeat music, one hoop per person if possible but not necessary
  2. Hop-the-hoops relay Big-Group Games Hop through the hoops on one foot with planned landings. Equipment: 6 hoops per team.
  3. Hungry Snake Big-Group Games Teams stay linked as a snake and pass objects back to the tail. Equipment: 20–40 beanbags or yarn balls, 2 bags or bins.

Week 22 · Feb 16–19 4-day week: Mon Feb 15 off (Family Day)

  1. Rush Hour Big-Group Games A traffic game for speed changes and safe spacing. Equipment: None
  2. Helicopter Jump Big-Group Games Jump the sliding noodle and stick the landing. Equipment: Foam pool noodle
  3. Jump to Hoop Race Big-Group Games A jumping relay from hoop to hoop. Equipment: One hoop per player as stepping-stones

Week 23 · Feb 22–26

  1. Mirror Mirror Big-Group Games Run to music, then freeze and copy a partner’s pose. Equipment: Music
  2. Jumping Jack Hoop Challenge Big-Group Games Spin a hoop, do jumping jacks, catch it, and add one. Equipment: One hoop each; personal space
  3. Chicken Checkers Big-Group Games Leave your base on the call and claim a new one. Equipment: Hoops or cones as bases

  2. Card Pass Challenge New Games Bump in a circle and beat the number on your card. Equipment: Deck of cards; volleyballs
  3. NitroBall 2.0 New Games Volleyball meets four square with a waist-high net. Equipment: Volleyball standards with the net lowered to waist height (or a rope between the standards); 1 four square / playground ball per court (beach…

Week 25 · Mar 8–12

  1. Reaction Speed Big-Group Games Quick hands and ready position for volleyball. Equipment: Trios: cone as a height marker, tennis ball
  2. Hoop Collect (set/volley) New Games A relay to set or volley balls into your team hoop. Equipment: Volleyballs or beach balls; one hoop per team
  3. Skip-rope Net 3v3 New Games Small-sided volleyball over a rope net. Equipment: Long skipping rope; volleyball or beach ball

Week 26 · Mar 15–18 4-day week: Fri Mar 19 off (School closure)

  1. Catch Clap Big-Group Games Eyes on the ball, the habit every volleyball hit needs. Equipment: One ball
  2. Hand Tennis Big-Group Games Open-hand rallies over a line or bench. Equipment: Soft ball; a line or bench as net
  3. FLY BACK Big-Group Games Throw and catch over the net, a stepping stone to volleyball. Equipment: Discs or balls, cones, pinnies.

Spring Break (Mar 22–26): no school, no games this week.

Week 27 · Mar 30–Apr 2 4-day week: Mon Mar 29 off (Easter Monday)

  1. Balloon Keep-Ups Big-Group Games An easy welcome back from Spring Break. Equipment: Balloons; pool noodles or rulers
  2. Wall Rally Big-Group Games Keep a rally going against the wall with one bounce. Equipment: 1 soft ball · wall, rope, or taped square
  3. Four Square Paddle Ball New Games Hoop courts and paddles for striking and rallies. Equipment: Hoops as courts; paddles; soft balls
April Gymnastics 4 weeks

Week 28 · Apr 5–9

  1. Animals Big-Group Games Animal moves warm up arms, legs and core for gymnastics. Equipment: None
  2. Frogs on the Lily Pads Big-Group Games Two-foot jumps with a stuck landing. Equipment: Hoops.
  3. Safe to Shore Big-Group Games Cross the floor using only the islands: balance and planning. Equipment: Hoops or mats as islands · start and finish lines

Week 29 · Apr 12–16

  1. Crab Tag New Games Everyone moves in crab walk: strong arms for gymnastics. Equipment: None (mats optional)
  2. Bench Balance Big-Group Games Walk the bench and move cones from side to side. Equipment: Bench, side hoops, cones
  3. Challenge Mat New Games Co-operative challenges on mats, done as a team. Equipment: 4–10 yoga or gym mats; challenge cards

Week 30 · Apr 19–23

  1. Daytime / Nighttime Big-Group Games Travel, then freeze in a strong, still shape. Equipment: None
  2. Spot-Jump Path Big-Group Games Jump the targets with quiet, stuck landings. Equipment: Spots, hoops, or a taped river · optional noodle
  3. Stick Tic-Tac-Toe Big-Group Games A stuck landing claims the hoop. Three in a row wins. Equipment: Nine hoops in a 3x3; agreed shapes

Week 31 · Apr 26–30

  1. Bridges & Rivers Big-Group Games Half the class makes shapes while the others travel over and under. Equipment: None · optional music
  2. Crawl and Roll Challenge Big-Group Games Link a crawl into a roll across the mats. Equipment: Mats
  3. Wheelbarrow Walk Olympics New Games Partner strength walks to bronze, silver and gold cones. Equipment: Cones at bronze, silver, gold distances
May Track and field 5 weeks

Week 32 · May 3–6 4-day week: Fri May 7 off (PL day)

  1. Sneak & React Big-Group Games Quick starts, like the start of a sprint. Equipment: None
  2. Long Jump Challenge Big-Group Games Beat the team mark from the same line. Equipment: 1 cone per team; jump line
  3. Jungle Run Relays Big-Group Games A relay with a hoop, a jump and a cone. Equipment: Cones, hoops, optional hurdles (low).

Week 33 · May 10–14

  1. Horse and Jockey New Games Run on the call in a circle: quick reactions. Equipment: None (music optional)
  2. Tennis Jump Big-Group Games Timed two-foot jumps over a rolling ball. Equipment: Pairs: one cone, one tennis ball
  3. Circle run relay Big-Group Games Laps of the circle with clean hand-offs. Equipment: Cone circle.

Week 34 · May 17–19 3-day week: Thu May 20 off (School closure), Fri May 21 off (School closure)

  1. Colour Hunt Big-Group Games Run and collect the colour that is called. Equipment: Coloured cones or markers
  2. Pace lap New Games Learn to pace a run so you reach the cone on time. Equipment: Cones around a lap; timer
  3. Balance Carry Big-Group Games Carry an object without dropping it: control while moving. Equipment: One object per person or pair (ball on cone, bag on racquet, bag on head)

Week 35 · May 25–28 4-day week: Mon May 24 off (Victoria Day)

  1. Catch Me If You Can (sprint) New Games Short sprints with a head start: speed practice. Equipment: Cones for start and finish
  2. Cone Relay Big-Group Games Remember the order, then sprint the cones. Equipment: Numbered cones 1–4.
  3. Fetch Relay Big-Group Games One item per trip, then tag the next runner. Equipment: Item pile · home hoop or sack

Week 36 · May 31–June 4

  1. Bull Chasers New Games Grab-and-go sprints for Track and Field Day week. Equipment: Cones; balls at a far cone
  2. Shuttles (cone ball) New Games A shuttle relay to sharpen starts and turns. Equipment: Cones in a line; tennis ball per team
  3. Rescue Relay Big-Group Games Run across and bring a teammate home. Equipment: None, or a beanbag to carry.
June Baseball 4 weeks

Week 37 · June 7–11

  1. Mat Tag New Games Run the bases and stay safe on the mats. Equipment: 4 mats (one per base); 2 soft foam balls or soft tags for the taggers (no hard dodgeballs — those stay on the Dodgeball page); pinnies for taggers
  2. Overarm throw-and-catch Big-Group Games Practise the overarm throw to a partner. Equipment: Playground ball, hoop.
  3. Continuous Kick Ball Big-Group Games Kickball where several runners can be on base at once. Equipment: Playground or t-ball, saucer cone, bases or hoops.

Week 38 · June 14–18

  1. Tidy Up Big-Group Games Chase a moving bucket to return balls: quick feet. Equipment: Scattered tennis balls; one bucket
  2. Bullseye New Games Throw tennis balls to knock volleyballs off cones. Equipment: Volleyballs on cones; tennis balls; pinnies
  3. Hit and Run Big-Group Games Hit, run to the hoop, and fielders throw to the hoop. Equipment: Tee or kick, t-ball, one or two hoops.

Week 39 · June 21–25

  1. Back to Base Big-Group Games Travel, then hurry back to your base on the whistle. Equipment: Home hoops or spots.
  2. Bozo / bucket toss New Games Underhand tosses into buckets at longer distances. Equipment: Buckets at increasing distances; beanbags
  3. Diamond Strike Big-Group Games All four hitters run a base on any hit. Equipment: 4 bases or buckets, t-ball, bats or hands.

Week 40 · Mon June 28 1-day week: last day of school Mon June 28

  1. Chuck the Chicken Warm Up Games A fun closer for the last class of the year. Equipment: Rubber chicken / fish / pig / ball
  2. Throw-Clap-Catch Big-Group Games Clap first, then catch: eyes on the ball. Equipment: 1 ball per circle or pair
  3. Fun Kickball New Games Six-base kickball to finish the year. Equipment: Kickball; six bases

SCA PE Playbook

Grades 1–6 · four 30-minute classes a week · Alberta Physical Education and Wellness. Real sport balls, dodgeball with its own house rules, no elimination in other games, floor-only gymnastics. Follow your school’s safety rules.
