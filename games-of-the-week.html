Games of the Week

Each week has two sets of three games that fit the month’s unit: one for Grades 1–3 (simpler rules and lots of moving) and one for Grades 4–6 (more strategy and game skills). Each set has a warm-up, a skill game and a bigger game for the whole class. Every game is already on the site, so each name opens its full card with set-up, rules and safety. Play them any day this week.

September Soccer, Terry Fox and football 5 weeks

Week 1 · Aug 31–Sept 4

Grades 1–3

  1. Say My Name Rock, Paper, Scissors New Games A quick way to learn everyone’s name in the first week. Equipment: None (music optional)
  2. Here, There, Everywhere Big-Group Games Teaches the stop and travel signals you will use all year. Equipment: None
  3. Kangaroos and Crocodiles Big-Group Games Listen for your team name, then race home: a first-week listening game. Equipment: None · two home lines and a centre line

Grades 4–6

  1. Everybody’s It Big-Group Games Everyone can tag, so the whole class is moving from the first minute. Equipment: Optional one soft ball or spot markers · optional clothespins
  2. Foosball Soccer New Games Short passes along a row teach first touch and passing lanes. Equipment: Pool noodles; soccer balls; two goals
  3. 3-Ball Soccer Big-Group Games Three balls at once mean more touches and quick decisions. Equipment: 3 soccer balls

Week 2 · Sept 8–11 4-day week: Mon Sept 7 off (Labour Day)

Grades 1–3

  1. Sharks and Dolphins Big-Group Games A tag warm-up with rescues, so nobody sits out. Equipment: 2 noodles · marked sea · optional parachute
  2. Traffic Lights (kick) Big-Group Games Dribble, trap and stop on a colour: ball control at their own speed. Equipment: Soccer balls, one per student if you can.
  3. Through the Gates Big-Group Games Partner passing and trapping, the first soccer skill of the unit. Equipment: Cone pairs as gates, soccer balls.

Grades 4–6

  1. Ten-Second Tag Big-Group Games Short bursts of chasing for a quick, fair warm-up. Equipment: Centre line of cones, whistle.
  2. Guard the Gates Big-Group Games Dribble or pass past defenders who win the ball, not the body. Equipment: Cone gates, soccer balls, pinnies.
  3. 4-Goal Soccer Big-Group Games Four nets keep a big class busy with lots of touches. Equipment: Soccer balls, 4 goals

Week 3 · Sept 14–18

Grades 1–3

  1. Circle Racing New Games Steady running with a partner to get ready for the Terry Fox run. Equipment: 1 cone per group of 4; 4 beanbags per group, each a different colour (for example red, blue, green, yellow); for 24 students, 6 cones and 24 beanbags
  2. Rob the Nest (dribble) Big-Group Games Everyone dribbles at once to bring a ball home. Equipment: 4 hoops, soccer balls.
  3. Avoid the Mines New Games Everyone dribbles at once with their head up. Equipment: Many cones as ‘mines’; one ball per student

Grades 4–6

  1. Pair Running Big-Group Games Builds steady running for the Terry Fox run on Friday. Equipment: None
  2. Collect Em All New Games Teams work together to win balls and carry them home. Equipment: Hoops (one per team); foam balls in the centre
  3. Pin Galore Soccer Big-Group Games Every pin is a goal, so passing and shooting pay off fast. Equipment: Plastic pins, indoor soccer balls or Softex foam balls

Week 4 · Sept 21–25

Grades 1–3

  1. Old Grey Cat New Games A simple chase game with one clear signal to run. Equipment: None. Optional: 8–12 cones to mark two safe lines (the “trees”); 1 pinny or scarf for each cat.
  2. Dash and Dribble Big-Group Games A dribbling relay with a trap at the cone. Equipment: Soccer balls, cones.
  3. End Zone Ball Big-Group Games Roll the ball to a teammate in the end zone: an easy first team game. Equipment: 4–8 footballs or soccer balls, pinnies, optional mats as obstacles.

Grades 4–6

  1. Wolf’s Den Big-Group Games Dodging past defenders, like a ball carrier finding space. Equipment: Two end lines and a 3-m middle strip.
  2. Crab Football Big-Group Games A low-key first football game that builds core strength. Equipment: Playground ball, two end lines.
  3. 4 vs 1 New Games Small-group keep-away for quick passes and getting open. Equipment: Cones for a small grid; one ball per group of 5

  1. Numbers (body shapes) New Games Travel, then freeze in a number shape. Equipment: None (optional number cards)
  2. Beanbag head walk Big-Group Games Still head and airplane arms with a beanbag on top. Equipment: Beanbag and a hoop per team.
  3. Turtle Race Big-Group Games Crawl with a cone on your back: slow and steady control. Equipment: 1 cone per racer

Grades 4–6

  1. Flip Flop Big-Group Games Make bridges and tunnels, then travel through them. Equipment: None (bodies as kit)
  2. Inchworm Rescue Big-Group Games Walk the hands out and back: core and shoulder strength. Equipment: Two hoops per pupil; five balls in the far hoop
  3. Crawl and Roll Challenge Big-Group Games Link a crawl into a roll across the mats. Equipment: Mats

Week 31 · Apr 26–30

Grades 1–3

  1. Bridges & Rivers Big-Group Games Half the class makes shapes while the others travel over and under. Equipment: None · optional music
  2. Crab Tag New Games Everyone moves in crab walk: strong arms for gymnastics. Equipment: None (mats optional)
  3. Follow the Leader Big-Group Games Copy the leader’s move or shape; take turns leading. Equipment: Optional whistle, clap or music.

Grades 4–6

  1. Magic Numbers Big-Group Games Three moves without stopping for strength and control. Equipment: None
  2. Tennis Jump Big-Group Games Jump and land so the rolling ball ends up between your feet. Equipment: Pairs: one cone, one tennis ball
  3. Wheelbarrow Walk Olympics New Games Partner strength walks to bronze, silver and gold cones. Equipment: Cones at bronze, silver, gold distances
May Track and field 5 weeks

Week 32 · May 3–6 4-day week: Fri May 7 off (PL day)

Grades 1–3

  1. Sneak & React Big-Group Games Quick starts, like the start of a sprint. Equipment: None
  2. Jungle Run Relays Big-Group Games A relay with a hoop, a jump and a cone. Equipment: Cones, hoops, optional hurdles (low).
  3. Plant the Trees New Games Two jobs and nonstop movement: plant or knock down. Equipment: Many cones or markers

Grades 4–6

  1. Catch Me If You Can (sprint) New Games Short sprints with a head start: speed practice. Equipment: Cones for start and finish
  2. Long Jump Challenge Big-Group Games Beat the team mark from the same line. Equipment: 1 cone per team; jump line
  3. Circle run relay Big-Group Games Run a lap and tag the next runner fairly. Equipment: Cone circle.

Week 33 · May 10–14

Grades 1–3

  1. Survivor Tag Big-Group Games Tagged players do a skill, then get right back in. Equipment: 2–3 pinnies.
  2. Shuttles (cone ball) New Games A shuttle relay to sharpen starts and turns. Equipment: Cones in a line; tennis ball per team
  3. Colour Hunt Big-Group Games Run and collect the colour that is called. Equipment: Coloured cones or markers

Grades 4–6

  1. Horse and Jockey New Games Run on the call in a circle: quick reactions. Equipment: None (music optional)
  2. Tennis Jump Big-Group Games Timed two-foot jumps over a rolling ball. Equipment: Pairs: one cone, one tennis ball
  3. Speed Run Relay Big-Group Games Sprint, do a skill at the cone, sprint back. Equipment: Cones, optional batons.

Week 34 · May 17–19 3-day week: Thu May 20 off (School closure), Fri May 21 off (School closure)

Grades 1–3

  1. Dash for Safety Big-Group Games Run to the called colour before the taggers reach you. Equipment: 4 coloured cones or walls.
  2. Hop-the-hoops relay Big-Group Games One-foot hops with a planned landing. Equipment: 6 hoops per team.
  3. Balance Carry Big-Group Games Carry an object without dropping it: control while moving. Equipment: One object per person or pair (ball on cone, bag on racquet, bag on head)

Grades 4–6

  1. Bull Chasers New Games Grab-and-go sprints for Track and Field Day week. Equipment: Cones; balls at a far cone
  2. Pace lap New Games Learn to pace a run so you reach the cone on time. Equipment: Cones around a lap; timer
  3. Capture the Egg Big-Group Games Carry a beanbag through traffic: speed and dodging. Equipment: Beanbags, pinnies.

Week 35 · May 25–28 4-day week: Mon May 24 off (Victoria Day)

Grades 1–3

  1. Rubber Band Big-Group Games Travel away, then sprint back to the circle on the signal. Equipment: A centre circle of spots or a centre cone.
  2. Zig-zag touch relay Big-Group Games Change direction at each cone and tag the next runner. Equipment: 5 cones and a hoop of beanbags per team.
  3. Fetch Relay Big-Group Games One item per trip, then tag the next runner. Equipment: Item pile · home hoop or sack

Grades 4–6

  1. Elements Sprint Big-Group Games Sprint to the right zone on the call: reaction starts. Equipment: Three wall signs: Fire, Earth, Water.
  2. Cone Relay Big-Group Games Remember the order, then sprint the cones. Equipment: Numbered cones 1–4.
  3. Partner Orienteering Big-Group Games Read a map with a partner and run to each control. Equipment: Simple maps; controls; record cards

Week 36 · May 31–June 4

Grades 1–3

  1. Kangaroos and Crocodiles Big-Group Games Listen for your team name, then race home. Equipment: None · two home lines and a centre line
  2. Jump The River Big-Group Games Jump across, and the river gets wider each round. Equipment: 2 skipping ropes (or court lines)
  3. Rescue Relay Big-Group Games Run across and bring a teammate home. Equipment: None, or a beanbag to carry.

Grades 4–6

  1. Ten-Second Tag Big-Group Games Short bursts of chasing for a quick, fair warm-up. Equipment: Centre line of cones, whistle.
  2. Knock the Pin Big-Group Games Run the triangle, then throw to knock the pin. Equipment: Cones in a triangle; centre cone in a hoop; softballs
  3. Ice Cream Cone Relays Big-Group Games Three quick relays to finish the track unit. Equipment: foam balls, cones
June Baseball 4 weeks

Week 37 · June 7–11

Grades 1–3

  1. Tidy Up Big-Group Games Chase a moving bucket to return balls: quick feet. Equipment: Scattered tennis balls; one bucket
  2. Overarm throw-and-catch Big-Group Games Practise the overarm throw to a partner. Equipment: Playground ball, hoop.
  3. Quick Baseball Big-Group Games Hit, run to one hoop and back: everyone bats. Equipment: Tee or coach toss, t-ball and bat, one hoop.

Grades 4–6

  1. Mat Tag New Games Run the bases and stay safe on the mats. Equipment: 4 mats (one per base); 2 soft foam balls or soft tags for the taggers (no hard dodgeballs — those stay on the Dodgeball page); pinnies for taggers
  2. Catch & Step Big-Group Games Partner throwing and catching that gets harder as you succeed. Equipment: Type of ball, or water balloons
  3. Continuous Kick Ball Big-Group Games Kickball where several runners can be on base at once. Equipment: Playground or t-ball, saucer cone, bases or hoops.

Week 38 · June 14–18

Grades 1–3

  1. Chuck the Chicken Warm Up Games A fun closer for the last class of the year. Equipment: Rubber chicken / fish / pig / ball
  2. Catch the Cone Big-Group Games Flip a small cone into a bigger one: hand-eye timing. Equipment: Two cones per player
  3. Jailbreakers Big-Group Games A fair hit frees a teammate from jail. Equipment: Tee or kick, t-ball, a jail hoop.

Grades 4–6

  1. Everybody’s It Big-Group Games Everyone can tag, so the whole class is moving from the first minute. Equipment: Optional one soft ball or spot markers · optional clothespins
  2. Bullseye New Games Throw tennis balls to knock volleyballs off cones. Equipment: Volleyballs on cones; tennis balls; pinnies
  3. Hit and Run Big-Group Games Hit, run to the hoop, and fielders throw to the hoop. Equipment: Tee or kick, t-ball, one or two hoops.

Week 39 · June 21–25

Grades 1–3

  1. Back to Base Big-Group Games Travel, then hurry back to your base on the whistle. Equipment: Home hoops or spots.
  2. Hoop underhand relay Big-Group Games Three underhand tries to land a beanbag in the hoop. Equipment: Beanbags, two hoops.
  3. Shark Ball Big-Group Games Kick and run for the far line past the shark. Equipment: 1 foam ball

Grades 4–6

  1. Blob Tag Big-Group Games The blob grows, so players must plan their escape routes. Equipment: Optional noodles or cone hats · spare kit at the side
  2. Strikeball Big-Group Games Strike the ball between players for placement. Equipment: Ball
  3. Diamond Strike Big-Group Games All four hitters run a base on any hit. Equipment: 4 bases or buckets, t-ball, bats or hands.

Week 40 · Mon June 28 1-day week: last day of school Mon June 28

Grades 1–3

  1. Octopus Big-Group Games Cross the gym past the octopus; tagged players become helpers. Equipment: Two end lines.
  2. Throw-Clap-Catch Big-Group Games Clap first, then catch: eyes on the ball. Equipment: 1 ball per circle or pair
  3. Barkball Big-Group Games Hit or kick, run to the far line and back. Equipment: Playground or t-ball, bat or kick, an end line.

Grades 4–6

  1. Ten-Second Tag Big-Group Games Short bursts of chasing for a quick, fair warm-up. Equipment: Centre line of cones, whistle.
  2. 4 Ball Kickball New Games Four balls in play: fielders must decide fast. Equipment: Four kickballs; bases
  3. Fun Kickball New Games Six-base kickball to finish the year. Equipment: Kickball; six bases

SCA PE Playbook

Grades 1–6 · four 30-minute classes a week · Alberta Physical Education and Wellness. Real sport balls, dodgeball with its own house rules, no elimination in other games, floor-only gymnastics. Follow your school’s safety rules.
