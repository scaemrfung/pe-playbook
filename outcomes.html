Alberta PEW outcomes & assessment

This page maps the Physical Education and Wellness (K–6) curriculum to a 4 × 30-minute PE week. Use it when you plan the month, write report-card comments, or check who needs another look.

Source: LearnAlberta · Physical Education and Wellness K–6 (implemented 2022; minor updates 2026). Confirm the exact learning-outcome wording for your grade before reporting.

Organizing ideas (all eight)

Organizing ideas (OIs) are the big strands. Each has guiding questions and learning outcomes. In the gym this playbook leans on the movement-heavy strands; wellness strands still show up in cool-downs, food/energy talk, and relationships.

Healthy Eating, Growth and Development, and Financial Literacy are part of PEW but are often taught outside the 30-minute PE block. Link them when a lesson names food, body changes, or choices; do not force them into every tag game.

How to assess in this playbook

  • Report the learning outcome, not the score. Who won Hospital Tag does not go on the report card.
  • One or two look-fors per class is enough. Tick a checklist once a week, not every 30 minutes.
  • Fitness numbers stay private. Never post times or ranks. Date the sheet; compare a student to their own baseline.
  • Use three tools: observation during the game, a short exit question (name a muscle, show a freeze), and a monthly skill look-for.
  • Grade bands. Same game, different evidence: Grades 1–2 walk and listen; 3–4 refine and cooperate; 5–6 lead and adapt.

Quick evidence bank (gym)

Organizing ideaSample look-fors in a 30-minute class
Active LivingEnters the game; names how the body feels after; chooses water or a quieter recovery when needed.
Movement Skill DevelopmentShows the cue of the day (trap, underhand send, soft land); changes speed or pathway on a signal.
Character DevelopmentTries again after a miss; leads a stretch or a call; accepts a hard role without shaming others.
SafetyFreezes on the whistle; stays in bounds; soft tag; equipment parked before the next game.
Healthy RelationshipsUnfreezes a classmate; uses a kind phrase; accepts teacher-formed teams; no public picking.
Healthy Eating(Often outside PE) Links water or a snack choice to energy for play when the lesson names it.
Growth and Development(Often outside PE) Names a change in breath or heart rate after activity; respects personal space.
Financial Literacy(Usually classroom) Not assessed in the PE block of this playbook.
