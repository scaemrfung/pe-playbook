April

Month focus: Floor-only gymnastics · shapes, rolls, balances, jump-stick

Alberta PEW focus: Movement Skill Development, Safety, Character Development

Equipment: Mats, tape beam, hoops — no benches, boxes, or beams

Fitness update: Jump-stick and a hold. Private record.

Floor only. Skills check list and Grade 6 booklet live on the Gymnastics page.

Show differentiation for

All grades G1 G2 G3 G4 G5 G6

Showing all grade bands (1–2 / 3–4 / 5–6).

Outcomes and assessment

Alberta PEW organizing ideas: Movement Skill Development · Safety · Character Development

Gymnastics: shapes, travel, balance, jump-land, log roll and rock (no neck-loading). Control over speed.

Grades 1–2

  • Show tuck and stretch (and other copied shapes) with tight muscles.
  • Travel like an animal with a still start and still end.
  • Jump and stick a landing on a mat.
  • Rock or log-roll on a mat with the head not leading a collapse.

Grades 3–4

  • Hold tuck, pike, straddle, and stretch.
  • Link shape + roll or jump + shape.
  • Walk a tape line and step off to a stick.
  • Choose a still pair shape they can leave instantly.

Grades 5–6

  • Perform a 20–30 second routine with a level change and a still ending.
  • Use a simple 3-cue rubric (stillness, control, ending) when watching peers.
  • Peer safety-coach a station.
  • Explain why control beats speed.
