Horse and Jockey

Warm-ups & brain breaks

New this week

When: May · Track · Grades: 1–6 (est.) · Stay-in

Pairs around a circle (one Horse, one Jockey); teacher calls HORSE or JOCKEY; named role runs around outside, crawls through partner’s wide stance, then races to finish against other pairs

Equipment: None (music optional)

Set-up: Pairs around a big circle: one Horse (standing, feet wide), one Jockey.

How we play

  1. Teacher calls ‘Horse!’ or ‘Jockey!’.
  2. The named role runs once around the outside of the circle.
  3. They crawl through their partner’s wide stance.
  4. Race back to finish in their spot; swap roles often.

Variations

  • Call a travel style (skip, gallop).

Dance Code

Warm-ups & brain breaks

New this week

When: Oct · Football (also Dec · Games) · Grades: 1–6 (est.) · Stay-in

Letters map to moves (e.g. A jump, B spin, C clap, D balance, E slide, F freeze); teach codes, then combine longer “codes” for pupils to remember and perform

Equipment: Letter cards or whiteboard; music

Set-up: Teach letters mapped to moves, e.g. A = jump, B = spin, C = clap, D = balance, E = slide, F = freeze.

How we play

  1. Show one letter; students perform the move.
  2. Combine 3–4 letters into a ‘code’ (e.g. B-A-D).
  3. Students memorise and perform the code to the beat.
  4. Groups write their own code for another group to perform.

Dance Levels & Directions

Warm-ups & brain breaks

New this week

When: Oct · Football (also Apr · Gymnastics) · Grades: 1–6 (est.) · Stay-in

Teacher calls travel combining level (high/mid/low) and direction (forward/side/back); pupils invent safe locomotor responses

Equipment: Music

Set-up: Personal space. Teach levels (high / middle / low) and directions (forward / sideways / backward).

How we play

  1. Teacher calls a level + direction (‘low and sideways’).
  2. Students invent a safe way to travel matching the call.
  3. Freeze on the whistle in a matching shape.
  4. Pairs combine two calls into a short sequence.

Four Square Paddle Ball

Net–wall

New this week

When: Mar · Volleyball · Grades: 2–6 (est.) · Stay-in

Hula-hoop courts with paddles/soft ball; three progressive distances (Close / Challenge / Championship); striking, teamwork, hand-eye, spatial awareness

Equipment: Hula hoops as courts; paddles; soft balls

Set-up: Groups of four; hoop courts set at three distances (Close / Challenge / Championship).

How we play

  1. Hit the ball with the paddle so it bounces into another player’s hoop court.
  2. Return it after one bounce.
  3. Win a rally at Close, move to Challenge, then Championship.

Net–wall

Turtle Tag

Tag

When: Dec · Games · Grades: 1–6 (est.) · Stay-in

Noodle taggers; runners can drop to turtle-on-back (limbs up) up to 5 sec (longer only if tagger is hovering); tagged do 3 jumping jacks then back in

Equipment: Pool noodles for taggers

Set-up: 2–3 noodle taggers.

How we play

  1. Runners can drop onto their back like a turtle (arms and legs up) to be safe for up to 5 seconds.
  2. Longer only if a tagger is hovering.
  3. Tagged players do 3 jumping jacks, then back in.

Slam Ball

Net–wall

When: Mar · Volleyball · Grades: 1–6 (est.) · Stay-in

Partners RPS; bounce soft ball into hoop; palm-slap forward only back into hoop; miss = partner’s point

Equipment: Soft ball and hoop per pair

Set-up: Partners with a hoop on the floor between them.

How we play

  1. Rock–paper–scissors to decide who starts.
  2. Bounce the ball into the hoop; partner palm-slaps it forward back into the hoop.
  3. Miss the hoop = your partner’s point.

Knock ’em Downers

Warm-ups & brain breaks

When: Sep · Soccer (also Dec · Games) · Grades: 1–6 (est.) · Stay-in

Half knock pins/cones; half reset; switch

Equipment: 20–30 cones or pins spread out; pinnies for two teams

Set-up: Scatter cones standing up across the gym. Split the class into Knockers and Pickers.

How we play

  1. On GO, Knockers tip cones over with a hand (or a foot in soccer month).
  2. Pickers stand cones back up at the same time.
  3. After 45–60 seconds, freeze and count: more down = Knockers win, more up = Pickers win.
  4. Switch roles and play again.

Variations

  • Soccer month: knock down with a dribbled ball touch.
  • Add a locomotor rule (skip, gallop) between cones.

The Food Chain

Warm-ups & brain breaks

When: Dec · Games · Grades: 1–6 (est.) · Stay-in

RPS food-chain levels; losers stay in

Equipment: None

Set-up: Everyone starts at the bottom level (e.g. egg). Teach the levels and an action for each: egg → chicken → dinosaur → human (or class choice).

How we play

  1. Walk around doing your level’s action and find someone at the same level.
  2. Play rock–paper–scissors. The winner moves up a level; the loser moves down (or stays at egg).
  3. Top level players keep playing other top-level players to stay up.
  4. Play 3–5 minutes. No one is out — everyone keeps finding partners.

Variations

  • Use sport-themed levels (dribble → pass → shoot → score).

Numbers (body shapes)

Warm-ups & brain breaks

When: Apr · Gymnastics · Grades: 1–6 (est.) · Stay-in

Body shapes 1–10

Equipment: None (optional number cards)

Set-up: Personal space. Teach a body shape for each number 1–10 (e.g. 1 = pencil stand, 2 = two-foot balance, 5 = star).

How we play

  1. Travel around the space.
  2. Teacher calls a number; freeze into that shape.
  3. Hold 3–5 seconds, then travel again.
  4. Later, call number sums (2 + 3) for older grades.

Variations

  • Groups make the number shape together.
  • Hold the shape on one foot.

Warm-ups & brain breaks

When: Dec · Games (also Oct · Football) · Grades: 1–6 (est.) · Stay-in

Dance / freeze warm-up (use as teacher-led, not required video follow)

Equipment: Music

Set-up: Personal space; teacher controls the music.

How we play
