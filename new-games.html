Four Corners

New this weekUnit: Dec · GamesGrades 1–6 (est.)Stay-inWarm-ups & brain breaks

Label room/gym corners 1–4; students move, then each picks a corner on signal; teacher calls a corner; those there do a quick exercise then all stay in and play again (adapt classic elimination to exercise re-entry)

Equipment: Four labelled corners (numbers or colours)

Set-up: Label corners 1–4. Students start in the middle.

How to play

  1. Students travel on the signal; then each picks a corner.
  2. Teacher (eyes closed) calls a corner.
  3. Players in that corner do a quick exercise (5–8 jumping jacks) — nobody is out.
  4. Everyone returns to the middle and plays again.

Variations

  • Use a student caller.
  • Change the travel style each round.

Safety / house rules: No diving into corners.

New this weekUnit: Dec · GamesGrades 1–6 (est.)Stay-inWarm-ups & brain breaks

One detective faces away; secret leader starts movements and class copies; detective turns and guesses the leader; rotate so everyone moves

Equipment: None

Set-up: Circle. One detective steps away or faces away.

How to play

  1. Pick a secret leader who starts a movement; everyone copies.
  2. The detective turns back and watches.
  3. The leader changes moves every few seconds; the detective has 3 guesses.
  4. Rotate detective and leader.

Bottle Flip

New this weekUnit: Dec · GamesGrades 1–6 (est.)Stay-inTag (stay-in style)

Everyone has a cone (“bottle”); on GO flip cone to land upright; first successful flip grabs a soft ball while others flee; thrower soft-tags below shoulders for points (not outs)

Equipment: One cone per student; 3–4 soft balls

Set-up: Everyone stands by a cone (the ‘bottle’).

How to play

  1. On GO, flip your cone to land upright.
  2. The first successful flip grabs a soft ball; others flee.
  3. The thrower soft-tags others below the shoulders for points (not outs).
  4. Reset and go again.

Safety / house rules: Soft balls, below the shoulders only.

Horse and Jockey

Unit: May · TrackGrades 1–6 (est.)Stay-inWarm-ups & brain breaks

Pairs around a circle (one Horse, one Jockey); teacher calls HORSE or JOCKEY; named role runs around outside, crawls through partner’s wide stance, then races to finish against other pairs

Equipment: None (music optional)

Set-up: Pairs around a big circle: one Horse (standing, feet wide), one Jockey.

How to play

  1. Teacher calls ‘Horse!’ or ‘Jockey!’.
  2. The named role runs once around the outside of the circle.
  3. They crawl through their partner’s wide stance.
  4. Race back to finish in their spot; swap roles often.

Variations

  • Call a travel style (skip, gallop).

Safety / house rules: All run the same direction; crawl slowly through legs.

Dance Code

Unit: Oct · FootballGrades 1–6 (est.)Stay-inWarm-ups & brain breaks

Letters map to moves (e.g. A jump, B spin, C clap, D balance, E slide, F freeze); teach codes, then combine longer “codes” for pupils to remember and perform

Equipment: Letter cards or whiteboard; music

Set-up: Teach letters mapped to moves, e.g. A = jump, B = spin, C = clap, D = balance, E = slide, F = freeze.

How to play

  1. Show one letter; students perform the move.
  2. Combine 3–4 letters into a ‘code’ (e.g. B-A-D).
  3. Students memorise and perform the code to the beat.
  4. Groups write their own code for another group to perform.

Dance Levels & Directions

Unit: Oct · FootballGrades 1–6 (est.)Stay-inWarm-ups & brain breaks

Teacher calls travel combining level (high/mid/low) and direction (forward/side/back); pupils invent safe locomotor responses

Equipment: Music

Set-up: Personal space. Teach levels (high / middle / low) and directions (forward / sideways / backward).

How to play

  1. Teacher calls a level + direction (‘low and sideways’).
  2. Students invent a safe way to travel matching the call.
  3. Freeze on the whistle in a matching shape.
  4. Pairs combine two calls into a short sequence.

Safety / house rules: Backward travel slowly with eyes over the shoulder.

RPS Warm-Up

Unit: Dec · GamesGrades 1–6 (est.)Stay-inWarm-ups & brain breaks

Four cones; play RPS; winner advances clockwise, loser stays (lose 3 in a row = advance); optional stick per full lap

Equipment: Four cones in a square; optional sticks/popsicle sticks as lap tokens

Set-up: Four cones in a square. Split students evenly at the cones.

How to play

  1. Find someone at your cone and play rock–paper–scissors.
  2. Winner jogs clockwise to the next cone; loser stays and plays again.
  3. Lose three in a row? You advance anyway.
  4. Optional: collect a stick each full lap. Play 3–4 minutes.

Variations

  • Change travel between cones (skip, side-step, bear walk).

Hot Air Balloon (parachute)

Unit: Oct · FootballGrades K–3 (est.)Stay-inWarm-ups & brain breaks

Lift chute, 5 steps in under canopy, 5 out; repeat

Equipment: Parachute

Set-up: Everyone holds the parachute edge with two hands, spread evenly.

How to play

  1. On ‘Up!’, lift the chute together.
  2. Take 5 steps in under the canopy as it rises.
  3. Take 5 steps back out as it floats down.
  4. Repeat; try to keep it up longer each time.

Variations

  • Call names to run under and swap sides.

Safety / house rules: Walk only; watch toes when stepping in.
